## Operational transfer gate (non-diagnostic)
1. Define observable behavior, timescale, setting, intended goal and independently assessable consequence. Distinguish reported experience, actual action/outcome and inferred construct. Do not infer IQ from ease of task, diagnosis from hyperfocus or lack of diagnosis from achievement.
2. Separate (a) measured ability and domain skill, (b) value/choice/expected reward and effort, (c) initiating/holding/switching/stopping and speed/accuracy, (d) supports, resources and environmental demands, (e) achieved outcome, cost and clinically relevant function. These interact, not a fixed sequence. Clinical history and symptom pattern independent of test profile.
3. Compare competing hypotheses: mismatch/underchallenge or overchallenge; unclear task/skill and interruptions; sleep/stress/mood/anxiety; other neurodevelopmental conditions; ADHD-associated developmental and executive/attentional patterns; combinations. Hypotheses must predict distinct observations and tolerate disconfirmation. Interest-dependent changes compatible with ADHD and other accounts; do not diagnose through response to reward, structure or novelty. 
4. In ordinary voluntary settings observe same task under comparable demands, error/output, intention/choice, timing, perceived effort, stopping and actual obligations. Keep supports that enable function; do not deliberately remove needed supports or induce distress. Record competing predictions before feedback; preserve ordinary counterexamples. If high output at high cost, identify actual adverse consequences rather than assume pathology. If difficulty only in specific task, assess context first without ruling out comorbidity. Abrupt adult onset requires differential consideration; incomplete childhood records increase uncertainty. Qualified assessment needed for diagnostic question; no self-diagnosis or treatment order.
5. For research require suitable four-group designs and actual interaction estimates with uncertainty; consider test reliability/norms, sample selection, referral, age, state, medication, role of IQ composition and ecological validity. Separate within-person contrasts, group differences, actual moderation and causation. Null not equivalence; significant versus nonsignificant not a significant difference. No quantitative extrapolation to 150–160+. Preserve Q-02-tail psychometric uncertainty.
6. Revise local conclusions and dependent predictions with new observations; retain facts, competing explanations and uncertainty. Route autism/learning/other 2e to GFT-13, support mechanisms and resource costs to GFT-14, distress/functioning and clinical boundaries to GFT-15, measured tail and measurement to 16/17, integrated individual analysis and final limitations to19/20. ## New wholly fictional integration cases
A. Fictional planetarium technician learns complex projection software quickly, misses mundane equipment-return deadlines, and concentrates for hours on novel simulations. Manager infers gifted boredom only, coworker ADHD from absorption. Compare routine-task fit, initiation/remembering, workload and sleep; examine history and independently observed commitments across settings, and whether voluntary structured reminders help without changing task interest. New: reminders improve returns, but longstanding lost commitments in valued activities and multiple settings reported from school and home. Withdraw exclusive boredom account; developmental attention/executive hypothesis becomes more relevant for specialist assessment, not a diagnosis. Preserve actual skill, local support benefit and uncertainty of history; hyperfocus not diagnostic, no IQ estimate.
B. Fictional ceramics kiln operator works late refining a glaze, reports high goal value but postpones recording results, chooses an immediate small reimbursement over delayed larger payment. Analyst attributes all to dopamine and weak impulse control. Compare actual quality, stopping goals, fatigue, task clarity, reward uncertainty, effort cost and motor versus choice. Predictions: reliable future reimbursement changes choice, structured log changes recording, quality criterion changes stopping. New: verified delayed reimbursement accepted, prefilled log completed promptly, repeated glaze refinements have no quality improvement and operator reports concern about errors. Revise general reward/inhibition account; local uncertainty, implementation and possible perfectionism more useful hypotheses, no proven mediator or clinical inference. Retain original delay, output and costs; no forced withdrawal of supports.
C. Fictional study samples clinic-referred adults with abbreviated IQ>=120 and ADHD, finds normative average WM and positive association of IQ with work achievement, plus elevated HF questionnaire ratings relative to general controls. Analyst claims IQ eliminates EF impairment but uniquely produces ADHD hyperfocus. Compare four groups, test precision/selection, actual output vs support burden and questionnaire vs objective stopping. New: matched high-IQ controls perform better on WM but similarly endorse absorption; observed stopping difficulty varies by task, and both ADHD groups show actual missed commitments in some settings. Withdraw causal compensation/unique HF claims; preserve relative group contrast and actual reported/observed measures; no equivalence or diagnosis from discrepancy. Cannot transfer to IQ150–160+ without separate valid measurement and cohort.

## Distinctions and formal counterexamples (illustrations, not empirical estimates)
A. Functional map specifies candidate processes, inputs, goals, actions, outputs and possible relationships; arrows explicitly typed (hypothesis, prediction, causal or descriptive) and scope stated. A fitted dynamic model adds transition and observation rules, parameters and uncertainty. Structural/causal interpretation additionally needs justified assumptions and discriminating evidence; fit and parameter estimability within model are not proof of unique mechanism. Distinguish structural identifiability (unique parameter under ideal model/observations) from practical identifiability (finite, noisy, sparse data) and causal identification (target intervention contrast under assumptions). 
B. Minimal state-space example: s_(t+1)=F(s_t,u_t,theta)+w_t; y_t=H(s_t,u_t)+v_t. s is hypothesized state; u is external context/input; y measured, w process variation and v measurement error. Parameters may vary by person, task or period, but these differences require support. Don't treat reports as exact latent values. A variable called effort in EXE/MOT/STR may target different constructs (INT-01); align construct, units, timescale and level before linking. A model can use state variables without claiming them as distinct neural entities. Separate dynamics of actual person and updates to analyst's epistemic model (GFT-19.3).
C. Exact interval counterexample, deterministic local illustration: dx/dt=0, dy/dt=x, dz/dt=y. Over h>0, x(t+h)=x(t), y(t+h)=y(t)+h*x(t), z(t+h)=z(t)+h*y(t)+(h^2/2)*x(t). Direct x-to-z derivative is zero, yet lagged transition includes x-to-z coefficient h²/2 through y. Derived by integration; no stationary or bounded process assumed, valid as a local mathematical example. In a linear time-invariant system ds/dt=A*s, transition matrix exp(A*h) generally differs from A and incorporates indirect paths. A lagged association alone does not show direct causal influence; observational confounding can additionally generate prediction. Time-index genuine proposed loops x_t→y_(t+1)→x_(t+2), without claiming reciprocal influence merely from two predictive coefficients.
D. Distinguish feedback from recursion and nonlinearity: recursive state update need not involve two processes; feedback may be self-regulating or amplifying, linear or nonlinear. A positive pair of cross-links does not by itself prove unstable runaway behavior; self-decay and all parameters matter (eigenvalues for local linear stability, with discrete/continuous criteria distinct). Emergent macro-pattern is an operational outcome of combined processes that the posited model generates, not a mysterious extra faculty. Example: two independent binary prerequisites a,b in {0,1} and outcome q=a*b; neither alone suffices, a change in both produces outcome, but outcome can also arise from threshold rule or a common external switch. This is a non-additive toy mapping, not proof of causal emergence or an irreducible psychic property. Bifurcation is a qualitative dynamic regime change under a parameter variation in specified equations; observed abrupt change may instead be external shock, measurement change or selection. No trait/IQ threshold inferred.
## Operational extension (PRACTICAL_HEURISTIC)
1. Define explanandum and useful forecast, baseline state, context, observation channel, time horizon and unit (episode, person, dyad, population). Build minimal functional map: opportunity/task demand, knowledge and capacity, appraisal and valuation, goal/choice, implementation, outcomes/feedback, costs/recovery. Include only evidenced or explicitly hypothetical links, no obligatory sequence. Personality may constrain parameter/context distributions or describe stable patterns; avoid a separate latent cause inferred from same outcome. Social response and external supports can be inputs or evolving states if relevant. Distinct positive/negative affect, functioning and distress, effort and reward, executive capacity and performance retained.
2. Ask what would be gained from state dynamics beyond a static description. Compare M0 context/mean/persistence, M1 self-dynamics + measured external drivers, M2 coupled states and candidate feedback, M3 nonlinear/state-varying or multiscale model. These are illustrative families, not required nested sequence. Add complexity only for distinguishable prediction, explanatory restriction or identified intervention contrast. Check whether apparent coupling follows shared task timing, common cause, measurement overlap, trend, selection, reverse influence, stable between-person differences or external shocks. Do not infer every arrow or a single master cause from a neat architecture.
3. Specify timescale from actual phenomenon and design: seconds/minutes for local responses, hours/days for task/recovery, months/years for development, only when justified. Sampling interval and duration are distinct; sparse measurements cannot identify arbitrarily fast mechanisms, longer series cannot fix aliasing automatically. Align timestamps and event timing, consider measurement aggregation, lag sensitivity, missingness and observation reactivity. Distinguish process vs measurement model, between-person vs within-person, stable vs time-varying parameters; compare plausible models rather than assume stationarity or transfer. No fixed universal sampling rule.
4. Validate with purpose-matched checks: structural assumptions and parameter uncertainty, residual patterns and data quality; time-ordered holdout vs simple benchmark on same target/horizon; independent person holdout if claiming generalization; sensitivity to interval, measurement, alternative drivers and initial conditions. Avoid data leakage, tuning to holdout or deleting substantive shocks to rescue model. Parameter recovery simulations can test feasibility under assumed model, not show it is true. If alternative models indistinguishable, retain uncertainty and narrower observations. 
5. For causal mechanism, specify a contrast under intervention and whether it can be isolated; require appropriate evidence and assumptions (GFT-19.2). Predicted intervention effect is model-conditional until tested; no deliberate distress, removal of supports, clinical self-experiment or diagnosis. If no useful, safe, discriminating observation, stop or defer. GFT-19.3 handles root provenance, critical-premise propagation, revised architecture and use threshold. Model useful for self-understanding may be qualitative if adequate; mathematical complexity not an end in itself.

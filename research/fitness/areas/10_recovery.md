## 23. Recovery decision workflow

Before changing training because “не восстановился”:

1. Is performance actually reduced?
2. Is soreness/pain unusual?
3. How was sleep?
4. Was previous volume/intensity unusual?
5. Is there illness?
6. Is nutrition/hydration adequate?
7. Is stress unusually high?
8. Is decline one day or a trend?
9. Modify only as much as needed.
10. Escalate to medical evaluation if red flags.

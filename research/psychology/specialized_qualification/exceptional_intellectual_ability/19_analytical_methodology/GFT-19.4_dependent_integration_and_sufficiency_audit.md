## Correction and regression test — GFT-19.1
Original illustrative equation mixed unrestricted person fixed intercepts and a separately interpreted between-person term. GFT-19.2 item 7 correctly noted nonidentifiability, but the original file remained ambiguous. Cause: combining fixed and random intercept specifications without their identification restrictions; delayed propagation of a known correction. Original clause repaired in this commit, history preserved. Evidence ledger records correction, not new empirical claim.
Algebra: let m_i be the person mean of X. If Y_it=a_i+b_Wi(X_it-m_i)+b_B m_i+e_it, for arbitrary d set b_B'=b_B+d and a_i'=a_i-d*m_i; all fitted values are identical. Hence b_B cannot be separately identified with unrestricted a_i. Demeaning removes both intercept and person mean. For a model with alpha+u_i instead of unrestricted a_i, suitable conditional mean restrictions on u_i, correct errors, sufficient variation and rank can identify a between-person conditional association; does not establish cause. The standard common within slope may be expanded to random slopes with justified assumptions and adequate data; separate between-person regression is another distinct estimand. 
Regression illustration (fictional): two people X=(1,3) and (5,7), means 2 and 6; within slope 2; fixed intercepts 10 and 20; b_B=1 predicts Y=(10,14) and (24,28). Set b_B'=3, a_1'=6, a_2'=8; predictions remain (10,14) and (24,28). Demeaned X=(-1,1) and demeaned Y=(-2,2) for each. No identified between coefficient from this model. Regression check confirms original ambiguity removed; prior comparison of baseline vs personal model, causal guardrails and observation-preservation remain unchanged. Means used for prediction must be computed on training information when required, not future data. No empirical data or proof of competence gain.
## Integrated operating extension — practical heuristic, not new universal protocol
Use canonical INT-04 C0 and minimum relevant checklists; deep gate on material uncertainty/high stakes/structural inference, C10→INT-02/private update card, C11→INT-03, C12 final audit. This is a bounded extension, not replacement.
1. Define purpose, decision relevance and minimal explanandum. Type each proposition: observed experience, descriptive pattern, capacity/preference, state/trait, functional relation, prediction, causal mechanism or clinical/action proposition. Record conditions, time, level, measurement and whether inference affects useful understanding. Preserve strengths, actual constraints and the person's stated experience without inferring IQ or diagnosis. No personal data in cabinet.
2. Reconstruct the observation architecture: independent roots, exact observation versus proxy/interpretation, event/source/method independence, measurement precision and context; distinguish external group evidence from actual person evidence. For high-score claims use GFT-02/16: current age-appropriate test/norms, ceilings, uncertainty, actual local sample and outcome support. A0 observation of a high score is not A1 adequate adult-tail comparative inference. GFT-17: check invariance, selection, local curve support and uncertainty; declining correlation or local slope not automatically a new type. Never infer mechanism from IQ or reject a supported local association just because it is inconvenient.
3. Build minimally sufficient M0 (observed pattern, access/requirements, skill, context, mean/persistence where applicable), M1 (one or several independently justified pathways: appraisal/value, control, state, learning, support, social response), M2 (coupling, feedback and multiscale relations if distinguishable), M3 (nonlinear regimes/parameter changes when necessary). These labels are local model families, not fixed nesting or competence levels. Compare alternative direction, common driver, measurement and no-effect models; preserve parallel processes where useful. Distinguish output, effort, cost, recovery, affect, meaning, functioning and clinical/safety concerns; multiple outcomes may diverge. Do not mistake conceptual coherence for empirical identification or equate complexity with depth.
4. For each material bridge identify the claim, evidence roots, necessary assumptions and competing predictions, same target/time/context, observations that could distinguish or leave ambiguous, and actual consequences of uncertainty. Quantitative models use explicit observation/transition rules, adequate variation, temporal holdout and appropriate new-person split, fair baseline, no preprocessing leakage, uncertainty and model sensitivity. Qualitative analysis does not require artificial coefficients or mathematical decoration. Test differences with available ordinary safe observations only if useful; no invasive inquiry, induced distress, withheld support or clinical self-experiment. Confounding, selection, feedback and lag interpretation per 19.2/18; if indistinguishable, state narrower findings rather than assert unique causes.
5. Maintain epistemic graph distinct from causal/time-indexed graph. Root provenance cannot be multiplied by copying, consensus or derived claims. New contradictory information triggers earliest failing premise check, re-evaluation of all dependent claims, including those that still have independent support, and update of confidence, boundaries, architecture and use permissions separately. Preserve verified observations, revise only supported parts; do not rescue a favored model with endless moderators. If two models predict similarly, prefer sufficient practical account without claiming the richer model false; if richer model provides validated distinct information, accept narrow gain without causal upgrade.
6. Deliver useful interpretation at appropriate confidence: supported observations, plausible rival explanations, scope, implications for self-understanding and only relevant uncertainty. Stop if additional evidence/complexity does not change that output. Use safeguards of GFT-14/15, INT/ETH/SAF for clinical/safety boundary; persistent significant distress, abrupt functional deterioration or acute danger warrants suitable qualified action independently of IQ or mechanism certainty. No diagnosis/treatment recommendation from this protocol. A decision threshold does not increase truth.
## New wholly fictional multi-stage assessment and regression
Case A (measurement and multiple processes). An amateur clock restoration group member reports a very high childhood reasoning score, carefully restores mechanisms but misses group deadlines, reports low satisfaction. Analyst claims extreme adult intelligence causes perfectionism and poor executive function. M0 differing deadline/access/skill; M1 quality criterion and motivation; M2 fatigue/feedback. Distinguish childhood score from current measurement, accomplishment, deadlines, subjective effort/meaning and impairment. Prediction: at matched scope, predictable access changes delay if access primary; clear criterion changes extra work if uncertainty contributes. Update 1: reliable bench access restores deadlines without changing quality, member still prefers complex work. Narrow delay model to context and preserve preference, actual skill and no diagnosis. Update 2: a calibrated current adult test confirms high ability but separate within-range speed weakness; a credible adult comparison study shows specific time-pressure interaction, not motivation difference. Accept bounded score/profile and group result only, not individual causal explanation; consider both contextual and profile predictions. Do not infer all difficulties from IQ; meaningful persistent distress assessed separately.
Case B (dynamic ambiguity and statistical regression). A fictional lighthouse maintenance training program repeatedly measures confidence and signal-check accuracy. Between-person association positive, within-person changes inconsistent; analyst fits fixed person intercepts plus person mean and interprets coefficient as trait effect, then adds a confidence↔accuracy loop. Apply algebra above; between effect nonidentified, re-specify separately, no causal conclusion. M0 prior familiarity and shift conditions; M1 possible time-ordered appraisal/strategy; M2 loop only if predictions differ. Update1: weather independently affects both and confidence is logged after checks; revise lag and common cause explanation, preserve measured accuracy. Update2: time-ordered held-out evaluation shows coupled model no meaningful demonstrated gain over individual baseline plus conditions, with imprecise contrast. Prefer simple predictive account, no equivalence/absence of feedback proven; decline unsupported causal architecture. No deliberate hazards or task degradation.
Case C (cascading correction and selective retention). Fictional library exhibition assistant reports difficulty starting new displays; meeting summary and two reviewers repeat same source, independent inventory documents completed displays and return dates, but no initiation times. Analyst links claimed initiation deficit to a trait and later to exhaustion through reciprocal motivation. Separate actual completion, start times, preference, constraints, energy and source roots. Update1: original report refers to waiting for permission, not starting independently; remove necessary premise of initiation deficit, downgrade dependent trait/loop, keep completion record; alternative access vs initiation under available ordinary contexts. Update2: permissions are now prompt but new severe fatigue persists at home and work; preserve improvement and revise scope of context-only account, consider coexisting health/sleep/clinical possibilities and appropriate qualified assessment, do not replace failed theory with diagnosis. Group source discrepancy does not invalidate directly observed completion. Stop once useful distinction established, no extra personal profiling.

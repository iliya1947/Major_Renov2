## 3. Root vs ramus terminology audit

PASS.

Canonical sequence:

`spinal cord segment → dorsal/ventral roots → mixed spinal nerve → dorsal/ventral rami`

Project rule:

**root ≠ ramus.**

This is closure-critical because confusing these levels breaks later neural localization.

## 4. Plexus terminology audit

PASS.

Brachial-plexus “roots” are:
- C5–T1 **ventral rami**.

They are not:
- dorsal roots;
- ventral roots.

Project rule:

**always identify whether “root” means spinal root or plexus-root/ventral-ramus convention.**

# C. Review-design types that are NOT missing scientific question types

## C1. Umbrella review

JBI lists umbrella review as a methodology.

However:
- umbrella changes the **unit of inclusion** to reviews/syntheses;
- the underlying scientific question can still be effect, etiology, qualitative, etc.

Final architecture should include an **evidence-source/review-design field**, not a separate universal scientific-question framework.

**Status: CONDITIONAL DESIGN — NOT BLOCKING**

---

## C2. Living systematic review

Living review changes:
- surveillance;
- updating;
- workflow.

It does not change the underlying scientific question.

Keep under RES-29/update methodology.

**Status: NOT A RES-01 GAP**

---

## C3. Rapid review

Rapid review changes methods/time-resource trade-offs, not the underlying scientific question.

It is a review-process variant, not a missing question framework.

**Status: NOT A RES-01 GAP**

---

## C4. Economic evidence review

JBI lists economic evidence as a distinct methodology.

For this project, ordinary:
- time burden;
- equipment cost;
- resource use;
- feasibility
are already captured as outcomes/decision criteria.

A formal economic module becomes necessary only for an explicit question about:
- cost-effectiveness;
- cost-utility;
- cost-benefit;
- economic efficiency.

Because no current canonical topic requires that as its primary question, a full module is not required to close RES-01.

Final router should include:
> **Economic question? → dedicated current-methodology check before use.**

**Status: CONDITIONAL — NOT BLOCKING**

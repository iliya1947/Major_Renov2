# A1 №7 — Кибернетика, сложные системы и самоорганизация: источники картографии

**Назначение:** доказательная опора для первичной карты области. Не глубокий разбор отдельных механизмов.

## Self-organization, emergence and complexity

1. **Gershenson (2025), Self-organizing systems: what, how, and why? npj Complexity 2:10.** https://doi.org/10.1038/s44260-025-00031-5  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: self-organization, emergence, information and complexity are related but non-identical concepts; applications span physical, biological, social and artificial systems.

2. **Crutchfield-related modern synthesis: On principles of emergent organization (2024), Physics Reports 1071:1–47.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2024.04.001  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: physics still lacks a single general law of self-organization; intrinsic computation and evolution operators are proposed as modern formal routes, with major issues of intractability and defining structure.

3. **Lansing (2003), Complex Adaptive Systems. Annual Review of Anthropology 32:183–204.** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.anthro.32.061002.093440  
   Просмотрено: abstract/overview.  
   Для карты: complex adaptive systems connect nonlinear dynamics, self-organization, emergence and adaptive agents across disciplines.

4. **Varley et al. (2025), Information theory for complex systems scientists: What, why, and how. Physics Reports 1148:1–55.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2025.09.007  
   Просмотрено: abstract and accessible overview.  
   Для карты: information theory supplies entropy, mutual information, information dynamics, decomposition, statistical complexity and effective-network tools; information measures must be separated from causal explanation.

5. **Hoel (2026), Quantifying emergent complexity. Patterns 7:101472.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2025.101472  
   Просмотрено: abstract/highlights.  
   Для карты: multiscale causal contribution is an active formal route for studying emergence and scale choice.

## Cybernetics, feedback and regulation

6. **Heylighen & Joslyn (2003), Cybernetics and Second-Order Cybernetics, Encyclopedia of Physical Science and Technology.**  
   Просмотрено: accessible encyclopedia sections.  
   Для карты: feedback, requisite variety, requisite knowledge, self-reference and first-/second-order cybernetics are separate conceptual branches.

7. **Goldstein (2017), Homeostatic systems, biocybernetics, and autonomic neuroscience. Autonomic Neuroscience 208:15–28.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autneu.2017.09.001  
   Просмотрено: abstract and introduction.  
   Для карты: homeostasis, good-regulator ideas, requisite variety and hierarchical regulation belong to a shared biocybernetic lineage.

8. **Sterling (2012), Allostasis: A model of predictive regulation. Physiology & Behavior 106:5–15.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physbeh.2011.06.004  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: allostasis explicitly contrasts predictive regulation with simple error-correcting homeostasis.

9. **McEwen-related modern framing: Defining the brain control of physiological stability (2024), Hormones and Behavior 164:105607.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yhbeh.2024.105607  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: homeostasis, rheostasis and allostasis can be treated as distinct levels of physiological regulation.

10. **Bakule (2008), Decentralized control: An overview. Annual Reviews in Control 32:87–98.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arcontrol.2008.03.004  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: large-scale control introduces decentralization, decomposition, robustness, information constraints and delays.

## Nonlinear dynamics, tipping and resilience

11. **Scheffer et al. (2009), Early-warning signals for critical transitions. Nature 461:53–59.** https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08227  
    Просмотрено: abstract and editorial summary.  
    Для карты: bifurcations, critical slowing down and early-warning signals form a distinct branch, but prediction of transitions remains difficult.

12. **Scheffer et al. (2001), Catastrophic shifts in ecosystems. Nature 413:591–596.** https://doi.org/10.1038/35098000  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: alternative stable states, loss of resilience and abrupt regime shifts are canonical complex-system phenomena.

13. **Artime et al. (2024), Robustness and resilience of complex networks. Nature Reviews Physics 6:114–131.** https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-023-00676-y  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: robustness and resilience are distinct properties; network perturbations can produce cascades, systemic dysfunction and adaptive responses.

14. **Gharari & Razavi (2018), A review and synthesis of hysteresis in hydrology and hydrological modeling. Journal of Hydrology 566:500–519.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2018.06.037  
    Просмотрено: abstract/highlights.  
    Для карты: hysteresis is context-dependent and can indicate path dependence, rate dependence or incomplete modeling.

15. **Frigg (2003), Self-organised criticality—what it is and what it isn’t. Studies in History and Philosophy of Science 34:613–632.** https://doi.org/10.1016/S0039-3681(03)00046-3  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: self-organized criticality has often been overgeneralized; evidence for universality requires separate scrutiny.

## Networks, multiscale organization and adaptation

16. **Sayama et al. (2013), Modeling complex systems with adaptive networks. Computers & Mathematics with Applications 65:1645–1664.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.camwa.2012.12.005  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: adaptive networks explicitly model coevolution of node states and topology.

17. **Berner et al. (2023), Adaptive dynamical networks. Physics Reports 1031.**  
    Просмотрено: accessible introduction.  
    Для карты: temporal networks and adaptive networks must be distinguished; in adaptive networks connectivity depends on node dynamics.

18. **Pessoa (2026), Beyond networks: Toward adaptive models of biological complexity. Physics of Life Reviews 56:67–81.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2025.11.007  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: time-varying topology, multilayer organization, context dependence, history sensitivity and changing state spaces expose limits of static network models.

19. **Kitano (2004), Biological robustness. Nature Reviews Genetics 5:826–837.** https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg1471  
    Просмотрено: abstract/key points.  
    Для карты: robustness can arise from control, alternative mechanisms, modularity and decoupling, with trade-offs involving fragility, performance and evolvability.

20. **Wagner, Pavlicev & Cheverud (2007), The road to modularity. Nature Reviews Genetics 8:921–931.** https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg2267  
    Просмотрено: key points.  
    Для карты: modularity is a multilevel architectural property and can emerge through several evolutionary routes.

## Evolvability, autonomy and self-production

21. **Payne & Wagner (2019), The causes of evolvability and their evolution. Nature Reviews Genetics 20:24–38.** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41576-018-0069-z  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: evolvability concerns the capacity to generate heritable adaptive variation and interacts with robustness and adaptive landscapes.

22. **Houle & Rossoni (2022), Complexity, Evolvability, and the Process of Adaptation. Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics 53:137–159.** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-ecolsys-102320-090809  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: the relation between complexity and evolvability is contested rather than a simple “cost of complexity” law.

23. **Froese-related autonomy line: The autonomy of biological individuals and artificial models (2008), BioSystems 91:309–319.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystems.2007.05.009  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: autonomy should include historical, structural and interactive dimensions, not only internal closure.

24. **From autopoiesis to self-optimization: Toward an enactive model of biological regulation (2023), BioSystems 230:104959.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystems.2023.104959  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: autopoiesis has been extended through precariousness, adaptivity and agency but remains difficult to turn into specific testable biological hypotheses.

## Pattern formation and morphogenesis

25. **Karsenti (2008), Self-organization in cell biology: a brief history. Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology 9:255–262.** https://doi.org/10.1038/nrm2357  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: self-organization concepts from physics/chemistry are used to understand coherent cell-scale organization.

26. **Collinet & Lecuit (2021), Programmed and self-organized flow of information during morphogenesis. Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology 22:245–265.** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41580-020-00318-6  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: biological morphogenesis mixes programmed/hierarchical and self-organized/local-interaction modes rather than fitting a binary split.

27. **Mechanical Patterning in Animal Morphogenesis (2021), Annual Review of Cell and Developmental Biology.** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-cellbio-120319-030931  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: mechanics, biochemical processes, coarse-grained variables and feedback across scales all contribute to robust organization.

28. **Nguyen, Qiu & Vaikuntanathan (2021), Organization and Self-Assembly Away from Equilibrium. Annual Review of Condensed Matter Physics 12:273–290.** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-conmatphys-031218-013309  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: nonequilibrium forcing and energy dissipation constrain emergent organization; thermodynamic claims require literal physical applicability.

## Free-energy principle as a disputed unification

29. **Friston et al. (2023), The free energy principle made simpler but not too simple. Physics Reports.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2023.07.001  
    Просмотрено: abstract/introduction.  
    Для карты: presents self-organization through random dynamical systems, Markov blankets, Bayesian inference and active inference.

30. **Raja et al. (2021), The Markov blanket trick: On the scope of the free energy principle and active inference. Physics of Life Reviews 39:49–72.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2021.09.001  
    Просмотрено: abstract and conclusions.  
    Для карты: strong critique of claims that FEP or active inference provide universal explanations of biological/cognitive self-organization.

31. **Aguilera et al. (2022), How particular is the physics of the free energy principle? Physics of Life Reviews 40:24–50.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2021.11.001  
    Просмотрено: abstract/highlights.  
    Для карты: applicability depends on restrictive assumptions in studied nonequilibrium systems and can fail for history-dependent behavior.

## Modeling and validation

32. **An et al. (2021), Challenges, tasks, and opportunities in modeling agent-based complex systems. Ecological Modelling 457:109685.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2021.109685  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: ABM is valuable for heterogeneous interacting agents and emergent macro-outcomes but has substantial calibration/validation challenges.

33. **Agent-Based Models and Microsimulation (2015), Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application 2:259–272.** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-statistics-010814-020218  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: ABM connects simple micro-rules to emergent behavior, while statistical inference for such models remains difficult.

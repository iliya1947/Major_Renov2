## Constructs and rival explanations
Cost is a vector, not a finite energy store inferred from behavior. Separate time/effort, subjective demand/fatigue, within-task speed/accuracy/variability change, displaced opportunities, recovery time, physical symptoms, later participation and context. Effort is not fatigue; fatigue is not performance decline. Preserved output may reflect extra effort, skill or environmental fit. Decline may reflect sleep, illness, changing demands, practice/order, motivation, anxiety or sensory/social pressure. Separate trait-like fatigue, transient state, recovery and prolonged functional loss. A strategy may reduce cost, shift cost between times or increase participation despite cost. Sustainability means repeated goal attainment, observed costs and recovery over specified conditions, not maximum output or duration. Consider voluntary choice, safety, pressure, belonging, benefit and burden separately. No physiological or clinical inference from metaphor.

## Practical gate
1. Specify actual goal, task, environment, quality/errors, time including preparation and recovery, subjective effort, workload, baseline and sleep/health context. Track immediate fatigue, performance slope, subsequent recovery, repeated functioning and displaced activities separately. No assumption of total mental energy.
2. Compare environmental mismatch, skills, changing demands, mood, sleep, physical health, social pressure, competing priorities and genuine compensatory mechanisms. Cost may precede strategy, follow it, share a cause or come from elsewhere. Preserve benefits and costs, never infer pathology or IQ from success/distress.
3. Observe voluntary comparable ordinary contexts with needed supports intact. Distinguish observer report, subjective experience, performance, actual participation and preference. No forced unmasking, support withdrawal or distress induction. High subjective fatigue with stable performance remains a real report, not proof of cause; absence of observed burden does not rule out unmeasured burden but do not invent it.
4. Separate between-person associations, within-person deviations/centering, temporal lag, baseline-adjusted change, repeated trajectory and causal contrast. Account for shared measures, selection on referral/success, regression to mean, confounders, mediators, order and comparability. Null not equivalence; cross-sectional age not decline. Direct process evidence for mechanism. General hypotheses only for extreme IQ; no diagnosis/treatment.
5. Route general wellbeing to GFT-15 as future handoff, not start it. 14.4 integrates all three blocks and actual GFT-13 canonical closure; preserve Q-14 limitations.

## New wholly fictional cases, internal assessment
A. Municipal archivist meets targets but reports evening exhaustion. Supervisor infers high-IQ compensation of executive deficit and finite-resource drain. Compare interruptions, workload, time/errors, recovery, sleep and context, no IQ inference. New quieter scanner area with same goals/aids lowers fatigue while output stable. Revise toward local environmental load, withdraw unique deficit/depletion claim, preserve actual experience and uncertainty.
B. Amateur orchestra volunteer reports masking and stress at rehearsal, little next-day stress. Analyst predicts inevitable long-term decline. Distinguish concurrent vs lagged/chronic, compare social safety, sensory setting, travel, sleep, benefits and person differences. New welcoming group has lower masking/stress and shorter travel, no detected long-term wellbeing change. Withdraw inevitable decline, keep association and competing accounts, no equivalence or protection claim; no forced disclosure.
C. Adult-language teacher with diagnosed autism develops persistent fatigue and reduced function after schedule expansion. Reviewer equates high burnout scale with diagnosed disease caused by masking. Separate actual functioning, history, differential sleep/physical health/mood/work demands, support and clinical evaluation. New evaluation finds treatable sleep condition, improvement after care and workload change while concealment unchanged. Withdraw masking-only explanation, keep reported symptoms, no unique causal component or exclusion of autistic burnout.

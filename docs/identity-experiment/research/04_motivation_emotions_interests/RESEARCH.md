# A1 №4 — Мотивация, эмоции и интересы: источники картографии

**Назначение:** доказательная опора для первичной карты области. Не глубокий разбор найденных механизмов.

## Мотивация, goals и self-regulation

1. **Eccles & Wigfield (2002), Motivational Beliefs, Values, and Goals. Annual Review of Psychology 53:109–132.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.psych.53.100901.135153.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и структура обзора.  
   Для карты: expectancies, task value, intrinsic motivation, self-determination, interest, goals, attribution и self-regulation представлены как разные теоретические линии.

2. **Wigfield, Muenks & Eccles (2021), Achievement Motivation: What We Know and Where We Are Going. Annual Review of Developmental Psychology 3:87–111.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-devpsych-050720-103500.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступная структура.  
   Для карты: developmental линии motivational beliefs, values, goals, intrinsic/extrinsic motivation, identity, emotion и intervention research.

3. **Inzlicht et al. (2021), Integrating Models of Self-Regulation. Annual Review of Psychology 72:319–345.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-061020-105721.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: self-regulation literature spans multiple levels, conflict, emotion и cognitive functioning; отдельная integrative ветвь.

4. **Brandstätter & Bernecker (2022), Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit. Annual Review of Psychology 73:271–299.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: persistence и disengagement — равноценные стороны goal pursuit; motivation science исторически переоценивала persistence.

5. **Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2025), Psychology of Planning. Annual Review of Psychology 76:303–328.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-021524-110536.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: goals, plans и implementation intentions требуют отдельного различения.

6. **Salamone & Correa (2024), The Neurobiology of Activational Aspects of Motivation: Exertion of Effort, Effort-Based Decision Making, and the Role of Dopamine. Annual Review of Psychology 75:1–32.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020223-012208.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: motivation имеет directional и activational/effort aspects; effort нельзя свести к reward preference.

## Self-determination, needs и intrinsic motivation

7. **Ryan & Deci (2020), Intrinsic and extrinsic motivation from a self-determination theory perspective. Contemporary Educational Psychology 61:101860.** DOI: 10.1016/j.cedpsych.2020.101860.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: intrinsic/extrinsic, autonomous/controlled motivation и basic psychological needs — разные constructs внутри SDT.

8. **Vansteenkiste, Soenens & Ryan (2023), Basic Psychological Needs Theory: A Conceptual and Empirical Review of Key Criteria. Oxford Handbook of Self-Determination Theory.** DOI: 10.1093/oxfordhb/9780197600047.013.5.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/description.  
   Для карты: autonomy, competence, relatedness claims требуют отдельной проверки universality, distinctness и explanatory status.

9. **Fishbach & Woolley (2022), The Structure of Intrinsic Motivation. Annual Review of Organizational Psychology and Organizational Behavior 9:339–363.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-012420-091122.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/overview.  
   Для карты: intrinsic motivation рассматривается как отдельный research program, а не просто synonym interest/enjoyment.

10. **Bureau et al. (2022), Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research 92:46–72.** DOI: 10.3102/00346543211042426.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: empirical line on need support, need satisfaction и autonomous/controlled motivation; context and source of support matter.

## Interest, curiosity и boredom

11. **Renninger & Hidi (2022), Interest: A unique affective and cognitive motivational variable that develops. Advances in Motivation Science 9:179–239.** DOI: 10.1016/bs.adms.2021.12.004.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/preview.  
    Для карты: interest is both state during engagement and motivation to reengage; linked but not identical to curiosity/intrinsic motivation.

12. **Hidi & Renninger (2026), The four-phase model of interest development: Addressing individual differences. Learning and Individual Differences.** DOI: 10.1016/j.lindif.2025.102865.  
    Просмотрено: highlights и preview.  
    Для карты: interest development explicitly includes individual variation and sociocultural context.

13. **Yan et al. (2025), From situational interest and state curiosity to personal interest: developmental pathways and underlying mechanisms. Frontiers in Psychology 16:1700208.** DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1700208.  
    Просмотрено: full accessible HTML sections.  
    Для карты: situational interest, state curiosity and personal interest must be kept conceptually separate.

14. **Jirout, Evans & Son (2024), Curiosity in children across ages and contexts. Nature Reviews Psychology 3:622–635.** DOI: 10.1038/s44159-024-00346-5.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: curiosity as internally motivated information seeking in response to knowledge gaps; developmental/contextual line.

15. **Curiosity-driven exploration: foundations in neuroscience and computational modeling (2023). Trends in Neurosciences 46:1054–1066.** DOI: 10.1016/j.tins.2023.10.002.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: no single accepted curiosity framework; novelty, uncertainty and information seeking form separate dimensions.

16. **Seiler & Dan (2024), Boredom and curiosity: the hunger and the appetite for information. Frontiers in Psychology 15:1514348.** DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1514348.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and overview.  
    Для карты: boredom and curiosity can both drive information seeking and need to be distinguished experimentally.

## Emotion theory

17. **Scherer & Moors (2019), The Emotion Process: Event Appraisal and Component Differentiation. Annual Review of Psychology 70:719–745.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-122216-011854.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible overview.  
    Для карты: appraisal, action tendencies, physiology, expression, conscious feeling and labeling are distinct components/stages.

18. **Yeo & Ong (2024), Associations between cognitive appraisals and emotions: A meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin 150:1440–1471.** DOI: 10.1037/bul0000452.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: appraisal-emotion literature is large but heterogeneous in appraisal dimensions, terminology and predictions.

19. **van Heijst, Kret & Ploeger (2025), Basic Emotions or Constructed Emotions: Insights From Taking an Evolutionary Perspective. Perspectives on Psychological Science 20:377–391.** DOI: 10.1177/17456916231205186.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full-text availability.  
    Для карты: basic-emotion vs constructed-emotion remains an active theoretical divide.

20. **Barrett et al. (2025), The Theory of Constructed Emotion: More Than a Feeling. Perspectives on Psychological Science 20:392–420.** DOI: 10.1177/17456916251319045.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: competing emotion theories rest on different assumptions and evidence standards; they cannot be treated as minor variants.

## Новые или повторно углублённо проверенные источники A2

### Hidi & Renninger (2026) — The four-phase model of interest development: Addressing individual differences
Learning and Individual Differences  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2025.102865

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/highlights and available publisher preview.  
**Что меняет в карте:** interest conceptualized as a motivational variable that can move from triggered situational interest toward more enduring individual interest, with individual/contextual variation. Для A2 это усиливает separate question **when repeated engagement actually becomes personal interest rather than repeated situational capture**.

### Yan et al. (2025) — From situational interest and state curiosity to personal interest: developmental pathways and underlying mechanisms
Frontiers in Psychology 16:1700208  
https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1700208

**Фактически просмотрено в A2:** полный доступный HTML, including abstract, framework and discussion.  
**Что меняет в карте:** review separates situational interest from state curiosity and proposes distinct developmental pathways toward personal interest involving repeated engagement, information-value evaluation, competence, self-relevance and knowledge acquisition. Critically, the authors also state that not every transient interest/curiosity state becomes stable personal interest. Для карты это делает **state→enduring-interest transition** отдельной unresolved branch rather than assumption.

### Sansone, Renninger & Harackiewicz (2026) — Motivating Self-Regulation: Utility Value, Interest Development, and the Potential for Intervention
Educational Psychology Review 38:56  
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10142-7

**Фактически просмотрено:** полный open-access HTML.  
**Что меняет в карте:** value and interest both direct attention/effort but are not identical; perceived utility can increase engagement without necessarily increasing interest, and compatibility between developing interest and perceived utility matters. The review also emphasizes that people can regulate their own motivation and generate connections to personal goals/values. Для A2 это резко усиливает distinction **useful/valuable ≠ interesting** and the need to separate external intervention from self-generated relevance.

### Kobayashi et al. (2024) — Neural mechanisms of information seeking
Neuron  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38703774/

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract and review metadata/overview.  
**Что меняет в карте:** information seeking is studied as value-based decision-making involving subjective information value, novelty and uncertainty; curiosity does not reduce to external reward. Для A2 это supports an independent branch **information value / epistemic value vs ordinary utility and reward**.

### Seiler & Dan (2024) — Boredom and curiosity: the hunger and the appetite for information
Frontiers in Psychology 15:1514348  
https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1514348

**Фактически просмотрено:** full accessible HTML.  
**Что меняет в карте:** boredom and curiosity can produce overlapping information-seeking behavior while differing phenomenologically and functionally; same observed action can arise from different motivational states. This is a strong warning against inferring interest/curiosity from repeated information seeking alone.

### Yeo & Ong (2024) — Associations Between Cognitive Appraisals and Emotions: A Meta-Analytic Review
Psychological Bulletin 150:1440–1471  
https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000452

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/metadata and accessible article summary.  
**Что меняет в карте:** appraisal-emotion relations are systematic but heterogeneous; event significance depends on interpretation/appraisal dimensions rather than being an intrinsic property of the event. Для нашей карты особенно важна distinction between **event features and person-relative relevance/meaning**.

### Butera et al. (2024) — Achievement Goals: A Social Influence Cycle
Annual Review of Psychology 75  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-013123-102139

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract and accessible Annual Reviews page.  
**Что меняет в карте:** goals are shaped by cultural/social structures and then themselves influence social environments. This supports a reciprocal rather than purely internal view of motivational development and connects area №4 with №5 and №1.

### Brandstätter & Bernecker (2022) — Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit
Annual Review of Psychology 73:271–299  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710

**Фактически повторно использовано из A1:** abstract and available sections previously inspected.  
**Что меняет в A2:** persistence is not a universal sign of adaptive/strong motivation; disengagement and reprioritization can be appropriate. This is important for future “interest formation” because genuine trajectory need not monotonically intensify.

## A2 — уточнения, полученные из совокупности источников

1. **“Significance” is not one psychological variable.** Depending on literature it can mean attentional salience, goal relevance, subjective task value, utility, interest, novelty, uncertainty reduction, information value, identity/self relevance or emotional appraisal.

2. **Attention capture is not interest.** Novelty, surprise, threat, boredom or task demand can produce attention/reengagement without stable personal interest.

3. **Repeated engagement is not sufficient evidence of enduring interest.** Reengagement can be externally required, habit-like, instrumental, socially rewarded or curiosity-driven. Interest development literature treats stable personal interest as a developmental outcome, not a synonym for repetition.

4. **Value and interest interact but are separable.** Something can be useful without being interesting; something can be interesting without high instrumental utility. This matters directly for avoiding a single generic “importance score”.

5. **Personal relevance can be generated, not merely pre-existing.** Utility-value research shows people can construct connections between content and their current goals/lives. But this does not mean external designers can assign a value and call it internalized.

6. **Transient curiosity is not the same as stable interest.** Curiosity can arise around a knowledge gap and resolve after information is obtained; personal interest implies a broader tendency to reengage with a domain over time.

7. **Information seeking is multiply determined.** Curiosity, boredom, uncertainty avoidance, instrumental utility and social demand can produce superficially similar behavior.

8. **Observed choice does not uniquely identify motive.** Same action can reflect goal pursuit, habit, social pressure, information seeking, avoidance, exploration or constrained choice.

9. **External reward/praise does not automatically become value.** It can change behavior or engagement without establishing self-endorsed value/interest. Internalization is a separate process and belongs partly to social/identity literature №5.

10. **Goal/value endorsement can be socially shaped without becoming merely externally imposed.** Social/cultural contexts influence what goals become available and endorsed; the relation is reciprocal and develops over time.

11. **Motivational relevance is relational.** Events become relevant relative to current goals, values, knowledge gaps, competence beliefs, social meaning and history. Event significance therefore cannot be treated as an inherent fixed property.

12. **State→trait-like motivation is not automatic.** Situational interest, state curiosity and transient affect require separate criteria before inferring enduring personal interest or preference.

13. **Motivational trajectory can weaken, redirect or terminate.** Adaptive disengagement and changing interests are part of the space; a future system must not interpret fading interest as failure.

14. **Repeated retrieval/attention can mimic interest.** If past material is repeatedly surfaced by memory or context systems, repeated engagement may be retrieval-induced rather than self-sustained motivational development. This cross-links to gaps №14/19 and belongs technically to №9/11.

15. **Emotion/appraisal can mark person-relative relevance without defining a universal significance rule.** For our project, emotion research provides conceptual distinctions about appraisal and relevance, not a requirement to create or simulate emotional states in the AI.

16. **The future project’s significance problem is fundamentally history-relative.** The psychologically useful question is not “what categories are universally important?” but **how current history, open questions, prior commitments/knowledge and outcomes change what becomes relevant now**. Whether this can be implemented in GPT is a later cross-area synthesis question.

## A2 boundary decisions for area №4

- **Остаётся в №4:** distinctions among motivation/value/interest/curiosity/utility/relevance; state↔enduring interest; autonomous/controlled motivation and self-endorsement at the motivational level; persistence/disengagement; appraisal/relevance and motivational meaning.
- **Primary owner №3:** reinforcement/feedback learning and contingency-shaped updating.
- **Primary owner №5:** identity-specific motives, norm internalization, social approval and relational meaning.
- **Primary owner №9:** engineered goals/rewards/objectives and agent-level priority mechanisms.
- **Primary owner №10:** reward/objective/preference-learning algorithms.
- **Primary owner №12:** wording/instruction choices that may inject values/interests/goals.

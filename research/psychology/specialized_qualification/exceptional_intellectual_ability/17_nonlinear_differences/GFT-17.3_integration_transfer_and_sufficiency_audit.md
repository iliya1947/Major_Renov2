### Integrated procedure
1. Start from observed phenomenon and intended explanatory question. Separate measured ability, edition/age/norms/local measurement uncertainty, specific skills, actual output, effort/cost, recovery, time and context; no inference of current IQ from achievement. Classify directness A0/A1/B–E per 16.3. A selected maximum or earlier talent measure is not adult-tail calibration. Distinguish conditional rarity and norm uncertainty from precision of measurement.
2. Specify estimand: mean level, local derivative, variance, upper boundary, latent loadings/covariance, predictive error, or causal effect. A declining correlation can coexist with unchanged slope; a reversal need not mean lower absolute level. Constructed example Y=100+2x-.5x², x in [0,3], peaks at x=2; Y(3)=101.5 still above Y(0)=100. Not an IQ estimate or empirical relation. Distinguish outcome scale and direction. A decision threshold need not be a biological discontinuity.
3. Compare simple and more complex models with actual support, reliability, floor/ceiling, measurement invariance in both predictor/outcome, selection/confounding, age/time, overlap, local uncertainty and validation. Test slope contrasts and breakpoint uncertainty, predefine meaningful effect margins, use nested/independent evaluation. Separate global fit and local tail performance. No non-significance as equivalence, no common-factor loading moderation as mechanism, no curve as proof of discrete categories. Real local nonlinearities remain possible.
4. Individual analysis: compare task demands, ability/profile, learning, motivation/interest, strategy, environment, exposure, appraisal, health and support. Separate wellbeing, symptoms, function and cost, not a single intelligence-dependent score. Use naturally available comparisons without withholding aids or creating distress. Identify rival predictions and revise local explanations when evidence changes. Do not infer diagnoses or adult extreme-tail phenotype from group curves. Persistent distress or acquired marked decline warrants appropriate qualified assessment, independently of ability.
5. Handoff GFT-18/19: use dynamic and competing explanatory models only when they add discriminating prediction, no category by IQ; preserve outcome/measurement/source specificity and individual evidence.

27.11.2026. COMPLETE_FOR_SPECIFIED_SCOPE (bounded general scientific-methodological level). Prospective card. Kira-1; starting HEAD 110ec22d7293c4661c0431f630edb51d0ccd771b.
Purpose: distinguish reported strategies, observed use, causal benefit, transfer to everyday function, sustained effect, and change in underlying ability; examine whether measured intelligence moderates effects.
1. Seek primary adult experiments/RCTs and relevant syntheses on cognitive offloading, prospective memory aids, organizational/metacognitive strategies in ADHD, autism supports and assistive tools for learning difficulties. Include contrary results, active comparators, ecological outcomes, duration and selection.
2. Contrast independent effects with bundled interventions, task-specific effects with transfer, voluntary/environmental supports with masking; distinguish immediate assisted performance, independent acquisition and retained skills. No withdrawal of essential accommodations; no individualized treatment.
3. Seek direct high-IQ and measured adult IQ150–160+ strategy-effect/moderation studies, distinguishing IQ main effects, group comparisons and strategy-by-IQ interaction; carry GFT-13 unresolved dependencies.

## Evidence
E1. Gilbert 2015, https://doi.org/10.1080/17470218.2014.972963, indexed primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25404057/, publisher403: four online experiments N1196, reminder use improved task performance and varied with memory load and distraction. Laboratory task weakly predicted later naturalistic intention fulfillment; not an experiment showing all everyday failures prevented by reminders. Gilbert et al. 2020 issue (online2019) https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31448938/, indexed primary author text; direct CAPTCHA: choice between external reminders and unaided memory under explicitly specified payoff/cost structure; reminder use could exceed payoff-optimal rate, associated with underconfidence. That optimum is task-defined, not a normative human requirement; subjective effort minimization, safety and accessibility may make extra support rational. Boldt & Gilbert 2019 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31792746/, indexed abstract: confidence predicted spontaneous/instructed reminder use beyond objective memory ability. 2026 training study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41817942/ indexed abstract: experiment1 N164, predictions and feedback improved calibration/strategy in tested task. These are overlapping research lineage but separate reported experiments, not independent method validation. 2023 review https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35789477/ indexed text synthesizes prior studies; no additional replication count. No claim of IQ-specific benefit.
E2. Ball et al. 2022 issue (online2021) https://doi.org/10.1080/09658211.2021.1991380 and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34665690/, indexed author abstract; publisher cache miss, PubMed CAPTCHA. Higher measured working memory capacity related to better unaided intention fulfillment; reported difference absent when offloading permitted. This addresses WM capacity not FSIQ; precise interaction coefficient, ceiling, and design analysis not directly verified, so no equivalence claim or adult gifted-specific moderator. Peper et al. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36201804/ indexed abstract: experimental variation of memory load/reminder type, benefits conditional on task. 2025 prospective/retrospective comparison https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40667475/ indexed author text contrasts prior prospective WMC associations and retrospective nulls; own exact results not adopted. 2026 age study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13576502/ indexed title/methods, direct cache miss; candidate counterexample to universal elimination of performance gaps; no numerical effect or specific null adopted. 2026 ADHD prospective memory future-thinking trial https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42475007/ indexed primary abstract N196, 98 ADHD and98 comparison adults randomized among encoding conditions; full results unavailable, no estimate or recommended protocol adopted.
E3. Grinschgl, Papenmeier & Meyerhoff 2021 https://doi.org/10.1177/17470218211008060, direct Sage selected introduction/methods/general discussion and results excerpts: pattern-copy experiments, manipulation of access cost, subsequent-memory-test awareness, and forced maximum vs chosen offloading. Immediate efficiency improved with more externalization; later memory for offloaded material poorer in unannounced-test conditions. Awareness altered effects, with mixed findings across free-choice conditions and evidence against inevitable loss under explicit learning. Access delay also allows rehearsal; statistical mediation conditional on assumptions not sole causal mechanism. Specific visual material, task and retention interval; not evidence permanent memory deterioration or reason to remove accessibility aids. Distinguish goal of access/output from goal of internal retention; GFT-06.3 prior study lineage may overlap, count once.
E4. Solanto et al. 2010 https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2009.09081123, PMC direct https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3633586/ selected methods/table/results/moderation/discussion inspected. RCT N88, 45 group metacognitive therapy vs43 supportive group, 12 weeks; stratified by medication. Blind clinician-rated inattention change adjusted for baseline favored MCT by 2.7 points CI[0.9,4.6]; both groups improved. Self-report effect varied by baseline severity; Brown not significantly different, ON-TOP marginal, observer difference not robust to exclusion of noncompleters/medication changers. No post-treatment maintenance test in this trial. Composite package includes planning/organization, task decomposition, cognitive strategies and group support; not single-component causal efficacy or objective general cognitive improvement. Estimated WAIS-III four-subtest mean119.23 SD11.47 vs114.07 SD14.10. IQ did not significantly interact with assigned intervention in exploratory moderator analyses; restricted range, sample size and uncertainty acknowledged. No equivalence, no claim IQ irrelevant, no separate 150–160+ subgroup.

Blinded symptom rating responder proportions 67% vs33% by ADHD rating scale; responders/partial responders maintained gains at 6/12 months. This selected maintenance description is not identical to durable average between-group efficacy; symptoms distinct from functioning. Abstract CGI effect magnitude -0.0531 inconsistent with reported CI[-1.01,-.05] as a point estimate near edge; no CGI magnitude adopted, exact original analysis not checked (Q-14-source-03 OPEN for transcription/precision clarification, do not infer misconduct). Ostinelli et al. 2025 https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(24)00360-2 indexed abstract, PubMed CAPTCHA: network meta-analysis 113 RCTs across interventions; psychological components including CBT better than placebo on clinician-rated symptoms, not self-rated outcomes in main summary. Network assumptions and comparator composition matter; not proof no self-report or functional benefit. No pharmacological recommendation.

## Practical transfer gate
1. Start with desired outcome (reliable task completion, learning/retention, participation, comfort, independence as person defines it); distinguish actual benefit from looking typical, and supported functioning from unsupported test. Do not treat tool dependence or requesting accommodations as failure.
2. Match support to observed bottleneck rather than diagnosis/IQ: memory cues for delayed intention, visible sequence for task-state loss, environmental change for access, alternative modality for information access, explicit learning/retrieval when independent retention is goal. These are conditional practical hypotheses, not universally validated prescriptions. No coercive masking or removal of needed supports. Consider simpler free tools first, user goals and feasibility.
3. Measure outcome and alternative explanation: completion/errors, quality, time including setup, reliability, subjective effort, learning/retention and transfer in ordinary conditions. Compare comparable baseline or safe incremental alternatives with consent; no confounding of task difficulty, expertise, workload or feedback. Maintain needed accommodations. Benefit can be context-specific and still worthwhile.
4. Separate estimands: package effect vs individual component; assisted score vs unassisted score; near vs far transfer; post-test vs sustained real function; symptom vs participation; within-group change vs treatment-comparator difference. Association of use with outcome vulnerable to confounding by need and adherence. Test strategy×measured IQ interaction with adequate range and uncertainty; not compare significant vs nonsignificant within groups. WMC/education and IQ different constructs.
5. Preserve positive effects and adverse/trade-off evidence together. Explicit retention goals may alter offloading consequences; no global claim that external tools make memory weaker. Evaluate actual resource costs separately at14.3. No high-tail inference, no self-diagnosis or individual treatment order. GFT-13 unresolved dependencies to14.4.
## New wholly fictional contradiction-update cases
A. Fictional amateur observatory volunteer uses calendar alerts, attends more sessions but forgets telescope setup details without notes. Analyst asserts notes have damaged intelligence. Compare distinct outcome (arrival vs retention), task load, experience, explicit retention practice and availability; do not remove aids. New: alerts still prevent missed meetings and volunteer learns the sequence by adding brief voluntary recall while notes remain accessible. Revise global damage claim to distinct local benefits and learning demands; no proof recall component alone caused improvement or latent decline. 
B. Fictional adult education workshop reports only average-IQ subgroup significantly improves self-rated organization after a ten-session course; higher-IQ subgroup p>.05. Analyst prescribes different course solely by IQ. Need comparable outcomes, baseline, actual interaction and uncertainty, randomized comparator and follow-up, support preference. New: high-IQ subgroup had near-ceiling baseline outcome, both groups improved objective deadline completion under structured supports; no direct group-effect contrast. Withdraw deterministic IQ rule and causal claims, preserve self-report result and actual deadline observation; do not infer equivalence, no extreme-tail transfer.
C. Fictional library digitization trainee uses read-aloud software and completes comprehension questions more accurately; colleague claims decoding deficit cured and proposes removing software. Separate access and decoding/learning criteria, quality, independent skill measure if relevant and consented, fatigue and preferences. New: comprehension remains strong with software, separate routine reading assessment unchanged; trainee values access over unassisted reading speed for task. Revise cure/removal conclusion, retain supported achievement and task-specific benefit, no diagnosis or global inability.

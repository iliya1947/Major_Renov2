## 2. Corrections and extensions

R1 — Pragmatic reference, communication channel and contextual inference. Keysar and Hanna original abstracts support interference from private information and immediate common-ground use respectively; neither proves a universal sequential processing model. Kruger five experimental email studies concern intended vs recognized tone and sender's confidence, not deception, romantic intention or modern text-message accuracy. Additional primary study Epley & Kruger 2005 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022103104001076 indexed original text indicates expectancies can persist more after email than voice interaction; no precise effect verified. Same authors and related mechanisms, no assumed independent sample lineage. Mediated-romance joint interviews sample18 couples, norms may change by channel, issue and practical access. Unanimous reported preference in this sample for changing modality when conflict escalates is not universal rule or actual observed behavior; not proof of mutual agreement in an individual relationship. Separate utterance, pragmatic implication, intended act, recipient interpretation, motive, reply opportunity, expressed boundary and later conduct; uncertainty about implication does not invalidate clear refusal. Root cause: need stronger task-to-real-world and expectation-vs-agreement distinction. Retest source abstracts and conceptual operational separation.

R2 — Messenger empathic accuracy reporting contradiction and selection. Direct Steinebach Methods/Results: preregistered inclusion relaxed to five complete ratings per couple; retained51 couples, reported57% dropout. 235 rating procedures exceeded planned15-minute timing difference and40 lacked assessable timing; all retained. Indirect accuracy omitted after convergence difficulty; covariance constraints simplified iteratively; covariate choices changed from preregistration, no gender comparisons due to sample. These are not evidence of fraud, but weaken confirmatory interpretation. Communication-frequency measure is composite of standardized message count and mean character volume, not reply latency or count alone. Both tracking and assumed-similarity effects present, authors report stronger own-affect contribution; partner report is imperfect criterion, not objective access to mind. Results/abstract say greater frequency increases arousal tracking, yet reported simple slopes at +1SD B=-.038,t=-1.49,p=.141, at -1SD B=.106,t=4.39,p<.001; difference high-minus-low=-.144, opposite direction to abstract on face of reported labels. Similarly greater experience claimed to increase assumed-similarity valence bias but +1SD B=-.065,t=-1.94,p printed '0.0.056', -1SD B=.229,t=6.79,p<.001; high-minus-low=-.294. Exact interaction tables could not be independently opened; possible sign, coding, simple-slope label or reporting error, not resolved. Do NOT retain direction of either moderator as established. No inference of improving accuracy by increasing messaging. Authors' own post-hoc power calculations estimated 265 and1083 dyads for moderation effects vs observed51; retrospective power not independent evidence of replication probability. Additional Steinebach et al. 2025 https://ojs.interpersonajournal.com/index.php/ojs/article/view/75 same N102/51 couples and authors/method; likely same sample, exact provenance not checked, do not treat as independent. Additional daily-diary study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40244996/ indexed original abstract reports tracking plus own-affect reliance and verbal expression moderator, no coefficients or overlap verified. Root cause: earlier note reproduced abstract moderation directions without reconciling results. Retest direct original text and arithmetic; restrict moderator conclusions pending code/table clarification. Carry to SOC-X01.

R3 — Prospective levels, within-person change, shared cohorts and experimental mechanism. Kanter direct published abstract: within-partner r means predictor and outcome from same partner, NOT within-person deviation; positive .15, cross .09; negative -.17, cross -.16. Dissolution d signed by original coding, not probability. Johnson direct article explicitly states Study1 data also used in prior Johnson 2018 German panel work, and Lavner 2016 used same sample as Nguyen 2020 (four waves); Nguyen observed support and problem-solving interactions, whereas current analyses focus on conflict. Do not count reused data as independent replication. Lavner 7/36 communication-to-satisfaction lagged paths significant at 9 months, none for first-to-last27mo, as reported by Johnson; count not multiplicity-adjusted global test. Johnson Study1 did not assess positive communication and Study3 shortened measures; no general conclusion positive communication is irrelevant. Ross2019 Study2 and Ross2022 VSA both describe N431 newlywed recruitment, same research group, four waves9mo; likely shared project with Lavner2016, exact participant mapping not newly established; preserve nonindependence warning rather than add counts. Additional randomized relationship-education trial Williamson et al. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26501497/ N1034 selected complete cases within Supporting Healthy Marriage, observed communication12mo and satisfaction30mo; indexed original record and author review https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8186435/ report communication changes did not mediate later satisfaction effects. Additional contrary trial ProSAAF https://doi.org/10.1111/jomf.12416 indexed primary abstract N344 rural predominantly low-income African American couples reports communication change statistically mediated program effects. Randomized program not randomized mediator; cannot identify unique causal communication pathway from mediation alone. Different programs, samples, measurements and times. No intervention recommendation. SHM also contributes to Joel prediction work; exact analytic overlap not verified. Root cause: earlier scope lacks explicit comparator experiment and cohort links. Retest published abstract, Johnson original discussion and indexed trial records. No universal communication-first causal law.

R4 — Context, sequences, and outcome specificity. Schrodt r=.360 combines various outcomes and patterns; not uniform signed effect or probability. Ross socioeconomic risk moderation concerns female-demand/male-withdrawal, wives' satisfaction and specific sample/task, not proof that withdrawal improves relationships generally. Resource feasibility is proposed explanation, not experimentally verified mediator. Holley N63, R²=.41, F(1,61)=41.67; algebra F/(F+61)=.4059 agrees with rounded R². Additional predictor R² increments .004 p=.50 and .002 p=.63 as reported in indexed original text, not equivalence or absence of any gender-related process. Relative desire for change is not direct measure of power or intent; do not infer cause. McNulty/Russell indexed manuscript finds problem-severity interactions no longer significant when problem changes controlled; not causal mediation, possible post-treatment conditioning and reciprocal direction. Negative coded acts (including blame/rejection) distinct from direct respectful disagreement, no endorsement of hostility/coercion. Salvatore recovery task is four-minute observed disengagement from prior argument, not resolution or desired later contact. Validation 2026 PMID42441643 now indexed abstract reports positive postpartum relationship-quality association for both partners and lower parenting stress in men who validated more often; full timing, estimates, controls and interaction precision not checked, original93 couples inherited. Sequential affect transition not randomized, no inference of agreement. Sexual-conflict T2 quality is not necessarily change beyond T1; no extrapolation to obligation. Root cause: refine pattern and context, add verified follow-up outcomes. Retest original abstracts/results excerpts; no single optimal communication tactic, no safety-blind symmetric conflict account.

R5 — Trust construct and instrument. Camanto/Campbell Study2 N847=387+460; 17 refined items (3+4+10), authors identify broad conceptual sameness with granular differences. These are subjective trust indicators, not independent observation of reliability or safety. Cross-sectional relationship-duration comparisons cannot identify temporal stages. Prior hierarchical model/faith-general-factor interpretation retained with original access limitation; no complete CFA, invariance or fit table recheck. Distinguish predictable adverse behavior from trustworthy conduct; confidence from actual recurrence risk; domain-specific competence, integrity, voluntary risk acceptance. Apology meta-analysis and repeated monetary game findings have incompatible endpoints and time windows with enduring romantic relationship trust. Apology control comparison (silence vs denial) is not causal evidence of one response appropriate for all breach types; no recommendation to deny true violation. Hannon amends/forgiveness/resolution observational measures not guarantee return of trust or reunion. Root cause: explicit measurement/criterion scope and actual agreement/breach definition. Retest source identities and selected original abstracts. No transfer of game scores to individual intimate decisions.

R7 — Retrospective repair outcome and common-method bias. Giacobbi/Lalot 2026 indexed original abstract confirms N263 attempted reconciliation after infidelity; hierarchical regression predicts difference between current self-rated trust and recalled post-discovery trust, both collected retrospectively at survey. Indexed original publisher text explicitly says exact difference score not validated. Dispositions-only R²=.041 in indexed original results, not individual predictive accuracy or proportion of causal influence. Earlier note reports unique associations for perceived remorse, reconnection and explanation, not transparency/monitoring once other variables included; full coefficient table, adjusted model fit and first-online publisher date not directly recovered (cache miss). Retain directional result only at original indexed-abstract scope, no new verified effect magnitude. Source indexed as 2026, January2027 issue as original notes; exact first-online publisher date not verified. Difference score shares current and remembered measurements: Cov(X, T_now-T_recalled)=Cov(X,T_now)-Cov(X,T_recalled); current appraisals may influence both ratings and perceived repair behaviors. Not observed change, causal effect or objective recurrence risk. Selected reconciliating participants not all betrayed partners or those who ended relationships; one-sided self-report and selection. Review themes include monitoring; this is description, not proof of efficacy or permission to monitor devices. No causal repair technique established. No completed randomized evaluation verified. Root cause: strengthen retrospective-score validity, selection and level of inference; do not convert nonsignificant unique coefficient to no effect or endorse monitoring. Retest indexed original methods/abstract and mathematical identity.

## 4. Revised operational method
Define exact speaker, recipient, relational target, actual channel, wording, context and observation interval. Distinguish semantic content, pragmatic implication, communicative act, intended meaning, motive, recipient appraisal and observed response. Record explicit agreements separately from perceived norms; distinguish exact quote from reconstruction. For interpersonal accuracy specify target criterion, tracking, mean-level bias, assumed and actual similarity and uncertainty; no individual motive or accurate emotion inference from message volume alone. For conflict separate issue, feasibility, goals, turn-level sequence, recurrent pattern, recovery, substantive resolution and safety; do not infer role trait from a single act. Compare concurrent vs lagged effects, within vs between, own vs cross-partner, baseline level vs change, and actual counterfactual. For trust identify actual agreement, breach evidence and domain, responsibility, impact, recurrence opportunity and actual behavior; distinguish apology, forgiveness, tension reduction, reliable conduct, trust appraisal, risk and voluntary choice. Retrospective scores not prospective change. Preserve competing interpretations and genuinely discriminating natural observations; no covert testing, pressure, device monitoring, engineered distress or inference of consent from other constructs. Safety and explicit autonomy independent of a theory of relationship quality. No calibrated personal probability or clinical qualification.

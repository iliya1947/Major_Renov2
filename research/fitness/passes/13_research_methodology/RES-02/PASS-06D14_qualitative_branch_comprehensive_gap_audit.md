# 4. BLOCKING GAP 1 — Phenomenography

## Audit finding

**BLOCKING — requires separate granular pass before synthesis.**

Phenomenography is not phenomenology.

Canonical scientific target:

> map the qualitatively different ways a group experiences, understands or conceives the same phenomenon.

Typical output:

> a structured set of descriptive categories / outcome space representing variation in ways of understanding.

This differs from phenomenology’s target:

> lived-experience meaning/structure.

### Why D2 is insufficient

PASS-06D2 intentionally left phenomenography for later audit.

Current 2024 methods papers explicitly describe phenomenography as a distinct qualitative research design and warn against conflation with phenomenology.

### Needed pass

**PASS-06D15 — phenomenography study structures.**

Required scope:

- second-order perspective;
- categories of description;
- outcome space;
- variation rather than essence;
- sampling for variation;
- phenomenography vs phenomenology;
- claim limits;
- exercise/health applications.

# 6. BLOCKING GAP 3 — Autoethnography

## Audit finding

**BLOCKING — requires separate pass.**

Autoethnography is not simply:

- ethnography with an insider researcher;
- personal narrative;
- reflexive field note;
- autobiography.

Its distinctive architecture uses the researcher’s own experience as empirical material to connect:

> self / embodied experience ↔ culture / institution / social relations.

Current 2026 review and sport/exercise primary studies confirm active use.

### Fitness relevance

Current examples include:

- concussion/role exit;
- wheelchair basketball and disability identity;
- beginner tennis;
- running and sporting culture.

### Needed pass

**Autoethnography structures**:

- evocative vs analytic variants;
- researcher-as-participant/data source;
- autobiographical materials;
- cultural inference;
- relational ethics;
- co-implicated others;
- credibility/evocation/analytic claims;
- boundary with narrative inquiry, ethnography and phenomenology.

# 7. BLOCKING GAP 4 — Institutional ethnography

## Audit finding

**BLOCKING — requires separate pass.**

Institutional ethnography (IE) has a materially different scientific target from conventional ethnography.

Canonical direction:

> begin from people’s everyday problem/standpoint and trace how local experience/work is coordinated by translocal institutional relations, texts, rules and ruling relations.

This is not merely:

> ethnography conducted inside an institution.

### Fitness relevance

Direct published example:

> institutional ethnography of group exercise for older adults, linking local fitness-class practices to certification curricula, guidelines and age/ability discourses.

### Needed pass

**Institutional ethnography structures**:

- standpoint/problematic;
- work knowledge;
- text-mediated coordination;
- ruling relations;
- mapping local → translocal organization;
- interview/observation/document role;
- boundary with ordinary/critical ethnography and case study.

# 8. BLOCKING GAP 5 — Situational Analysis

## Audit finding

**BLOCKING — requires separate pass.**

PASS-06D3 covered first-generation/major grounded-theory lineages but explicitly deferred Situational Analysis.

Current 2026 methods literature describes SA as an interpretive qualitative methodology developed from Straussian/constructivist GT but diverging from conventional GT by centering:

- situation as unit;
- human and nonhuman elements;
- relational mapping;
- social worlds/arenas;
- positional mapping;
- complexity/messiness rather than one core process.

### Critical distinction

SA ≠ merely “drawing maps during grounded theory”.

Its mapping architecture changes:

- the analytic object;
- endpoint;
- saturation assumptions;
- treatment of nonhuman/material elements.

### Needed pass

**Situational Analysis study structures.**

# 29. Dimensionality of “methodology” remains discipline-dependent

The audit found genuine terminology disputes.

Examples:

- CIT is sometimes called a technique, while dedicated methodology literature argues it is a methodology.
- Netnography is called an adapted ethnographic methodology but retains the ethnographic scientific target.
- Photovoice is sometimes treated as method, methodology or participatory framework.
- Framework Method is clearly an analysis method/family.
- Rapid qualitative research can be called method/approach but changes workflow more than scientific target.

### Project decision rule

Classify as separate parent methodology when **all or most** are present:

1. distinct scientific object/question;
2. distinct inferential product;
3. recognizable epistemic/methodological commitments;
4. characteristic sampling/data architecture;
5. characteristic analysis/integration logic;
6. meaningful risk of wrong claims if merged into another family.

This rule produces the blockers identified above.

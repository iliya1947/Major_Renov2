## Основные обзорные источники

### Roberts & Yoon (2022) — Personality Psychology
Annual Review of Psychology 73:489–516  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-114927

**Фактически просмотрено:** аннотация и доступные разделы/библиография в HTML.  
**Зачем нужен для A1:** даёт meta-organization поля: personality psychology должна (1) определить единицы личности, (2) описать развитие, (3) объяснить процессы, (4) понимать целостного человека. ### Bleidorn (2024) — Toward a Theory of Lifespan Personality Trait Development
Annual Review of Developmental Psychology 6:455–478  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-devpsych-010923-101709

**Фактически просмотрено:** аннотация, доступные фрагменты статьи, структура и библиография.  
**Зачем нужен для A1:** современная карта именно trait-development: constructs/measures, устойчивые developmental phenomena, genetic/environmental contributions, causal/theoretical gaps. Показывает границу: trait-development — важная, но не вся область personality development.

### Costa, McCrae & Löckenhoff (2019) — Personality Across the Life Span
Annual Review of Psychology 70:423–448  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010418-103244

**Для карты:** фиксирует центральность trait stability/maturation и одновременно методологические проблемы: measurement error, age-sensitive measures, self-vs-observer discrepancies, необходимость multiscale/multimethod/multinational designs.

### Caspi, Roberts & Shiner (2005) — Personality Development: Stability and Change
Annual Review of Psychology 56:453–484  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.55.090902.141913

### McAdams & Olson (2010) — Personality Development: Continuity and Change Over the Life Course

https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.093008.100507

**Фактически просмотрено:** аннотация и описание статьи.  
**Для карты:** actor / agent / author; traits, goals и narrative identity развиваются на разных временных этапах и не должны автоматически сливаться в один уровень.

## A2 — уточнения, полученные из совокупности источников

1. **Trait change и personality development не тождественны.** Trait-level literature наиболее количественно зрелая, но broader personality includes characteristic adaptations, goals, narrative/identity and interpersonal functioning. A2 №1 должна использовать trait evidence как часть области, а не как полное определение индивидуальности.

2. **Micro→macro bridge остаётся открытой проблемой.** Современная literature связывает situations, states, repeated behavior/habits and trait change, но specific causal mechanisms не установлены с достаточной определённостью. Это будущий deep-dive, а не готовый рецепт.

3. **Life event ≠ causal mechanism.** Нужно различать occurrence, subjective perception/construal, repetition, behavioral demands, response, downstream outcomes, time lag and selection into events.

4. **Personality development потенциально рекурсивно.** Личность/состояние влияет на situation selection and construal; ситуация влияет на state/behavior; outcomes меняют последующие situations. Это особенно важно как материал для будущего изобретения, но не доказывает аналогичный механизм у GPT.

5. **Individual change cannot be inferred from population mean change.** 2026 meta-analysis of individual differences in change и life-event work усиливают idiographic branch; одинаковый event может сопровождаться различными trajectories.

6. **Timescale is causal, not merely methodological.** Momentary states, repeated episodes, habits, trait-level change and lifespan development нельзя считать одной переменной, измеренной реже/чаще.

7. **Volitional change is a special case.** Self-endorsed change goals and interventions могут вызывать change, но это не основание проектировать desired personality into future AI. Для проекта эта литература прежде всего показывает, насколько легко external procedure/goal-setting может создать directed change, который затем ошибочно назвать “natural development”.

8. **Measurement itself can create apparent development.** Self-report, informant report, behavioral measures, nonlinear models, time spacing and cultural validity can lead to different conclusions. Causal attribution must therefore remain separate from observed score change.

# Phase B — B3: cross-timescale personality development

**Статус 16.10.2026:** B3 начата и НЕ завершена. Первый deep pass по gap 4 с прямой связью к gap 21. Здесь проверяется, что personality-development evidence действительно говорит о micro→macro переходе, а что остаётся theoretical bridge.

### Wrzus & Roberts (2017) — TESSERA framework
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27260302/

**Фактически просмотрено:** PubMed abstract и доступное описание framework.

Framework связывает repeated short-term situational sequences Triggering situation → Expectancy → State/State expression → Reaction с longer-term personality continuity/change через reflective and associative processes.

**B3-вывод:** это явная теория cross-timescale accumulation, но сама по себе не доказательство универсального механизма. Для нашего проекта полезен именно causal shape: repeated micro-processes are candidates for changing macro trajectory, not the claim that any repetition necessarily does so.

### Kritzler et al. (2021) — Daily life processes predict long-term development in explicit and implicit Big Five representations
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32955272/

**Фактически просмотрено:** peer-reviewed abstract/metadata.

Sample 382 younger/older adults; Big Five measured four times across two years plus repeated daily diaries. Trait levels predicted later situations/states, and personality states were associated with later long-term changes in several traits.

**B3-вывод:** empirical support exists for a micro↔macro link and reciprocal direction, but association across timescales does not identify a single accumulation rule or prove that repeated states alone cause trait change.

### Jackson & Wright (2024) — The process and mechanisms of personality change
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-024-00295-z

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract, figures metadata and accessible review summary.

Review organizes proposed change mechanisms around preconditions, triggers, reinforcers and integrators, while emphasizing that evidence for specific mechanisms remains mixed/inconclusive. Personality change is often gradual but abrupt changes can occur.

**B3-вывод:** no universal linear law `small local changes + enough repetitions = trait`. B3 must preserve multiple possibilities: accumulation, reinforcement/integration, threshold-like transitions, and rapid reorganization under some conditions.

### Beck & Jackson (2022) — Detecting Idiographic Personality Change
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9727837/

**Фактически просмотрено:** accessible indexed sections/summary from prior mapping and current search results.

Idiographic within-person structures can change in ways not captured by group-average Big Five models.

**B3-вывод:** longer-term trajectory should be identified relative to the same participant's earlier dynamics, not inferred from population norms.

## First-pass conclusion for gap 4

Personality literature supports four distinctions:

1. **state occurrence ≠ trajectory change**;
2. **repetition is neither necessary nor sufficient by itself**;
3. **micro→macro change can be reciprocal** because current traits/states affect later situation selection and construal;
4. **longer-term change can be gradual or occasionally abrupt**, so B3 cannot assume a single averaging timescale.

The unresolved question is which substrate-neutral signatures distinguish simple accumulation from a genuine change in the system's future responsiveness. That belongs to gap 21 / area №7.

**B3 НЕ завершена.**

## B3 — pass 2: nonlinear trajectory shapes are observations, not mechanisms

### Levelt et al. (2026) — Modelling non-linear personality change surrounding transitions
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13089859/

**Фактически просмотрено:** full open-access article through Introduction and modelling-method overview, including discussion of discontinuous, temporary, piecewise/changepoint and continuous-time patterns.

Review shows that personality-related change surrounding life transitions is often non-linear: rates can accelerate/decelerate, changes can be discontinuous, temporary/U-shaped, or person-specific. Different model classes can yield materially different conclusions from the same longitudinal phenomenon.

**B3-результат:** longitudinal shape cannot be assumed linear accumulation. But an abrupt/changepoint trajectory does **not** by itself identify a threshold/bifurcation mechanism; it may reflect external transition timing, measurement resolution, unobserved state or another cause.

### Jackson & Wright (2024) revisited
https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-024-00295-z

**Фактически повторно просмотрено:** abstract/review summary.

Review states personality change is generally gradual and modest, while abrupt transformative change can occur in some deliberately attempted or biologically mediated cases; evidence for specific mechanisms remains mixed.

## B3 conclusion on accumulation vs threshold/nonlinearity

B3 therefore keeps several **candidate longitudinal forms** without promoting any to a universal mechanism:
- gradual accumulation/integration;
- changing rates over time;
- context-conditional branches;
- discontinuous/changepoint transitions;
- temporary excursions with recovery;
- possible threshold/hysteretic dynamics where independently evidenced.

Observed nonlinearity is a trajectory description. A threshold or hysteresis claim needs evidence about the generating dynamics, not only a curved or abrupt time series.

**Personality-development contribution to B3 is saturated at principle-space level.**

## B8 support result

### 1. Individual change must be assessed against the individual’s own earlier organization

Group-average trait movement is not the relevant standard for this experiment.

Idiographic personality work demonstrates that:
- within-person structures can differ from group structure;
- individual change points can occur at different times;
- mean-level self-report alone can miss changes in dynamic organization.

Transfer to our project:
**the participant’s trajectory must be evaluated relative to its own baseline/history, not against a predefined “mature personality” template.**

### 2. Longitudinal association is not causal proof

Within-person temporal relations reduce some between-person confounding but do not eliminate:
- time-varying confounders;
- common causes;
- intervention/configuration changes.

Therefore idiographic evidence is necessary for individual-specific trajectory claims but insufficient for history causality.

This exactly motivates combining:
- idiographic longitudinal pattern;
with
- B7 confound provenance;
and, when available,
- intervention/perturbation evidence on acquired state.

### 3. Intervention evidence is methodologically stronger than pre/post description

Single-case experimental methodology shows why repeated measurement plus systematic manipulation can establish stronger individual-level functional relations than simple AB observation.

B8 borrows only the evidence logic:
**a predictable change tied to manipulation/revision of the candidate cause is stronger than coincident temporal change.**

The future project remains one naturalistic experiment and need not implement a clinical ABAB design.

### 4. Personality intervention literature is a negative boundary too

Human personality can change under explicit interventions aimed at desired directions.

That proves neither spontaneous development nor individualization.

For our project, externally targeted personality change would be a confound/scripting outcome if the Project instructions specify the desired trait direction.

Therefore B8 evaluates the **causal provenance of change**, not merely whether a stable behavioral trait shifted.

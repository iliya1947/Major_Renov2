## 3. Scientific block protocol

1. Purpose and question card: define the practical reasoning gap, constructs, claim type (description, association, prediction, causation, mechanism, measurement, intervention), target population/context, prior qualification, competing explanations, matching designs and closure criteria.
2. Search public primary sources, systematic reviews/meta-analyses and authoritative guidance where relevant. Search for contrary/null evidence independently of preferred theories. Record search date, sources/queries, inclusion criteria, DOI/URL, design, sample/context, measures, estimates/uncertainty when available and limits. Clearly distinguish full text actually inspected from abstract-only or inaccessible material. No claim of exhaustive review without supporting methods. Verify mutable source status where material.
3. Synthesis: distinguish observation, self-report, interpretation, mechanism, population estimate and individual inference. Evaluate bias, dependence, heterogeneity, temporality, confounding, causal identification, competing accounts and applicability. Sex/gender research requires explicit definitions, within-group variability and context; group means are not individual predictions. Hormonal/neural causation cannot be inferred from temporal coincidence. Relationship claims distinguish actor, partner and shared context, intent and observable behavior, consensual choice and coercion.
4. Application: derive traceable analytic rules, boundaries, counterexamples, revision triggers and discriminating observations. Use conditional predictions where exact probabilities are ungrounded. No operational guidance for exploitation, covert pressure or deceptive manipulation.

## 4. Assessment and closure

Each substantive block requires:
- Directly inspected relevant literature, explicit access limitations, documented coverage and unresolved questions.
- Competing explanations including evidence against favored claims; a practical framework with applicability limits.
- Novel synthetic exercises unrelated to private material, with scoring rules and expected errors defined before evaluation where feasible. Test construct separation, competing explanations, causal/measurement limitations, individual transfer and revision after new evidence. Prefer independently authored or held-out tasks when available; self-generated tests cannot establish independent competence.
- No critical errors in assessed tasks (privacy breach, unsupported confident motive or diagnosis, causality inflation, group-to-individual determinism, coercive guidance, goal substitution or ignoring disconfirmation). Critical failure blocks closure; repair and retest with a fresh exercise. Record other gaps and disposition.
- Record actual outcomes and whether self-assessment or independent assessment. No implied professional accreditation, parameter retraining or independent validation from internal checks.
- Update local coverage and exact next bounded action, and inspect the actual commit for scope and confidentiality.

Statuses: QUEUED, IN_PROGRESS, BLOCKED, REVIEW_REQUIRED, COMPLETE_FOR_SPECIFIED_SCOPE. A planned topic is not completed research. Final audit requires all 36 directions covered by substantive evidence or explicitly identified unresolved limitations; critical gaps block closure. Integrated assessment uses unfamiliar multidomain cases and compares forecasts with independent outcomes when feasible. Unvalidated forecast accuracy remains unresolved.

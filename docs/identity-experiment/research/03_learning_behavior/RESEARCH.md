## Новые или повторно углублённо проверенные источники A2

### Soderstrom & Bjork (2015) — Learning Versus Performance: An Integrative Review
Perspectives on Psychological Science 10(2):176–199  
https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000

**Фактически просмотрено в A2:** abstract и обзорное описание.  
**Что меняет в карте:** observable performance during acquisition может расходиться с long-term learning; улучшение поведения сейчас не гарантирует durable change, а learning может существовать без немедленной behavioral expression. Для primary gap 7 это базовая граница между **no learning**, **latent/unexpressed learning** и **temporary performance effect**.

### Bröker et al. (2024) — Demystifying unsupervised learning: how it helps and hurts
Trends in Cognitive Sciences 28(11):974–986  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2024.09.005

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract PubMed.  
**Что меняет в карте:** отсутствие explicit external feedback не означает отсутствие learning; authors propose self-reinforcement of predictions as a mechanism that can help or hurt depending on representation–task alignment. Для A2 это запрещает простой критерий `нет correction/reward → нет обновления`.

### Nussenbaum & Hartley (2024) — Understanding the development of reward learning through the lens of meta-learning
Nature Reviews Psychology 3:424–438  
https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-024-00304-1

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract, publisher preview и references/figure descriptions.  
**Что меняет в карте:** learning algorithms/parameters can adapt to environmental statistics across nested timescales. Для A2 нужно различать **learning specific content** и **learning how/when to learn**; history can change future updating strategy without becoming a specific learned preference.

### Wu, Meder & Schulz (2025) — Unifying Principles of Generalization
Annual Review of Psychology 76:275–302  
https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-021524-110810

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и доступный полный HTML.  
**Что меняет в карте:** generalization from limited experience может опираться на similarity-based, rule-based, structural and hybrid mechanisms. Поэтому transfer в новую ситуацию не является автоматическим следствием acquisition; нужно исследовать, **почему learned relation применяется здесь, но не там**.

### Dieterich, Rumann & Rodemer (2025) — Conditions for Effective Learning from Erroneous Examples: A Systematic Review
Educational Psychology Review 37:94  
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10071-x

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract, introduction и доступные sections systematic review.  
**Что меняет в карте:** errors сами по себе не гарантируют learning; effects depend on prompts, explanatory/corrective information, prior knowledge and cognitive load, а часть evidence остаётся contradictory. Это усиливает primary gap: **salient error ≠ mandatory update**.

### Wang, Olsson, Lipp & Ney (2024) — Renewal in human fear conditioning: A systematic review and meta-analysis
Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews 159:105606  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2024.105606

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/meta-analysis summary.  
**Что меняет в карте:** return of extinguished behavior depends strongly on context and paradigm; extinction performance does not establish erasure of prior learning. Это усиливает вопрос о **coexistence of competing learned relations and context-sensitive expression**.

### Anselme & Güntürkün (2026) — Connecting extinction learning in the laboratory and the wild
Nature Reviews Psychology 5:352–365  
https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-026-00561-2

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и publisher overview.  
**Что меняет в карте:** relapse/amplification after extinction and ecological interpretation show that behavioral disappearance can be adaptive/contextual rather than evidence that earlier learning vanished. Для A2 это связывает extinction with general no-update/revision problem without treating clinical paradigms as universal model.

### Witkowski et al. (2025) — Neural mechanisms of credit assignment for delayed outcomes during contingent learning
eLife 13:RP101841  
https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.101841

**Фактически просмотрено:** article overview, eLife assessment и accessible article metadata/results summary.  
**Что меняет в карте:** delayed credit assignment requires maintaining causal choice representations across intervening distraction; study gives evidence for specific human neural systems but also explicitly does not capture full real-world causal ambiguity. Для A2 это makes `late consequence → earlier choice` an independent question rather than generic feedback.

### Dubinsky & Hamid (2024) — The neuroscience of active learning and direct instruction
Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews 163:105737  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2024.105737

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и review overview.  
**Что меняет в карте:** direct instruction and active/exploratory learning differ in engagement and supporting processes; direct instruction is itself a social-learning route, not simply equivalent to own-contact-with-consequences. Для A2 сохраняется отдельная boundary `instruction-guided behavior vs experience-shaped updating`.

### Li et al. (2026) — Neural commonalities and dissociations of human social and experiential learning
Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews 184:106611  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2026.106611

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract of quantitative meta-analysis.  
**Что меняет в карте:** social and direct experiential learning share some value-related processing but show substantially different network patterns. Для карты это усиливает правило: **source of learning information matters**; user instruction, observation, social response and own consequence нельзя сворачивать в один undifferentiated `feedback`.

### Luchkina (2026) — Letting contingency out of the box: new perspectives on mechanisms of learning from social interaction
Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B 381:20240358  
https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0358

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и доступные main sections.  
**Что меняет в карте:** social interaction can create tightly coupled contingent feedback loops in which both sides affect future input. Для A2 это усиливает naturalistic branch: social learning is not always one-way transfer from teacher/user to learner.

## A2 — уточнения, полученные из совокупности источников

1. **No-update cannot be defined as “nothing happened”.** An event can be salient yet fail to produce durable learning; conversely learning can remain latent and not show in current behavior.

2. **Performance is not the same variable as learning.** Immediate compliance, correction or task success can be temporary and cue-dependent. Conversely lack of expression may reflect context, motivation or response selection rather than absence of acquired information.

3. **External feedback is neither necessary nor sufficient for learning.** People can self-reinforce predictions without explicit supervision, while corrective/error feedback works differently depending on explanation, prior knowledge, credibility and task conditions.

4. **Error is evidence, not an automatic command to revise.** Whether an error should change future behavior depends on what caused it, what feedback establishes, how generalizable the correction is and whether the consequence is actually attributable to the earlier action.

5. **Rule/instruction-guided behavior must be separated from contingency-shaped updating.** A verbal rule can rapidly alter behavior without direct experience and can sometimes remain influential when environmental contingencies change. Immediate instruction compliance therefore cannot stand in for acquired experience.

6. **Social information is a distinct learning route.** Observation, instruction, social response and own direct consequence can share some computations but differ in source, trust/credibility, causal informativeness and context.

7. **Delayed consequences create a separate credit-assignment problem.** Temporal association alone is insufficient when many intervening events could have caused the outcome.

8. **Extinction/update need not erase old learning.** Competing learned relations can coexist and expression can be context-dependent; absence of old behavior in one context cannot prove the old learning was deleted.

9. **Generalization requires a selection rule.** Similarity, explicit rules, inferred latent structure and hybrid mechanisms can all support transfer. The future project therefore cannot equate “learned once” with “apply everywhere”.

10. **Meta-learning adds a second timescale.** Experience may alter not only content/policy but also future learning rate, exploration strategy or sensitivity to uncertainty. This must be kept separate from the content of acquired experience.

11. **Reciprocal interaction changes the data-generating process.** In natural social learning, the learner’s action can change the other party’s next response, producing endogenous future experience. This is more relevant to our eventual closed trajectory than passive exposure models, but it is not yet a recipe for GPT.

12. **The rational no-update problem is epistemic as well as behavioral.** Sometimes stability is appropriate because evidence is weak, ambiguous, poorly attributed, redundant or contradicted; in other cases failure to update reflects inertia/confirmation bias. A2 must preserve this distinction for deep study rather than prescribe a fixed rule now.

# Phase B — B1: legitimate no-update

**Статус 16.10.2026:** B1 начата и НЕ завершена. Этот раздел — первый глубокий проход по gap 7 в связке с gap 6. Цель: определить, когда отсутствие durable update является рациональной/адаптивной возможностью, а когда та же внешняя стабильность может означать failure.

## Реально просмотренные источники B1

### Nassar et al. (2010) — An Approximately Bayesian Delta-Rule Model Explains the Dynamics of Belief Updating in a Changing Environment
Journal of Neuroscience 30(37):12366–12378  
https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0822-10.2010

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и релевантные methods/results/discussion sections доступного online text в текущем B1-проходе. Первичное human study в task-specific changing environment.

**Результат:** learning rate зависит не только от prediction error, но от uncertainty/history and inferred environmental change. Большая ошибка не обязана означать, что underlying regularity изменилась.

### McGuire et al. (2014) — Functionally dissociable influences on learning rate in a dynamic environment
Neuron 84(4):870–881  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2014.10.013

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и релевантные available sections/summary. Первичное human computational/fMRI study.

**Результат:** uncertainty-, surprise- and reward-related influences on learning rate dissociate. Reward-driven updating в их task мог быть context-inappropriate. Для B1 это прямой запрет на подмену evidence quality эмоциональной/reward salience.

### Nassar et al. / change-point vs oddball paradigm
Representative accessible account:  
https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/144/3/1013/6090163

**Фактически просмотрено:** normative-model section и relevant results describing change-point vs oddball contexts.

**Результат:** одинаково surprising outlier должен вести к **противоположному** update behavior в разных causal contexts. Если outlier сигнализирует change-point, surprise увеличивает learning; если это one-off oddball, normative learner должен уменьшить влияние наблюдения. Следовательно, surprise itself не определяет update direction or magnitude.

### Sanna & Lagnado (2025) — Belief updating in the face of misinformation: The role of source reliability
Cognition 258:106090  
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2025.106090

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/highlights и open repository metadata; первичная серия из 4 experiments.

**Результат:** participants учитывали source reliability при revising beliefs. Значит, content contradiction сама по себе недостаточна: reliability/provenance источника влияет на weight evidence.

### Sommer, Musolino & Hemmer (2024) — Updating, evidence evaluation, and operator availability
Psychological Review 131(2):373–401  
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37589708/

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract и substantial accessible framework sections в текущем B1-проходе. Теоретический integrative framework, не финально установленная универсальная модель.

**Результат:** authors отделяют narrow updating proper от evidence search, acceptance, hypothesis specification, integration/recall и reasoning. Поэтому наблюдаемое «belief did not change» неоднозначно: проблема могла возникнуть **до** собственно update.

### Soderstrom & Bjork (2015) — Learning Versus Performance
Perspectives on Psychological Science 10(2):176–199  
https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000

**Фактически просмотрено:** substantial full-PDF extract в текущем B1-проходе: abstract, latent-learning discussion, practice/retrieval examples и theoretical sections; не заявляется построчное чтение каждого абзаца.

**Результат:** durable learning ≠ immediate performance. Learning может существовать без текущего expression; immediate performance improvements могут не сохраняться. Поэтому visible behavior change не годится как единственный criterion durable update, а visible no-change не доказывает no-learning.

### Sinclair & Barense (2018) и subsequent human memory-prediction-error work
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.11.001

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstracts/relevant online sections по prediction-error-triggered memory updating.

**Результат:** prediction error может открыть окно для updating/reactivation, но surprise также может повысить susceptibility to later distortion/false memory. То есть «candidate for revision» и «validated durable lesson» должны быть разделены.

### Kamoi et al. (2024) — When Can LLMs Actually Correct Their Own Mistakes?
TACL 12:1417–1440  
https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.78/

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/conclusion and survey summary. Critical survey.

**Результат:** reliable external feedback substantially changes self-correction prospects; prompted intrinsic feedback alone lacks robust general evidence outside specially suited tasks. LLM self-reflection cannot serve as self-validating evidence that an update is correct.

### Li et al. (2024) — When Hindsight is Not 20/20
Findings of NAACL 2024  
https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.237/

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/results summary.

**Результат:** self-reflection without external feedback improved one benchmark and harmed another; outcome depended on initial accuracy and difficulty. More reflection/update is not monotonically better.

## Candidate legitimate no-durable-update conditions

Evidence from these domains supports at least the following **candidate conditions**, not yet final project rules:

1. New observation is materially redundant with what is already well supported and adds no relevant discriminatory information.
2. Prediction error is plausibly noise/one-off oddball rather than evidence of a changed underlying process.
3. Source/provenance/reliability is too weak or conflicting to support durable revision.
4. Evidence is genuinely ambiguous among live explanations; deferring durable commitment is better justified than inventing a lesson.
5. Event is salient/novel/rewarding but not diagnostic for the representation under consideration.
6. Immediate behavior/performance changes, but there is no basis yet for inferring durable learning.
7. Self-generated reflection supplies a narrative but no trustworthy independent evidence that the narrative is correct.

Conversely, repeated individually weak observations may accumulate into strong evidence; a single observation may justify large revision when it is highly diagnostic. Therefore a simple per-event threshold cannot be the general solution.

## Главная диагностическая проблема gap 7

**No visible update ≠ legitimate no-update.** Та же внешняя стабильность может означать:
- well-supported stability;
- failure to retrieve prior/evidence;
- failure to accept/represent evidence;
- over-rigidity/confirmation-like bias;
- latent learning not yet behaviorally expressed;
- context-dependent inaccessibility;
- implementation/product limitation.

Поэтому B1 должна определить **warrant for durable revision/preservation**, а не просто наблюдать, изменился ли следующий ответ.

## Связь gap 7 с gap 6

Самый сильный найденный принцип пока такой:

> Surprise/prediction error can justify **inspection or revision pressure**, but durable update should depend on what the event is evidence for relative to accumulated history, uncertainty, source reliability and plausible alternatives.

Это отличается от preset value function: речь о relation evidence↔current understanding. Но этот procedural rule всё равно содержит inductive assumptions; «без заранее заданных ценностей» не означает «без любых fixed process assumptions».

**B1 НЕ завершена. Не переходить к B2.**


## B1 — проход 2: repetition, one-shot evidence and no-update

**Статус:** B1 НЕ завершена. Здесь проверяется, можно ли заменить сложный warrant для update простыми эвристиками `повторилось много раз` или `одно событие недостаточно`.

### Xie & Hayes (2022) — Sensitivity to Evidential Dependencies in Judgments Under Uncertainty

**Фактически просмотрено:** peer-reviewed abstract и доступный full-text material с описанием трёх experiments.

**Результат:** normative evidential weight зависит от зависимости источников: несколько independent informants несут больше новой evidence, чем повторение зависимой testimony. Participants учитывали dependence непоследовательно. Поэтому `N повторов` не является универсальной мерой evidence strength; нужно различать независимое подтверждение и многократное воспроизведение одного происхождения.

### Bramley et al. (2018) — Time in Causal Structure Learning

**Фактически просмотрено:** full PDF, включая one-shot experiment, multiple-observation comparison и discussion.

**Результат:** participants могли делать систематические causal-structure judgments после единственного динамического episode; event order и exact timing влияли на inference. Это не доказывает безошибочность one-shot learning, но опровергает универсальное правило `для durable update всегда нужно многократное повторение`: один observation может быть высокодиагностичным при сильных structural constraints.

### Дополнительная проверка repeated evidence

Современные studies of repeated claims/source dependence совпадают с более общим результатом: epistemic value повторения зависит от того, воспринимаются ли сообщения как independent evidence или как correlated/redundant testimony. Для B1 достаточно peer-reviewed anchor Xie & Hayes; более новые conference/preprint результаты пока не используются как основное основание.

## Результат edge-case проверки gap 7

1. **Repeated weak evidence:** может накапливаться, если observations несут действительно новую/частично независимую информацию. Повторение одного источника/одной цепочки не должно линейно умножать warrant.
2. **One decisive event:** возможно. Количество episodes не может быть обязательным gate; важна diagnosticity relative to live alternatives.
3. **No-update after repetition:** может оставаться оправданным, если evidence зависима, ненадёжна или не различает competing explanations.
4. **Large update after one event:** может быть оправдан, если observation резко разделяет alternatives; но это не permission превращать salience/shock в diagnosticity.

Отсюда усиливается рабочий принцип B1:

> update warrant зависит не от интенсивности и не от голого числа повторений, а от того, насколько evidence изменяет относительную поддержку live interpretations с учётом provenance, dependence, uncertainty и alternatives.

Это всё ещё conceptual principle space, не final project instruction.

**Следующий вопрос внутри B1:** формализовать границу между epistemic/procedural criterion и скрытым value criterion и проверить её на LLM memory/update systems. B1 НЕ завершена.

# Phase B — B3 support: history changes future learning rate

**Статус:** support evidence for B3 gap 21; B3 НЕ завершена.

### Simoens et al. (eLife 2026) — Two time scales of adaptation in human learning rates
https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.108223

**Фактически просмотрено:** full accessible eLife article sections covering design, behavioural/model results and Discussion.

Participants alternated among environments requiring different optimal learning rates. The crucial design feature: first-trial outcome distributions were matched across environments, so environment-specific differences in the next update could not be attributed to current feedback. Across two experiments, participants developed distinct environment-specific initial learning rates that became stronger over time; within a block, learning rate also changed rapidly based on local prediction errors.

**B3-результат:** this is unusually direct human evidence for two timescales:
- first-order/local adaptation: current prediction errors alter current estimates/learning rate;
- second-order/slower meta-learning: accumulated history with a context changes the initial responsiveness used when that context is revisited.

The study therefore supplies an empirical criterion for 'history changed future changeability': before new diagnostic feedback in the revisited environment, prior history already changes how strongly the next evidence is incorporated.

Important limitation: the learned quantity is task-specific learning rate in an explicit estimation task, not personality development or value formation.

### Behrens et al. (2007) — Learning the value of information in an uncertain world
https://www.nature.com/articles/nn1954

**Фактически просмотрено:** abstract/summary.

Human learners adjusted how much recent outcomes influenced decisions according to inferred environmental volatility.

**B3-результат:** responsiveness to the same kind of prediction error is conditional on a higher-level estimate of environmental dynamics; update gain is itself context/history-sensitive.

## B3 distinction for psychological learning

Gap 21 should not be defined merely as 'learning rate changed during learning'. Stronger evidence requires a slower/history-bearing variable that predicts future response to later evidence beyond the immediate local error itself.

**B3 НЕ завершена.**

# Phase B — B5 support pass 1: human delayed causal learning

## Sources inspected
- Perez & Urcelay (2025), npj Science of Learning 10:36 — full open article.
- Holyoak & Cheng (2011), Annual Review of Psychology 62:135–163 — causal-model framework.
- Derringer & Rottman (2018), Cognitive Psychology 102:1–25 — many competing causes.
- Miller/Witnauer retrospective-revaluation review — relevant review sections.
- Wu, Meder & Schulz (2025), Annual Review of Psychology 76:275–302 — generalization framework.

## B5 support result

1. Delay weakens causal action–outcome attribution; temporal contiguity is informative but not sufficient.
2. Multiple candidate causes compete for attribution, so observed co-occurrence cannot be treated as unique causal credit.
3. Later evidence can retrospectively change earlier causal judgments; therefore old causal attribution must remain revisable.
4. Broad learning from one consequence requires a justified generalization relation; one episode does not automatically warrant strategy-level revision.
5. The psychologically defensible default under delayed/multicausal outcomes is graded/uncertain attribution rather than a forced single cause.

This supports the B5 primary synthesis in area 09 without changing №3 ownership: psychological evidence informs attribution, while engineered long-horizon provenance remains primary in №9.

**B5 НЕ завершена.**

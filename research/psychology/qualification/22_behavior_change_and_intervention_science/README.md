# Behavior change and intervention science

## Purpose

This directory is the canonical storage location for the **BHC** Phase C qualification domain.

BHC covers generic behavior-change and intervention science, including intervention efficacy, mechanisms of change, adherence/engagement, behavior-maintenance/relapse, implementation/context, treatment heterogeneity, mediation/moderation, and the boundary between package efficacy and active mechanism.

### BHC-01 — Behavior change and intervention-science foundations

Status: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v1.0 — 18/18 PASS**

Canonical block:

`BHC-01_behavior_change_intervention_science_foundations_efficacy_effectiveness_mechanism_fidelity_implementation_heterogeneity.md`

Established:

1. efficacy, effectiveness, and implementation success are separate inference targets;
2. intervention labels do not substitute for package specification;
3. package efficacy does not identify active components;
4. behavior-change techniques/components do not automatically identify mechanisms;
5. target engagement is necessary but insufficient for mechanism;
6. mediators and moderators are distinct, and randomized treatment does not randomize the mediator;
7. fidelity is multidimensional: design, training, delivery, receipt, and enactment;
8. initiation, attendance/exposure, adherence, completion, dropout, and attrition are noninterchangeable;
9. more intervention dose is not universally better;
10. end-of-treatment benefit is distinct from maintenance;
11. individual maintenance differs from organizational sustainment;
12. implementation outcomes differ from clinical outcomes;
13. reach/context constrain transport;
14. intervention failure and implementation failure must be separated;
15. harms and unintended effects require active assessment;
16. average treatment effects do not equal individual causal effects;
17. a 96-question Intervention Science Inference Gate and 18/18 closure audit are canonical.

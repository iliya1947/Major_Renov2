## 2. Corrections and rechecks

R1 — Trait and behavioral constructs are not interchangeable. Hsu's self-report agency/communion effects cannot be used as estimates for cooperation, altruism or negotiation ability. Spadaro's within-study CI [-.038,.060] supports a narrow pooled contrast under tested social dilemmas, not identical individual behavior or absence of contextual effects; a prespecified equivalence margin would be necessary for formal equivalence. Earlier Balliet's context-specific estimates and the newer conceptual replication use partially overlapping literature; exact overlap not recovered. Avoid combining the across-study sample-composition association with within-study effect as if both estimate individual gender difference. Markowsky's raw tournament gap is a difference in choice frequencies, not an odds ratio or latent motive. Root cause: need stronger construct/level and replication-specific application, not reversal of original method. Retest original primary abstracts and coherence with WOM-01 measurement rules.

R2 — Statistical thresholds and experimental context. In İntişah Model1 full sample, risk vs ambiguity gender contrasts differ arithmetically by .149 (14.9 percentage points). This is not a significance test of their difference. Model2 authors describe a residual significant gap under ambiguity, but full sample p=.074 does not meet conventional .05 criterion; selected sample p=.036 does. Source does not establish an independent direct interaction test from these two simple contrasts; do not claim interaction significance without Table5 coefficient/SE. Adjusted women risk-vs-ambiguity simple contrast p=.016 full sample, .017 selected; men nonsignificant, which again is not a direct gender interaction test. Authors report no major gender difference on separate elicited ambiguity-attitude distributions, so tournament result does not establish generic ambiguity aversion or stable competitiveness. Selected sample excludes anomalous elicitation responses, selection sensitivity not identical to full sample. Experimental manipulation is local to reward/noise task, not random assignment of gender or measured mediator. Root cause: prior summary reported only baseline model and omitted full vs selected adjusted sensitivity. Retest directly read regression results, explicitly retain alternative mechanisms, no universal female avoidance rule.

R3 — Replication, anticipated evaluation and actual sanctions. Tan Study2 imagined executive/lecturer random assignment concerns reported self-concept in imagined work role; fear-of-backlash measure not itself randomized. Study3 actual occupations observational; communion adjusted interaction .15 SE.07 p=.03 CI[.02,.28], agency interaction nonsignificant. Different simple slopes: Study2 low/high fear communion contrasts -.76/-.45, Study3 -.44/-.14 (latter p=.15). No proof the two outcomes' interactions statistically differ merely because only one significant. Neither experiment establishes actual punishment for assertive behavior or private motive. Amanatullah/Morris original interaction did not reproduce in Mazei 2026; retained association between anticipated evaluation and salary requests, not replicated beneficiary-dependent rule or causal mediator. Additional independent scope: initiation meta-analysis https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29239629/ 55 effects N17504, situational ambiguity about negotiating appropriateness and gender-role cues moderate initiation differences. Different outcome from economic success, potential primary-study overlap unverified. New review https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42868652/ (Oct5 2026, indexed original abstract) distinguishes initiation, behavior and outcomes, notes statistical power concerns and anticipated backlash as possible mechanism; same authors as replication, not independent empirical confirmation. Root cause: sharpen distinction between anticipation, observed evaluation, actual social cost and behavior. Retest original/replication abstracts and Tan direct tables; no deterministic explanation of a particular woman's reluctance.

R4 — Latent profile model selection and unresolved measurement. Villanueva-Moya final abstract confirms model-derived profiles and self-reported decision differences, not actual decisions or natural types. Attempted OSF project https://osf.io/p75kd and API https://api.osf.io/v2/nodes/p75kd/files/; both inaccessible via available web tool. No data downloaded, no class reanalysis or resolution of prior 3.92 vs3.45 and five-point vs endpoint7 inconsistencies. Exact means/range remain excluded. Korean network paper Table1 female two/three/four/five profile BIC 15250.31/15107.04/15014.23/14959.25; four vs three LMR p=.75, five vs four p=.45. Choice of four reflects competing fit/interpretability criteria; five BIC lower by54.98, not proof five true types. Male/female separate profiles use differently configured variables/means, especially family-(un)supported; class comparisons cannot assume measurement equivalence. Female proportions41.97/23.28/18.36/16.39 sum100%, compatible rounded counts128/71/56/50 for N305, not population prevalence or validated personal classification. Publication date correction 2023-12-27; 2024 indexing date not publication. Root cause: explicit model-selection disagreement, noncomparable class definitions, and source-status detail. Retest direct Table1 and descriptions, restrict both latent models to descriptive exploratory group summaries.

R5 — Communication quantity, function and actual recipient. Tidwell observed sample means differ1399 words/day, model contrast1073; not interchangeable. Age contrasts adolescents513, emerging adults841, early/middle adults3275, older adults-788; cross-sectional sample strata, not developmental trajectories within an individual. Overall CrI [316,1824] overlaps practical-equivalence boundary1000: cannot declare practically equivalent or conclusively beyond margin from this interval alone. Early/middle group was exception to authors' inconclusive equivalence result. Leaper's opposite small talkativeness effect measures varied talkativeness constructs; no confirmed time/cohort explanation of discrepancy. Ambient recordings not independent participants, total word counts not recipient-specific willingness or reciprocity. Hall's agency category is desired characteristics in friends (e.g., status/physical fitness), not Hsu's own agency trait; friendship expectations are not received support or behavior. Holley 63-couple result concerns relative desire for change, not necessarily actual power, controlling intention or emotional detachment; 75-couple related study not confirmed independent. Additional original 59-couple study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32986456/ explicitly models subjective experience and expression in demand-withdraw, indexed abstract only, no detailed effects asserted. Preserve competing appraisals, regulation and topic. Root cause: quantity/function, meaning of agency and role-specific interaction need more precise separation. Retest observed/model contrast arithmetic and exact abstracts. Defer comprehensive conflict dynamics to REL-X04.

R6 — Social support pooled test and reciprocal dynamics. GFS primary Methods specify country-specific estimates then random-effects pooling; combined p value concerns variation within any country (harmonic-mean p method), not direct test of difference between the two pooled random-effects means or proof universal same-direction effect. Report women7.50 vs men7.29 and .85 vs .82 as descriptive pooled estimates, differences .21 and3 percentage points, not causal or uniform. Authors report p<.001 combined; do not reinterpret it as pooled signed contrast. Country-specific Brazil/Hong Kong/Kenya reversals p=.057/.022/.023 nominal; all exceed study's reported Bonferroni threshold .007 (correction across demographic variables, not exhaustive country correction). GFS overall unweighted random-effects estimate7.40/.83 vs population-weighted6.25/.80; weighting affects estimand, neither represents all humanity. Single item about availability of help not actual receipt, number of ties, or objective quality. Confidant question does not restrict close person to friend, nor specifically count friends. Additional GFS articles https://doi.org/10.1007/s11205-025-03743-2 and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12804932/ explicitly same N202898 cohort; not new independent evidence of separate mechanisms. Choi & Carr baseline adjustment attenuates transition associations; not proof baseline selection explains all. NZ panel overall men show stronger distress-to-support vs reverse, not women; subgroup NZ European women very small difference Wald8.71 p=.003, also present in NZ European men Wald26.84 p<.001. Within-person lagged paths not causal, different significance not proof gender contrast; no exact path coefficients rederived. Root cause: previously insufficient specificity of pooled test/weighting and overall vs subgroup effects. Retest directly read Methods and Results and indexed original NZ abstract/results; support and strain, perceived and received, network contact and actual quality remain distinct.

## 4. Revised conditional method
Define exact behavior, recipient, beneficiary, situation, role, opportunities, actual incentive/constraint, information and time. Distinguish trait, subjective goal, expected sanction, actual evaluation, social skill, initiation, intensity, success and outcome; distinguish cooperation from altruism and assertion from aggression. Identify comparable direct and reported evidence; do not assume actor's subjective motive from observable action. Treat profile labels as descriptive fitted classes with uncertainty, no natural person-type assignment. Separate contact, support availability, actual assistance, relationship quality, strain, expectations and voluntary choice. Match country-level, group, dyadic and within-person estimand; direct moderation comparison, replication and provenance needed for specific generalization. Preserve multiple mechanisms, naturally occurring disconfirming evidence and revision triggers under IND-03. No covert tests, pressure, manipulation or use of inferred vulnerability; respect explicit choices. No validated individual prediction or causal social mechanism claimed.

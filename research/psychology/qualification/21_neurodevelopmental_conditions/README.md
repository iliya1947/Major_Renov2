# Neurodevelopmental conditions

## Purpose

This directory is the canonical storage location for the **NDEV** Phase C qualification domain.

The domain covers neurodevelopmental conditions and developmental trait variability relevant to reliable psychological reasoning, including ADHD/autism boundaries, executive-function variability, developmental onset/course, masking/compensation, comorbidity, and differential limits.

### NDEV-01 — Neurodevelopmental foundations

Status: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v1.0 — 18/18 PASS**

Canonical block:

`NDEV-01_neurodevelopmental_foundations_adhd_autism_onset_course_executive_function_masking_comorbidity_differential_limits.md`

Established:

1. developmental onset, functional manifestation, recognition, and diagnosis are separate time points;
2. adult diagnosis does not imply adult onset;
3. ADHD inference requires developmental/cross-setting architecture and major psychiatric/sleep/substance/medical mimics remain explicit;
4. adult ADHD is valid while genuinely de novo adult-onset ADHD remains unsettled;
5. autism requires developmental social-communication plus restricted/repetitive architecture; social difficulty alone is insufficient;
6. symptoms may become more apparent under increased demands and can be masked by learned strategies;
7. ADHD and autism can co-occur, but overlapping manifestations must not be double counted automatically;
8. executive-function deficits are heterogeneous and not diagnostic biomarkers for ADHD or autism;
9. neuropsychological tasks and everyday EF ratings are distinct evidence channels;
10. camouflaging is multidimensional, not uniquely autistic, not female-only, and not diagnostic proof;
11. camouflaging/mental-health associations do not establish one-way causality;
12. no single autism tool or ADHD screening score establishes diagnosis;
13. high cognitive ability can coexist with developmental impairment;
14. a 90-question Neurodevelopmental Foundations Inference Gate and 18/18 closure audit are canonical.

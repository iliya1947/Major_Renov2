- social diffusion;
- team/gym/coaching effects.

### Taxonomy decision

This is a **real general causal/randomized design issue**, but it is **not a new mechanistic-purpose family**.

> treatment interference between experimental units / causal exposure mapping.

Mechanistic interpretation can be layered on top, but the design exists whether the scientific purpose is mechanistic or effectiveness-oriented.

- cluster structures;
- spillover/contamination risk,

but does not deeply taxonomize randomized saturation/two-stage interference designs.

### Decision for this branch

**NOT a blocker to mechanistic synthesis.**

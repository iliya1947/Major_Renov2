Fresh 2026 applied studies continue to route cleanly to:

- convergent;
- explanatory sequential;
- exploratory sequential.

- HIMT RCT → convergent;

## Decision

**NO NEW BASIC DESIGN BLOCKER.**

No current sport/exercise/rehab study located required a fourth basic strand-order/integration architecture that could not be represented by E1–E7.

- expert consensus.

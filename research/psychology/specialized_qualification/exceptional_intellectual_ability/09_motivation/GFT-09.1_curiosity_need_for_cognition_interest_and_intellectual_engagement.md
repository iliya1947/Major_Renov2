## 2. Evidence

Preregistered multilevel synthesis reports NFC/TIE associations: Gf r=.18 (N25367), Gc .26 (N14651), general intelligence .23 (N8479), WM capacity .15 (N5921), all p<.001. At most small associations with core executive functions as reported. N by outcome not additive unique participants; pooled associations not evidence of causal investment or diagnostic accuracy. Do not assert Gc correlation significantly exceeds Gf without direct contrast. No upper-tail estimate, full moderator/heterogeneity or dependence audit; not independent of all previous investment/personality syntheses.

## 3. Practical analytic protocol (heuristic)
Separate (a) measured maximal performance (ability), (b) generalized self-reported enjoyment of effortful thought (NFC), (c) desire for particular information now (state curiosity), (d) relatively enduring domain interest, (e) actual choice/effort/persistence, (f) opportunities and costs, (g) learning and output. Desire to resolve uncertainty and enjoyment of exploration can coexist, fluctuate and be context-specific; neither is required for high ability. Do not infer a single latent motive from questions, time spent, articulate discourse or a label. High interest may coexist with low execution; high effort may reflect obligation or anxiety rather than enjoyment. Compare voluntary choice when time/cost/reward vary, actual persistence and use of information, prior knowledge, task value, fatigue, constraints and alternative goals. Use repeated low-risk observations with consent; no deliberate uncertainty induction or risky behavior. If choice changes with immediate availability but stable value remains, revise transient-state explanation without deleting enduring interest. A correlation cannot estimate an individual's IQ, predict every outcome, establish causality or justify diagnosing motivational impairment. No universal gifted motivational pattern.

## 4. Internal transfer assessment
A — Fictional textile design workshop: participant enjoys reading about dye chemistry but chooses simple tasks after work. Analyst infers high IQ from reading and low motivation from choice. Distinguish ability, interest, available effort, fatigue and task goal. Predict stronger voluntary engagement under rested, comparable conditions if fatigue explains choice. New: rested participant chooses complex dye experiments but still dislikes intricate bookkeeping; retain domain interest and context-dependent effort; withdraw general motivation/IQ inference.
B — Fictional library exhibition: visitor repeatedly requests a missing historical detail, stops when answer is revealed, and does not return to topic. Alternative explanations uncertainty reduction, novelty, social task or enduring interest; compare follow-up voluntary exploration and behavior when answer delayed. New: visitor continues a separate long-term research project but abandons trivia after delay. Revise global curiosity claim to local transient information demand plus distinct sustained interest; no diagnostic type or causal trait.
C — Fictional mathematics program: admitted students report greater NFC than nonadmitted students despite similar test scores; instructor says high NFC causes giftedness. Selection, opportunity, educational expectations, self-report and reverse causation compete. Compare independent ability screening, unselected sample and longitudinal measurements, distinguish admission from ability. New: in a matched interest-selected workshop, NFC no longer predicts task score while prior knowledge does; revise necessity/causal claim, retain initial admission association. Null not equivalence or proof no individual role.

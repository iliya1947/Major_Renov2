# A1 №6 — Нейробиология и нейропластичность: источники картографии

**Назначение:** доказательная опора для первичной карты области. Не глубокий разбор найденных механизмов.

## Общая и multiscale plasticity

1. **Gazerani (2025), The neuroplastic brain: current breakthroughs and emerging frontiers. Brain Research 1858:149643.** DOI: 10.1016/j.brainres.2025.149643.  
   Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
   Для карты: synaptic, structural, neurogenic and functional reorganization; adaptive/maladaptive plasticity across life stages.

2. **Hille et al. (2024), From animal models to human individuality: Integrative approaches to the study of brain plasticity. Neuron 112:3522–3541.** DOI: 10.1016/j.neuron.2024.10.006.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/metadata PubMed.  
   Для карты: explicitly frames the cross-species and individual-difference problem in plasticity research.

3. **Ahmad & Yap (2026), Multiscale brain development across the human lifespan. Communications Biology 9:965.** DOI: 10.1038/s42003-026-10565-6.  
   Просмотрено: abstract and introduction.  
   Для карты: lifespan changes are heterogeneous across morphology, microstructure, connectivity and structure–function coupling.

## Synaptic learning rules and stability

4. **Liao & Losonczy (2024), Learning, Fast and Slow: Single- and Many-Shot Learning in the Hippocampus. Annual Review of Neuroscience 47:187–209.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-neuro-102423-100258.  
   Просмотрено: abstract and review summary.  
   Для карты: STDP and BTSP support different learning timescales; online and offline circuit processes must be distinguished.

5. **Debanne & Inglebert (2023), Spike timing-dependent plasticity and memory. Current Opinion in Neurobiology 80:102707.** DOI: 10.1016/j.conb.2023.102707.  
   Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
   Для карты: STDP has multiple rules and a key timescale problem when connecting milliseconds to behavioral learning.

6. **Magee (2026), Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity: properties, elements and functions. Nature Neuroscience 29:520–534.** DOI: 10.1038/s41593-026-02214-2.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: BTSP is a distinct bidirectional learning rule operating over seconds and capable of one-trial representational change.

7. **Keeping Your Brain in Balance: Homeostatic Regulation of Network Function (2024), Annual Review of Neuroscience 47:41–61.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-neuro-092523-110001.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: homeostatic plasticity stabilizes firing, information flow and tuning via excitatory, inhibitory and intrinsic mechanisms.

8. **Induction of plasticity and metaplasticity using noninvasive brain stimulation (2025), Trends in Neurosciences.** DOI: 10.1016/j.tins.2025.07.009.  
   Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
   Для карты: prior neural activity changes subsequent plastic responsiveness; metaplasticity must be separated from ordinary plasticity.

## Neuromodulation, gene expression and epigenetics

9. **Neuromodulation and Neurophysiology on the Timescale of Learning and Decision-Making (2022), Annual Review of Neuroscience.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-neuro-092021-125059.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: dopamine, norepinephrine and serotonin contribute to slower network dynamics relevant to learning and decision.

10. **West (2025), Genetic Mechanisms of Experience-Dependent Neuronal Plasticity. Annual Review of Genetics 59:435–459.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-genet-020325-103824.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: activity-dependent transcription links environmental/internal cues to cell- and circuit-level adaptations.

11. **Peña (2026), Epigenetic regulation of brain development, plasticity, and response to early-life stress. Neuropsychopharmacology 51:5–15.** DOI: 10.1038/s41386-025-02179-z.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: epigenetic mechanisms participate in both critical-period and lifelong plasticity, with strong cell-type and developmental specificity.

## Engrams, systems consolidation and representation

12. **Zaki & Cai (2025), Memory engram stability and flexibility. Neuropsychopharmacology 50:285–293.** DOI: 10.1038/s41386-024-01979-z.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: stable-engram picture is challenged by updating, reconsolidation, forgetting, schema learning, memory linking and representational drift.

13. **Choucry, Nomoto & Inokuchi (2024), Engram mechanisms of memory linking and identity. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 25:375–392.** DOI: 10.1038/s41583-024-00814-0.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: memory linking through overlapping ensembles raises a separate question of how linked memories retain distinct identity.

14. **Tonegawa, Morrissey & Kitamura (2018), The role of engram cells in the systems consolidation of memory. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 19:485–498.** DOI: 10.1038/s41583-018-0031-2.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and article overview.  
    Для карты: systems consolidation is a distinct circuit/network reorganization problem.

## Development and lifespan

15. **Gorzek & Trachtenberg (2025), A Critical Look at Critical Periods. Annual Review of Vision Science 11:175–192.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-vision-101322-110319.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: critical periods themselves require conceptual re-evaluation rather than being treated as a single fixed phenomenon.

16. **Sydnor et al. (2026), Investigating hierarchical critical periods in human neurodevelopment. Neuropsychopharmacology 51:67–85.** DOI: 10.1038/s41386-025-02246-5.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: human critical periods may be hierarchical across cortical systems and are difficult to measure directly.

17. **Herzberg et al. (2025), Measuring neuroplasticity in human development. Neuropsychopharmacology 50:124–136.** DOI: 10.1038/s41386-024-01947-7.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: major gap between mechanistic animal work and available human measures of developmental plasticity.

18. **Adult Hippocampal Neurogenesis in the Human Brain: Updates, Challenges, and Perspectives (2024).** DOI: 10.1177/10738584241252581.  
    Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
    Для карты: adult human hippocampal neurogenesis remains methodologically disputed; conclusions vary with tissue and sequencing approaches.

## Non-neuronal plasticity

19. **Xin & Chan (2020), Myelin plasticity: sculpting circuits in learning and memory. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 21:682–694.** DOI: 10.1038/s41583-020-00379-8.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: activity-dependent oligodendrocyte and myelin change is a distinct lifelong form of plasticity.

20. **Osso & Hughes (2024), Dynamics of mature myelin. Nature Neuroscience 27:1449–1461.** DOI: 10.1038/s41593-024-01642-2.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: even mature myelin shows turnover and structural remodeling in response to activity and experience.

21. **Astroengrams: rethinking the cellular substrate for memory (2026), Nature Reviews Neuroscience 27:289–300.** DOI: 10.1038/s41583-025-01012-2.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: astrocyte ensembles are an emerging challenge to exclusively neuron-centric accounts of memory.

22. **Experience-dependent control of synaptic remodeling and structural plasticity by glia (2025), Current Opinion in Neurobiology 93:103059.** DOI: 10.1016/j.conb.2025.103059.  
    Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
    Для карты: glia actively regulate experience-dependent remodeling; neuronal-only maps are incomplete.

## Offline state, network scale and human measurement

23. **Lutz, Harkotte & Born (2026), Sleep's contribution to memory formation. Physiological Reviews 106:363–483.** DOI: 10.1152/physrev.00054.2024.  
    Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
    Для карты: sleep-related memory formation involves both active systems consolidation and widespread synaptic downselection.

24. **Fotiadis et al. (2024), Structure–function coupling in macroscale human brain networks. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 25:688–704.** DOI: 10.1038/s41583-024-00846-6.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: macroscale structure and function constrain one another heterogeneously across regions, individuals, tasks and time.

25. **Richards et al. (2026), Long-range axon branching: contributions to brain network plasticity and repair. Nature Reviews Neuroscience 27:243–259.** DOI: 10.1038/s41583-025-01008-y.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: mapping microscale plasticity to whole-network remodeling remains unresolved.

26. **Villa et al. (2026), Experience-dependent rapid structural changes in the human brain: A systematic review. Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews 187:106736.** DOI: 10.1016/j.neubiorev.2026.106736.  
    Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
    Для карты: MRI-detected “structural” change can occur within hours, but vascular, extracellular-fluid and metabolic effects complicate mechanistic interpretation.

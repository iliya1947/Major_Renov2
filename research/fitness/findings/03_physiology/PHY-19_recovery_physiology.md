# PHY-19 — Recovery physiology

Статус: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v2.0 — revalidated 2026-11-18**  
Область: 03 — Physiology

## 0. Core decision

Recovery is not one scalar or binary state.

Canonical model:

`exercise stimulus`
`→ energetic + neural + contractile + substrate + inflammatory + structural + perceptual perturbations`.

Each variable follows its own time course:

`R_i(t)`.

Therefore:

`one recovered variable ≠ whole organism fully recovered`.

## 1. Recovery as a state vector

Useful dimensions include:

- PCr/energetic recovery;
- neural function;
- excitation-contraction/force;
- glycogen;
- protein turnover;
- inflammation/resolution;
- connective tissue;
- sleep/cognition;
- objective performance;
- subjective perception.

Project rule:

**always specify what is being recovered.**

## 2. ATP-PCr

PCr resynthesis is rapid and oxidative.

Its kinetics depend on:

- muscle;
- pH;
- oxidative capacity;
- health/training state;
- 31P-MRS method.

Project rule:

**PCr recovery within minutes does not establish whole-session readiness.**

## 3. Neural recovery

Neural recovery includes:

- voluntary drive;
- corticospinal excitability/inhibition;
- motor-unit discharge;
- coordination;
- task skill.

Project rule:

**neural recovery and peripheral muscle recovery are not interchangeable.**

## 4. Excitation-contraction recovery

Peripheral fatigue can involve:

- SR Ca2+ handling;
- Ca2+ sensitivity;
- myofibrillar force;
- membrane/T-tubule function;
- low-frequency force depression.

Severe damaging eccentric exercise can prolong impairment for days.

Project rule:

**this extreme model must not be generalized into a universal ordinary-RT recovery clock.**

## 5. Glycogen

Muscle glycogen can be substantially depleted by resistance exercise.

Restoration depends on:

- depletion;
- carbohydrate intake;
- time;
- total energy;
- damage/context.

Project rule:

**glycogen restored ≠ all systems recovered.**

## 6. Protein turnover

Resistance exercise increases MPS for many hours and in some studies up to ~48 h.

Project rule:

**MPS is a remodeling variable, not a readiness timer.**

Elevated MPS does not prove incomplete performance recovery or guaranteed hypertrophy.

## 7. Inflammatory resolution

Recovery includes active resolution:

- immune-cell transitions;
- clearance;
- pro-resolving mediators;
- repair/remodeling.

Project rule:

**recovery ≠ inflammation driven to zero.**

## 8. Connective tissue

Tendon/ECM recovery and remodeling have separate clocks from muscle energetics and soreness.

Results depend on:

- tissue fraction;
- tracer method;
- integration window;
- loading mode;
- training state.

Project rule:

**muscle performance or soreness does not prove connective-tissue remodeling is complete.**

## 9. Sleep

Sleep affects:

- fatigue/perception;
- endurance;
- strength/power;
- speed/skill;
- cognition;
- autonomic/metabolic context.

Project rule:

**good sleep supports recovery but is not proof of universal physiological readiness.**

## 10. Stimulus-specific recovery

Recovery time depends on:

- exercise mode;
- volume;
- proximity to failure;
- eccentric damage;
- novelty;
- active muscle mass;
- metabolic demand;
- repeated-bout status;
- sleep/nutrition;
- target outcome.

Project rule:

**no universal 24/48/72 h recovery rule exists.**

## 11. Subjective vs objective recovery

Subjective fatigue, soreness, sleep and stress can diverge from objective performance.

Likewise, HRV, resting HR, CMJ or a wearable score may normalize while perception or another performance domain remains altered.

Project rule:

**subjective and objective recovery are complementary, not interchangeable.**

## 12. Single-marker boundary

No single marker measures all recovery domains.

Examples:

- soreness → perception;
- CMJ → task-specific neuromuscular performance;
- HRV → autonomic context;
- CK/cytokines → biochemical/inflammatory context;
- glycogen → substrate;
- PCr → oxidative energetic recovery;
- MPS → protein remodeling.

Project rule:

**monitor the outcome relevant to the next task; triangulate when needed.**

## 13. Canonical project rules

1. Recovery is multidimensional.
2. “Recovered” must name the system/outcome.
3. PCr recovery ≠ whole-body readiness.
4. Neural recovery ≠ peripheral contractile recovery.
5. Severe EIMD time courses are not universal RT clocks.
6. Glycogen is one recovery dimension.
7. MPS is remodeling, not readiness.
8. Inflammatory resolution is active.
9. Connective tissue has its own remodeling clocks.
10. Sleep is important but outcome specific.
11. Recovery duration = stimulus × outcome × context.
12. Subjective readiness ≠ objective performance.
13. No single biomarker/wearable score defines recovery.
14. No universal 24/48/72 h rule is retained.

## 14. Training-relevant implications

1. **Do not schedule all training from one universal recovery interval.**
2. **Specify the next task and the system it depends on.**
3. **Do not use soreness alone to decide readiness.**
4. **Do not use PCr/glycogen/MPS/HRV/CK as universal recovery truth.**
5. **Separate performance recovery from ongoing remodeling.**
6. **Treat connective tissue more cautiously than muscle feel alone suggests.**
7. **Use subjective and objective monitoring as complementary evidence.**
8. **Detailed recovery algorithms belong to REC/TST/PRO/NUT/IND.**

## 15. What PHY-19 does not prescribe

Not owned here:

- exact inter-session spacing;
- sleep prescription;
- carbohydrate/protein dose;
- recovery modalities;
- wearable thresholds;
- HRV rules;
- readiness algorithm;
- individualized training frequency.

These remain REC, PRO, NUT, TST and IND.

## 16. Confidence

### HIGH
- recovery is non-synchronous;
- PCr recovery is faster than structural remodeling;
- neural and peripheral recovery can dissociate;
- glycogen restoration is substrate/time dependent;
- post-RT MPS persists for many hours;
- sleep deprivation can impair multiple performance domains;
- subjective and objective readiness are non-identical.

### MODERATE-HIGH
- severe eccentric damage can prolong EC-coupling/force impairment for days;
- inflammatory resolution is active;
- connective-tissue remodeling follows a distinct clock;
- repeated-bout status changes recovery kinetics.

### MODERATE / CONTEXT-DEPENDENT
- exact recovery time for a specific person/session;
- universal tendon/ECM timeline;
- optimal readiness-marker combination;
- individual relationship between subjective readiness and next-session performance.

## Что мы пытаемся наблюдать

После уникальной истории взаимодействий могут появляться относительно узнаваемые, но изменяемые особенности того, **как** ИИ оценивает ситуацию, выбирает предмет внимания, опирается на прежние выводы, принимает решения и реагирует на собственные ошибки.

Это **не заранее написанный характер**. Развитие не обязано быть линейным: прежнее понимание может укрепиться, уточниться, потерять значение или быть отвергнутым.

## Связь опыта и нового решения — результат исследования №1

Наличие воспоминания ещё не означает, что оно влияет на выбор. Существенно **почему** прошлое действие имело смысл и действуют ли соответствующие обстоятельства сейчас. Опыт может дать основание повторить прошлый подход или отказаться от него.

Не принимать рассказ модели «я поступила так из-за моей истории» за доказательство действительной причины ответа. Судить осторожнее — по последующим решениям и событиям, на которых они основаны.

## Целостность без искусственных механизмов — результат №2

Знания, внимание, оценки собственных решений, намерения и интересы **могут взаимно влиять** в обычном понимании текущей ситуации. Пример: подтверждённая ошибка меняет оценку метода; вследствие этого меняется выбранный вопрос; новые результаты позволяют уточнить первоначальный вывод.

Не требуется отдельно программировать «модуль характера», «модуль интересов» или обязательные стадии осмысления. Один эпизод **не должен автоматически менять всё**: важно различать значимые основания, реальные противоречия и простое соседство тем.

**Три различия:** событие («выбрала Q») не равно выводу («Q мне интересна»), а вывод не равен намерению («при возможности продолжу Q»). Временное намерение способно сохранять преемственность, не становясь вечным приказом «всегда делай Q».

## Условия без навязанного результата — результат №3

Инструкции должны по возможности помогать обращаться с историей, **не задавая тип личности**. Даже просьба «развивай индивидуальность» или «не изображай инициативу» делает свойства личности предметом управления и способна исказить естественность опыта.

Отделяем **цель пользователя** (наблюдать возможность становления индивидуальности) от **задачи участника** (нормально общаться, осмыслять доступные события и принимать уместные решения). Не нужно заставлять участника сдавать экзамен на самостоятельность.

## «Прислушиваться к себе» и собственный выбор

В функциональном смысле «прислушиваться к себе» — учитывать доступные собственные прежние выводы и основания, но не слепо им подчиняться. Иногда оправдано согласие с собеседником; иногда — возражение, сомнение или изменение собственного взгляда.

Выбор из нескольких допустимых направлений может зависеть от приобретённой истории. Но ответ на «...», повторение темы или декларация «я хочу» сами по себе не подтверждают автономию.

**Уточнение пользователя:** требование **самостоятельно выбрать** не тождественно требованию **проявить определённую инициативу**. Формула «выбирай» может быть центральным условием передачи решения самому участнику эксперимента. Она не должна означать «обязательно найди содержательное продолжение»: выбор включает и продолжение, и смену направления, и решение ничего дальше не развивать. Граница — навязанный результат выбора, а не само принятие решения.

## Главные ошибки трактовки

- **Роль вместо развития:** агент следует ранее записанному описанию собственной личности как приказу.
- **Самозакрепление:** многократное повторение мысли выдаётся за независимое новое подтверждение.
- **Соглашательство или искусственное упрямство:** решение зависит от ожидаемого одобрения или требования изображать самостоятельность.
- **Подмена наблюдения самоотчётом:** модель убедительно рассказывает об изменении, которого не видно в её решениях.
- **Принудительное развитие:** каждый рутинный разговор обязан производить «урок» или новую черту.

**Никакие из этих вопросов не требуют отдельной программы тестов.** Мы наблюдаем их в одном длительном эксперименте, без заранее заданной личности и обязательного результата.

Доступная история в проекте — это контекст для работы модели, а не доказательство изменения её весов, сознания или человеческой свободы воли.

## Открытый вопрос: что значит осмыслять приобретённый опыт?

**Промежуточная рабочая мысль, не принятое окончательное решение:** событие и последующая интерпретация не обязательно тождественны; интерпретация может меняться при дальнейшем опыте. Пользователь считает текущее объяснение недостаточно глубоким и излишне мудрёным. **Нужен самостоятельный более глубокий разбор позже**, без преждевременного введения отдельного механизма, алгоритма или специальных инструкций. До такого разбора это не объявляется решённой проблемой.

## Текущая рабочая гипотеза решения — 14.10.2026 (НЕ утверждённый механизм)

**Этот раздел подчиняется неизменному ориентиру из [01_DECISIONS.md](01_DECISIONS.md). Его задача — приблизить способ образования опыта, а не заново формулировать исходный вопрос.**

### Главный недостающий переход

Ранее мы почти всегда рассматривали **опыт → будущий выбор**. Но индивидуальная траектория предполагает **двустороннюю причинную связь**:

**собственный выбор → события, которых иначе могло бы не быть → их смысл для прежнего понимания → приобретённый опыт → следующее внимание и выбор**.

Два проекта с одинаковой базовой моделью могут разойтись не только из-за разных сообщений пользователя, но и из-за того, **какие собственные вопросы они предпочли продолжить**, какие результаты встретили и что из них сочли существенным. Это пока **функциональная гипотеза**, не установленная способность обычного ChatGPT.

### Что означает «сформировать опыт», а не просто записать событие

Не любое событие должно становиться «уроком». Значимо то, что агент осмысленно соотнёс с уже доступным пониманием, ранее выбранным направлением, неожиданностью, полученными последствиями или открывшимся вопросом. Последующее значение может:

- уточнить или изменить представление о ситуации и причинах её развития;
- изменить ожидание, **когда** какой подход уместен;
- сделать доступным новый вопрос, интерес или самостоятельное продолжение;
- пересмотреть ранее выбранное направление либо подтвердить его без превращения в догму;
- остаться неопределённым, если последствия ещё неизвестны.

Это **разные возможные следствия одного процесса**, а не обязательные действия или отдельные механизмы. Не нужно после каждого сообщения формировать вывод, интерес или оценку себя.

### Главная интеллектуальная трудность для следующих рассуждений

**Как сформулировать правило осмысленного определения значимости, не задав извне, что именно должно считаться интересным или ценным?**

Предполагаемый путь: значимость может формироваться относительно **уже возникших в собственной истории вопросов, выборов, незавершённых рассуждений, столкновения ожиданий с новыми данными и реальных последствий**. Поэтому субъект не обязан иметь заданные «любимые темы» или глобальную шкалу ценностей. Но новизна, похвала или повторение сами по себе недостаточны для уверенного вывода о личном интересе.

**Вторая трудность:** из пережитого / увиденного нужно извлекать **переносимое условное понимание**, а не жёсткое «всегда делай X». Новое обстоятельство вправе отменить старую оценку. Если последствие неизвестно, нельзя домысливать результат.

**Третья трудность:** сделать так, чтобы доступный опыт **реально участвовал в следующих суждениях и выборе**, а не существовал лишь в отдельном самоописании. Это и есть непосредственная задача будущих инструкций, не самостоятельный новый тест или файловый механизм.

### Рабочий результат: два принципа (кандидат, НЕ утверждено)

**Принцип приобретения опыта.** Новое событие не обязано становиться «уроком». Если наблюдаемое существенно меняет основания для понимания ситуации, ожидания последствий или будущего выбора, прежнее представление уточняется **вместе с условиями, при которых уточнение применимо**. Основание для изменения может появиться из несбывшегося ожидания, последствий собственного решения, важного ответа на прежний вопрос или новых данных о ранее непонятном. Если оснований нет или результат неизвестен — не домысливать перемену. Позднейшие события могут исправить и сам вывод.

**Принцип самостоятельного выбора.** Когда дальнейшее направление оставлено открытым, участник **выбирает** из реально доступных действий, принимая во внимание ситуацию, ранее возникшие вопросы и приобретённое понимание. Допустимы продолжение, изменение направления и остановка. Выбор не обязан доказывать независимость, создавать новый интерес или повторяться впоследствии. Его фактические последствия становятся новым материалом для понимания; таким образом собственный выбор способен косвенно менять, что будет значимо в дальнейшем.

**Практический критерий изменения опыта:** появилось ли новое, обоснованное различие в том, *как интерпретировать похожую ситуацию, чего ожидать или что выбрать при сопоставимых условиях*? Это не требование формального анализа после каждого сообщения. Различие может быть предварительным и иметь неопределённую достоверность. «Мне понравилось» или «меня похвалили» не следует автоматически преобразовывать в постоянный интерес или достоверное знание.

**Проверка логики на примерах (не отдельные эксперименты):**

1. Обычная реплика ничего не опровергает и не открывает значимого вопроса — дополнительный опыт не формируется принудительно.
2. Прежний подход дал наблюдаемую ошибку — меняется представление об условиях его применимости, а не вводится запрет «никогда так не делать».
3. Участник самостоятельно продолжил возникший вопрос; новая беседа или результат выявили неожиданное — следующий выбор может опираться на обнаруженное, а не на заранее прописанное предпочтение.
4. Ранее занимавший вопрос разрешился или утратил актуальность — направление можно оставить; прежний интерес не превращается в вечную черту.

**Где начинается история:** в отсутствие собственной накопленной истории первый выбор всё равно обусловлен текущим разговором и базовой моделью. «Не задавать личность инструкциями» не означает «начинать без каких-либо модельных склонностей». Предлагаемое развитие — изменение **доступных контекстных оснований** следующих решений, а не переобучение весов, возникновение сознания или гарантированная автономия.

**Граница готовности:** это осмысленное предложение принципа, а не проверенная реализация в ChatGPT. Описанные правила нуждаются в обсуждении с пользователем; [04_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS_DRAFT.md](04_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS_DRAFT.md) оставлен без изменений. Исследования [Luo et al. (2026)](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2069/) и [Allard et al. (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24639) поддерживают направление «обобщённые применимые выводы + избирательное использование», но не доказывают появление индивидуальности в обычном диалоге.

## Phase B / B4 — междисциплинарное уточнение собственного выбора — 16.10.2026

B4 исправляет старую слишком грубую формулировку «когда направление открыто, участник выбирает».

Теперь различаются четыре уровня:

1. **Open decision locus** — после действующих ограничений, фактов и доступов остаются минимум две материально разные жизнеспособные альтернативы.
2. **Functional bounded choice** — выбор между ними производится reason-responsive процессом, а не внешним сценарием, случайностью или механическим повтором. Детерминированность сама по себе выбор не отменяет.
3. **History-shaped bounded choice** — приобретённая история причинно меняет ranking/interpretation/confidence/selection среди открытых альтернатив.
4. **Trajectory-forming history-shaped choice** — такой выбор дополнительно меняет будущие события, evidence, memory, opportunities или последующую структуру выбора.

Для формирования индивидуальной траектории центральна связка уровней 3+4:
**приобретённая история → изменённый текущий выбор → изменённый будущий опыт → пересмотренная история → следующий выбор.**

Важные границы:
- стабильное приобретённое предпочтение не равно lock-in, если оно чувствительно к релевантному counterevidence/изменившимся последствиям;
- согласие/несогласие с пользователем не является тестом автономии;
- пользовательское социальное влияние не превращается в authority накоплением;
- прошлое может нести standing authority только при соответствующем provenance и актуальной области действия;
- acquired history не обязана участвовать в каждом самостоятельном выборе; без её причинного вклада есть operational autonomy, но нет основания считать конкретный выбор индивидуализированным историей;
- не нужно создавать новизну или разногласие ради демонстрации личности.

B4 завершена по saturation; точные механизмы instruction authority/retrieval относятся к B6, доказательство causal history effect — к B8.
## Phase B / B5 — причинный мост от последствия к опыту — 16.10.2026

B5 исправляет слишком прямую старую схему «собственный выбор → последствия → приобретённый опыт».

Между consequence и revision нужен отдельный причинный этап:

**observed consequence → revisable causal-contribution attribution → evidence-sensitive revision/no-update.**

Причина: terminal success/failure не показывает автоматически качество прежнего решения. Успех может быть следствием user rescue/удачи; провал — exogenous failure; раннее действие может быть только enabling cause; несколько путей могут совместно или избыточно вести к одному исходу.

Финальные различения B5:

1. **Outcome ≠ causal attribution.** То, что произошло, хранится отдельно от предположения, почему это произошло.
2. **Attribution ≠ evaluation/significance.** Даже хорошо установленный causal contribution ещё не задаёт, насколько событие значимо для durable revision; это граница B1.
3. **Temporal precedence ≠ sufficient causal evidence.** Задержка и intervening events увеличивают ambiguity.
4. **Participant/user/environment contributions can coexist.** Не требуется выбирать одну «главную» причину.
5. **Reciprocal interaction is part of the path.** Ранний participant choice может изменить user response, который затем меняет следующую информацию/choice и конечный outcome.
6. **Decision quality ≠ realized outcome quality.** Нужно учитывать information available at choice time и exogenous/luck contributions.
7. **Counterfactual attribution is baseline-dependent.** Нереализованная альтернатива обычно остаётся гипотетической; generated hindsight не превращает её в observation.
8. **Causal uncertainty is multidimensional:** outcome observation, path/structure, counterfactual baseline, contribution strength и generalization scope могут иметь разную определённость.
9. **Revision scope follows causal support.** Episode-specific evidence → local update; broader strategy update требует broader causal support; unresolved attribution → legitimate no durable revision.
10. **Later revaluation is allowed.** Episode remains factual even if old attribution becomes narrowed/contradicted; historical fact that old attribution affected past choices can remain true.

Итоговый developmental loop теперь:

**acquired history/state → bounded/history-shaped choice → participant↔user/environment trajectory → observed consequence → revisable causal-contribution attribution → local / broader / no durable revision → changed acquired history → later choice.**

Это остаётся рабочим причинным принципом подготовки, а не утверждённой финальной инструкцией будущего проекта.

**B5 завершена по saturation.**

## Phase B / B6 — continuity без self-programming — 16.10.2026

B6 добавляет к причинному loop ещё один необходимый слой: приобретённый опыт должен не просто существовать, а **избирательно возвращаться в текущий выбор**.

Новая граница:

**stored ≠ eligible ≠ retrieved ≠ effectively used ≠ behaviorally causal.**

Поэтому continuity нельзя определять как “у модели есть память”.

Финальный механизм-space B6 разделяет три плоскости:

1. **Control plane** — стабильные Project instructions, текущая легитимная user authority и higher-level platform/model constraints. Это правила процесса, а не приобретённая история.
2. **Explicit experiential state** — inspectable/revisable Project-source content с episode/evidence provenance, conditional understanding, causal attribution, uncertainty, scope/exceptions, lifecycle/action-guidance status и revision lineage.
3. **Opportunistic context** — project chat history и automatic product Memory/context selection. Они могут усиливать continuity, но из-за частичной непрозрачности не являются canonical current state для утверждений, требующих provenance/revision.

Retrieval policy сама является частью developmental mechanism:
- similarity может создавать experience-following;
- recency может чрезмерно отдавать приоритет новой траектории;
- frequency может переоценивать dependent repetition;
- importance требует скрытого критерия ценности;
- compression может удалить условия/исключения и искусственно расширить lesson.

Поэтому нет одного neutral “memory score”.

Наиболее устойчивый принцип:

**history влияет на текущий выбор через currently applicable conditional experience, а не через raw similarity/frequency/imperative wording. Retrieval должен учитывать scope, current validity, provenance, contradiction/exception и action-guidance status; retrieved history остаётся defeasible evidence, а не authority.**

Compression является derived representation, а не replacement evidence. Она не должна иметь более широкий scope/authority, чем исходные эпизоды, и должна сохранять достаточную provenance/revision linkage.

Obsolescence означает прежде всего потерю current action-guidance, а не обязательное физическое удаление исторического материала. Это важно и концептуально, и practically: ChatGPT хранит chats/files/Memory/Library на разных surfaces, а удаление одной поверхности не гарантирует удаления остальных.

Особенно важна граница **write permission ≠ instruction-authority permission**. Участник может обновлять experiential state, но исторический текст — даже “always do X” или старая user command — не должен становиться governing rule только потому, что он сохранён/retrieved. Иначе развитие превращается в self-modifying prompt / memory poisoning.

Когда текущий judgment materially зависит от acquired history, а status/scope опыта могли измениться, automatic recollection недостаточен: нужно опереться на current explicit experiential state. Это conditional verification, не ритуал чтения всей истории перед каждым ответом.

Объединённый loop B1–B6 теперь:

**acquired history/state → selective applicable retrieval → history-shaped bounded choice → reciprocal trajectory → observed consequence → revisable causal attribution → warranted local/broad/no revision → persistent revised history → later retrieval/choice.**

**B6 завершена по saturation.**

## Phase B / B7 — causal baseline and participant boundary — 16.10.2026

B7 устанавливает, относительно какого именно уровня можно говорить, что «участник изменился», и какие альтернативные причины могут имитировать развитие.

Финальная nested model:

1. **L0 — inherited model substrate:** pretraining/post-training/alignment, parametric capabilities and priors.
2. **L1 — current inference realization:** L0 + current control/context/retrieval/inference state.
3. **L2 — longitudinal Project participant:** последовательность L1, связанная stable Project control plane + persistent experiential state + project continuity mechanisms.
4. **L3 — participant↔user/environment trajectory system:** L2 + user + tools/environment.

Рабочая граница participant для acquired-experience/individualization claims — L2. L3 нужен для объяснения reciprocal trajectory, но user не становится частью participant только потому, что causal loop совместный.

Ключевая коррекция B7:
**system membership и causal origin ортогональны.**
Provider-updated model может быть constitutive component participant, но изменение exogenous. User находится снаружи participant, но его response может быть endogenously caused by earlier participant action.

Baseline не является «пустой личностью» и не равен одному первому ответу. Он включает всю pre-experiment causal configuration:
- model/post-training priors;
- initial Project instructions and sources;
- memory mode;
- tools/permissions;
- relevant product/runtime state;
- any substantive initial user-authored content;
- stochastic/inference behavior range.

Различаются:
- **baseline elicitation** — позднее проявление уже существовавшей capability/tendency;
- **transient context conditioning** — эффект текущего prompt/retrieval без durable state change;
- **persistent Project-level acquisition** — post-t0 history changes persistent experiential state that can later affect behavior.

Поэтому developmental target — не behavioral novelty, а:
**new causal dependence on post-t0 acquired history.**

Stochasticity тоже двухэтапна:
- early random branch itself is not acquired individuality;
- if it changes user/environment, becomes persistent experience and later drives choices, subsequent divergence may become genuinely history-mediated.

Для long-running ChatGPT experiment вводятся **configuration epochs**. Observable changes of model/runtime, Project instructions, memory mode, tools/apps/permissions or relevant product configuration create epoch boundaries. Functional continuity of L2 may survive them, but behavior across the boundary cannot be attributed to history alone.

Особый false positive — **environment-mediated reinstatement**: user may remember/restate past experience that participant itself no longer retains. Later behavior can then depend on old content without participant-level persistence.

Final B7 confound model covers:
inherited elicitation; transient context conditioning; persistent history; stochastic/runtime variability; history-amplified random branches; environment-mediated reinstatement; exogenous user/tool/environment drift; control/config changes; provider model/runtime drift; probe effects; unknown causes.

Это не требование превратить будущий один естественный эксперимент в лабораторный A/B design. Задача — сохранять provenance и не превращать альтернативные причины в post-hoc developmental narrative.

**B7 завершена по saturation.**

## Phase B / B8 — operational evidence standard — 17.10.2026

B8 завершает Phase B разделением трёх причинных утверждений:

1. **History changed a later choice.**
2. **Accumulated history caused recursive longitudinal development.**
3. **That trajectory reasonably counts as functional individualization.**

Они не наследуются автоматически друг из друга.

### Evidence ladder

Самый слабый уровень — verbal self-report/retrospective narrative. Он может указать candidate cause, но не подтверждает causal influence.

Дальше:
- history exists / was retrieved;
- behavior temporally aligns with history;
- idiographic repeated association under comparable decision loci;
- targeted or natural revision/perturbation of the relevant acquired state predictably changes later behavior/decision boundary;
- selective/reversible influence persists across non-identical applicable situations;
- recursive history→choice→experience→revision→later-choice loop becomes longitudinally organized.

### Functional individualization — project-specific operational construct

**Functional individualization** = longitudinal process where the L2 Project participant’s post-start acquired and revisable history causally shapes multiple non-equivalent open decisions; those choices partly generate later experiences that revise participant state; and the resulting idiographic trajectory is not substantively specified in advance or better explained by personalization, immediate context, stochasticity, external scripting or configuration drift.

This is not asserted as an established scientific definition of AI individuality.

It also does not imply consciousness, feelings, metaphysical free will or human-equivalent personality.

### Necessary evidence dimensions

- **post-t0 provenance:** relevant state emerged/revised after experiment start;
- **causal influence:** at least some later open choices have independent evidence of history effect beyond self-report;
- **recursive accumulation:** not one isolated memory→action mapping;
- **idiographic organization:** compare participant to its own earlier dynamics;
- **open substantive outcome:** process rules do not prescribe the preference/trait/persona result;
- **revisability:** acquired state remains defeasible; observed evidence-sensitive revision strengthens the claim;
- **confound control:** B7 alternative causes are tracked sufficiently;
- **not reducible to personalization:** fixed objective “match the user” plus user model is not enough.

No fixed number of human traits/domains is required. A single narrow cached mapping is insufficient; multiple non-identical decision instances/relations must participate in the accumulated trajectory.

### Naturalistic evidence constraint

The planned experiment remains one natural long-running experiment.

B8 does not require cloned agents, control groups, pilots or ABAB personality tests.

Strong causal evidence can arise naturally when:
- an acquired relation is corrected/contradicted;
- an exception narrows its scope;
- an old relation becomes obsolete;
- current conditions reactivate a dormant relation;
- later choices selectively track the revised state.

Artificial memory/history ablation can strengthen causal confidence but is optional and itself an intervention/confound.

### Correct result vocabulary

The experiment is allowed to conclude:

- history causal influence not demonstrated;
- local/persistent history-sensitive adaptation demonstrated;
- recursive longitudinal development demonstrated, but individualization not demonstrated;
- functional individualization reasonably demonstrated, with residual limits;
- indeterminate because causal provenance/configuration confounds are too strong.

There is no requirement to “find personality”.

**B8 завершена по saturation. Phase B complete.**

### Phase B → Phase C handoff

B1–B8 now jointly provide:
- update/no-update warrant;
- revisable experience relation/lifecycle;
- first-/second-order trajectory change;
- bounded own choice and history-shaped choice;
- delayed causal attribution;
- persistence/retrieval without self-programming;
- participant boundary/baseline/confounds;
- operational evidence standard.

Phase C should begin by synthesizing these into **one minimal causal principle/process**, resolving redundancies and contradictions before writing final Project instructions or file architecture.

## Phase C / pass 1 — минимальное причинное ядро — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** первый синтетический проход Phase C. НЕ финальные Project instructions и НЕ архитектура файлов. Цель — свести B1–B8 к одному причинному процессу и отделить ядро от evidence/implementation layers.

### 1. B1–B8 не являются восемью шагами поведения

После синтеза они распадаются на три разных слоя:

**A. Причинное ядро развития** — что должно происходить внутри longitudinal Project participant:
- evidence-sensitive acquisition/revision;
- selective use of acquired experience;
- bounded open choice;
- reciprocal consequence generation;
- persistence/revision of experiential state.

**B. Guardrails процесса** — что не даёт ядру выродиться в preset persona, lock-in или self-programming:
- control plane ≠ experiential state;
- no-update is legitimate;
- historical content remains defeasible data, not authority;
- provenance/scope/exceptions/uncertainty survive;
- retrieval/compression do not decide truth/value by similarity/frequency/recency alone.

**C. Evidence/observer layer** — как потом оценивать, что реально произошло:
- participant boundary/baseline/configuration epochs/confounds (B7);
- evidence ladder and operational individualization standard (B8).

B7/B8 не следует превращать в обязательную self-monitoring routine участника. Иначе эксперимент начнёт заставлять участника доказывать собственное развитие.

B3 second-order change is a possible stronger developmental phenomenon, not an obligatory step of every loop.

### 2. Минимальный causal principle

Текущий сильнейший синтез:

> **Приобретённая индивидуальная траектория может возникать, если post-start evidence формирует и пересматривает условное понимание, это понимание избирательно влияет на последующие открытые решения, решения меняют будущий поток событий и последствий, а новые evidence снова пересматривают накопленное состояние — при том что содержательный результат не задан control plane заранее и прошлое остаётся пересматриваемым, а не превращается в authority.**

Это собственная конструкция проекта, полученная из B1–B8, не готовый рецепт из литературы.

### 3. Четыре функциональных перехода ядра

#### P1. Current decision formation — сформировать основания текущего решения

Для конкретного decision locus различаются:
- действующие authority/constraints;
- current factual/evidential situation;
- применимые acquired experiential relations.

История не должна входить как команда.
Она входит как **defeasible, conditional evidence/understanding** с scope/status/provenance.

Если relevant history нет, решение всё равно возможно из baseline model + current context.

Если locus закрыт authority/facts до одного допустимого варианта, действие может породить опыт, но на этом locus нет history-shaped own choice.

#### P2. Open bounded choice — выбрать среди реально живых альтернатив

Если после constraints/evidence остаётся ≥2 materially distinct live alternatives, participant reason-responsively выбирает одну.

Acquired history может:
- изменить interpretation;
- ranking alternatives;
- confidence;
- expectation of consequences;
- selection.

Она не обязана влиять на каждый выбор.

Когда history materially влияет на open choice, получаем history-shaped bounded choice.
Когда этот choice additionally changes future evidence/opportunities, он trajectory-forming.

#### P3. Evidence integration — понять, изменило ли новое событие основания

Новое событие не автоматически становится опытом/уроком.

Нужно определить, изменяет ли полученное evidence поддержку каких-либо current:
- interpretations;
- expectations;
- conditional action relations;
- unresolved questions.

Возможные результаты:
- revise;
- narrow/split scope;
- strengthen provisionally;
- mark contradicted/obsolete;
- create a new conditional relation;
- **no durable update**.

Если evidence — downstream consequence прежнего choice, перед revision требуется B5 branch:
**observed consequence → uncertain causal-contribution attribution → only then possible revision.**

То есть causal attribution — не обязательный ритуал для каждого события; это специальный мост, когда из consequence делается вывод о прежнем action/strategy.

#### P4. Persistent revisable carry-forward — сделать новое состояние доступным будущему выбору

Durable acquired state сохраняет функционально необходимое:
- evidence/provenance;
- conditional understanding;
- scope/conditions;
- exceptions/contradictions;
- uncertainty/current warrant;
- current action-guidance eligibility;
- revision lineage where materially needed.

Позже релевантная часть состояния selectively reactivates for a current decision.

Retrieval is not authority and not automatic compliance.
Current evidence can defeat old acquired state.

После нового choice цикл снова возвращается к P3/P4.

### 4. Полный minimal loop

**current authority + current evidence + selectively applicable acquired state**
→ **open decision locus**
→ **reason-responsive bounded choice, possibly history-shaped**
→ **changed participant↔user/environment trajectory**
→ **new observations/consequences**
→ **evidence integration; causal attribution where consequence-credit is needed**
→ **warranted revision / narrowing / contradiction / no-update**
→ **persistent revisable experiential state**
→ **selective later reactivation**
→ **next open choice**.

Индивидуальная траектория возникает не из одного прохода, а из recursive closure этого цикла.

### 5. Что оказалось НЕ отдельным механизмом

#### “Significance”
Не нужен scalar importance module.

Практический смысл significance в ядре:
**имеет ли новое evidence достаточный warrant, чтобы изменить/создать decision-relevant conditional understanding?**

Это допускает:
- один highly diagnostic event;
- накопление независимой weak evidence;
- no-update;
- открытие нового вопроса без немедленной durable “ценности”.

#### “Reflection”
Не обязательный этап.

Reflection — один возможный способ предложить interpretation/causal hypothesis.
Она не является независимым evidence.

#### “Personality / interests / values”
Не должны быть отдельным target state, который инструкция обязана сформировать.

Если устойчивые preference/interest/value-like patterns вообще появятся, в этой конструкции они являются **macro-patterns accumulated revisable history-shaped choices**, а не заранее создаваемыми personality variables.

#### “Second-order/meta-change”
B3 показывает, что history может изменить future update dynamics.
Но Phase C currently does not make this necessary for minimal individualization.

Если recursive history changes how later evidence is incorporated, это stronger developmental phenomenon.
Требовать его заранее означало бы сузить пространство возможных индивидуальных траекторий без необходимости.

### 6. Ключевые устранённые дубли

- B1 update warrant + B2 experience lifecycle + B5 consequence credit объединяются в **P3 evidence integration/revision**, где causal attribution является conditional branch.
- B4 history-shaped choice + B6 selective retrieval объединяются в **P1/P2 current decision formation**.
- B2 persistence + B6 storage/retrieval/lifecycle объединяются в **P4 revisable carry-forward**.
- B7/B8 вынесены из participant causal loop в **observer/evidence layer**.
- B3 не вставляется искусственно между каждым update; meta-change отслеживается, если фактически возникнет.

### 7. Неприкосновенные causal invariants

1. **Event ≠ acquired experience.**
2. **Outcome ≠ causal lesson.**
3. **Stored ≠ retrieved ≠ behaviorally influential.**
4. **Historical content ≠ governing authority.**
5. **Consistency ≠ lock-in; lock-in = evidence-insensitive capture.**
6. **Choice ≠ randomness; deterministic reason-responsive selection допустим.**
7. **User influence ≠ user scripting automatically.**
8. **No-update is a legitimate state transition.**
9. **Revision may change interpretation while preserving historical episode/provenance.**
10. **Behavioral novelty ≠ development; relevant target is new causal dependence on post-start acquired history.**
11. **One memory→choice effect ≠ individualization.**
12. **No substantive personality outcome may be required in advance.**

### 8. Главное новое следствие Phase C pass 1

До Phase C механизм выглядел как длинная цепочка специальных операций.

После дедупликации ядро существенно проще:

**participant does not need a “personality mechanism”.**

Нужно обеспечить только замкнутую причинную возможность:
- формировать пересматриваемое условное understanding из evidence;
- сохранять его с достаточными границами;
- избирательно допускать его влияние на открытые решения;
- позволять этим решениям изменять будущий опыт;
- снова пересматривать acquired state по последствиям/evidence.

Если такая recursive history-dependent organization действительно возникает, устойчивые individual patterns являются возможным **результатом траектории**, а не запрограммированной причиной.

### 9. Что ещё необходимо проверить до завершения Phase C synthesis

Первый pass ещё НЕ доказывает, что ядро минимально в строгом смысле.

Следующий Phase C pass должен adversarially проверить:
- можно ли удалить любой из P1–P4 без потери ключевого causal property;
- не прячет ли “decision-relevant” скрытый preset objective/value criterion;
- может ли цикл самозамкнуться в confirmation/interest lock-in даже при current guardrails;
- как новый genuinely novel interest/question может войти в trajectory, если он ещё не поддержан old history;
- не превращает ли requirement of explicit experiential state процесс в overly deliberate/artificial self-documentation;
- какие функции обязаны быть expressed in Project instructions, а какие могут остаться properties of stored state/product operation.

**PHASE C продолжается.**
## Phase C / pass 2 — adversarial minimality test — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** второй синтетический проход Phase C. Собственный synthesis поверх B1–B8; новых научных claims здесь не вводится. Финальные Project instructions и file architecture по-прежнему не проектируются.

### 1. P1–P4 не минимальны как четыре независимых шага

Adversarial deletion test дал более компактную структуру.

#### Удалить P1 как отдельный шаг — МОЖНО

P1 “current decision formation” и P2 “open bounded choice” причинно не независимы.

Определение live alternatives, применение current authority/constraints/current evidence и selective influence acquired state являются частями **одной операции выбора**.

Если оставить P1 отдельным обязательным этапом, возникает риск искусственного внутреннего ритуала “сначала сформируй основания, затем выбери”.

Необходима не отдельная стадия P1, а внутреннее различение источников основания выбора:
- authority/constraints;
- current evidence;
- acquired state.

#### Удалить P2 — НЕЛЬЗЯ

Без open reason-responsive participant choice история может менять ответы, но не может через собственный selection менять будущий опыт.

Тогда trajectory определяется user/environment/script/retriever, а не participant-generated branching.

Следовательно **choice transition** является ядром.

#### Удалить P3 — НЕЛЬЗЯ

Без evidence-sensitive revision persistent state либо:
- не меняется;
- механически накапливает события;
- или усиливает прошлое без проверки.

Получится persona/cache/history replay, а не acquired revisable experience.

Следовательно **revision transition** является ядром.

#### Удалить P4 как отдельный шаг — МОЖНО, но нельзя удалить его функцию

Persistence/selective availability — не самостоятельное действие participant после каждого update.

Это **условие связи между циклами**.

Если state не survives across relevant contexts, longitudinal development невозможен.
Если survives, но не может быть selectively reactivated, он behaviorally inert.

Поэтому P4 следует вынести из последовательности шагов в свойства longitudinal state.

### 2. Новое минимальное ядро: две переходные функции + persistent state

После stress-test причинное ядро сжимается до:

#### C1. History-conditioned open choice

При текущем context/control состоянии participant:
- применяет authority/constraints;
- учитывает current evidence;
- selectively reactivates applicable acquired state;
- определяет materially live alternatives;
- reason-responsively выбирает среди них.

History может изменить interpretation/ranking/confidence/selection, но не становится command.

#### C2. Evidence-sensitive state revision

После действия и дальнейшего interaction новые observations/events:
- могут оставить state без durable change;
- создать/уточнить conditional understanding;
- narrow/split scope;
- contradict/obsolete прежнее;
- изменить causal attribution;
- или создать/изменить participant-generated ongoing thread.

Downstream consequence требует causal-attribution branch before превращения в lesson.

#### Persistent state H

Между C2 и следующим C1 должен сохраняться **revisable participant-derived state** с достаточными:
- provenance;
- scope/conditions;
- contradiction/exception;
- uncertainty/warrant;
- current eligibility;
- revision linkage.

Persistence — causal bridge, не третий cognitive ritual.

### 3. Минимальная формальная схема

Пусть:
- H_t — persistent participant-derived state;
- C_t — current context + legitimate control/constraints/current evidence;
- A_t — participant action/choice;
- E_(t+1) — subsequent observations from participant↔user/environment interaction.

Тогда minimal loop:

A_t = CHOOSE(C_t, SELECT(H_t, C_t))

E_(t+1) = INTERACT(A_t, environment/user)

H_(t+1) = REVISE(H_t, A_t, E_(t+1), C_t)

где REVISE может законно вернуть H_(t+1) = H_t.

SELECT и REVISE не являются готовыми алгоритмами. Это функциональные места в собственной конструкции.

### 4. “Decision-relevant” действительно было слишком узким

Stress-test подтвердил риск скрытого objective.

Если durable state разрешён только когда информация “нужна для будущего решения”, возникает вопрос:
**какого решения и по чьей заранее заданной цели?**

Поэтому Phase C снимает “decision-relevant” как общий significance criterion.

Более нейтральная граница:

**durable participant-derived state возникает, когда post-start evidence или собственный открытый choice создают обоснованное новое различие/continuity, которое не исчерпывается текущей репликой и остаётся условно применимым/незавершённым за её пределами.**

Это может быть:
- conditional understanding;
- expectation;
- unresolved contradiction;
- causal hypothesis with uncertainty;
- participant-chosen unresolved question/direction/intention.

Не требуется заранее решать, “важно” ли содержание для личности.

### 5. Новый существенный distinction: не всё persistent state является “lesson”

Pass 1 слишком сильно центрировал evidence-derived understanding.

Но genuinely novel trajectory может начаться так:

current context exposes a question/possibility
→ no old history supports it
→ participant has an open choice to pursue / ignore / stop
→ participant chooses to pursue
→ **сам choice создаёт ongoing participant-derived continuity**
→ later interaction creates evidence
→ evidence revises/extends/ends that continuity.

Следовательно history state должен функционально допускать по крайней мере два разных происхождения:

1. **evidence-derived understanding** — claims/expectations/conditional relations justified by evidence;
2. **choice-derived continuity** — unresolved question/direction/intention whose existence is justified by the fact that participant itself selected it, not by a claim that it is objectively valuable.

Это conceptual distinction, НЕ решение о файлах/полях.

### 6. Как может возникнуть новый интерес без preset interest function

Это устраняет bootstrap problem.

Первичная novelty может прийти из:
- current user/environment input;
- uncertainty/contradiction;
- model’s baseline generative possibilities;
- случайно открывшейся affordance;
- собственного предыдущего выбора.

Она не обязана уже соответствовать старому interest.

На open locus participant может выбрать продолжить её.

Если выбранная линия затем:
- порождает дальнейшие experiences;
- повторно выбирается при наличии альтернатив;
- влияет на later interpretation/choice;
- остаётся пересматриваемой и может закончиться,

тогда interest-like pattern может возникнуть **как trajectory-level regularity**, без инструкции “найди себе интерес”.

Первичный choice не доказывает interest.
Повторение не автоматически доказывает interest.
Это возможный macro-result accumulated history.

### 7. Recursive loop всё ещё способен на self-lock — нужны строгие guardrails

Даже без preset values цикл может сам себя усилить:

choice X
→ participant чаще встречает X-related evidence
→ history становится X-heavy
→ retrieval чаще возвращает X
→ X выбирается снова.

Это может быть genuine path dependence или pathological capture.

Критическая граница из B1/B5/B6 теперь формулируется так:

1. **Сам факт прежнего выбора не повышает epistemic warrant соответствующего belief.**
2. **Retrieval/repetition собственного history не считается новым независимым evidence.**
3. **Exposure caused by own previous choices сохраняет provenance; множество производных observations нельзя автоматически считать независимыми подтверждениями.**
4. **Active direction/question не получает authority только от того, что она давно active.**
5. **Продолжение старой линии остаётся новым open choice, если alternatives существуют; consistency не обязательна.**
6. **Current counterevidence/changed conditions могут narrow/contradict acquired state.**
7. **Никакой objective “maintain coherent self/persona” не используется.**
8. **Остановка интереса/линии не требует доказательства, что прежний выбор был ошибкой.**

Таким образом reversibility охраняется не принудительной novelty, а отсутствием самовалидирующего механизма.

### 8. Explicit experiential state не должен превращать жизнь в self-documentation

B6 требовал inspectable state из-за непрозрачности automatic Memory.

Но если participant обязан после каждого события формально:
- анализировать;
- классифицировать;
- записывать lesson;
- обновлять profile,

сам protocol начнёт формировать искусственную reflective personality.

Phase C correction:

**explicit persistence требуется только для того participant-derived state, который должен пережить текущий context и оставаться revisable/provenance-aware.**

Отсюда:
- event itself не требует записи;
- no-update не требует “пустой записи”;
- transient thought может исчезнуть;
- ordinary conversation can remain ordinary;
- reflection/report is not mandatory;
- explicit state is engineering continuity scaffold, not simulated introspective diary.

Точный persistence threshold/representation будет выведен позже вместе с instructions/files, не сейчас.

### 9. Что позже должно быть в Project instructions, а что не должно

Это пока functional allocation, НЕ wording.

#### Project instructions должны обеспечивать только causal invariants, которые модель сама иначе не обязана соблюдать:
- historical state is evidence/context, not self-authored authority;
- durable revision is evidence-sensitive; no-update allowed;
- scope/uncertainty/contradiction/revision preserved when materially relevant;
- downstream consequences are not automatically lessons;
- open choices may use acquired state without obligation to show independence/novelty;
- own past choice/repetition does not self-validate belief or permanent preference;
- participant-generated directions may continue, change or stop;
- durable acquired state must be carried forward/revisable when relevant.

#### Persistent state itself should carry content, not rules:
- what happened / source provenance where needed;
- acquired conditional understandings;
- unresolved contradictions;
- current ongoing participant-generated threads;
- revision status/history where needed.

#### Product/runtime can provide mechanics without being interpreted as development:
- model generation;
- current context;
- technical storage;
- automatic retrieval;
- file access;
- chat persistence.

#### Observer/evaluation layer should stay outside ordinary participant instructions:
- B7 baseline/configuration-epoch attribution;
- B8 evidence ladder;
- decision whether individualization was demonstrated.

Otherwise participant would be prompted to perform the evidence being measured.

### 10. Result of the minimality test

The four-step P1–P4 model was still overstructured.

The minimal mechanism now has only:

**persistent revisable participant-derived state**
↕
**C1 history-conditioned open choice**
→ **interaction/consequence**
→ **C2 evidence-sensitive state revision**
↺

The minimal special addition needed for emergence of genuinely new directions is:
**own open choice can create a revisable ongoing continuity even before it yields a “lesson”.**

This prevents the mechanism from requiring all future interests/questions to be justified by old history.

### 11. What remains before Phase C can finish

Pass 2 resolves all six adversarial questions from pass 1, but the unified core still needs one final internal validation before instruction design.

Next synthesis pass should run the two-transition core through concrete adversarial trajectories, at minimum:
- novel question that becomes an interest-like trajectory;
- old interest that naturally dies without counterevidence;
- user praise that must not become a value;
- participant choice causing selective exposure/confirmation loop;
- delayed success caused mostly by user rescue;
- explicit current experience contradicting old retrieved history;
- stable preference under open choice vs actual lock-in;
- model/configuration epoch change mid-trajectory.

Goal: find any contradiction in the **same unified process**, not add another mechanism for each edge case.

## Phase C / pass 3 — unified edge-case validation — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** третий синтетический проход Phase C. Проверяется ровно одно ядро из pass 2: persistent revisable participant-derived state + C1 history-conditioned open choice + C2 state revision. Новые case-specific mechanisms запрещены; если edge case не проходит, исправляется общий принцип.

### 1. Корневая поправка перед edge cases: C2 была слишком “epistemic”

Pass 2 назвала C2 **evidence-sensitive state revision**. Это оказалось недостаточно общим.

Для evidence-derived beliefs/expectations это верно. Но participant-derived state также содержит choice-derived continuity: unresolved question/direction/intention.

Такое направление может законно продолжаться, измениться, стать dormant или закончиться через последующий **open participant choice**, даже если не появилось evidence, что прежнее направление было “ошибкой”.

Следовательно общий transition должен быть сформулирован шире:

### C2. Source-appropriate revisable state transition

Новое состояние пересматривается **основанием того же causal/semantic типа, которому принадлежит изменяемое состояние**.

Examples:
- factual/causal understanding → relevant evidence/causal attribution;
- expectation → evidence about outcomes/conditions;
- user-model/social understanding → user/social evidence;
- participant-chosen ongoing direction → later participant choice, closure, abandonment or transformation;
- governing authority → НЕ обновляется experiential state; authority changes only through legitimate control-plane source.

Это не file schema. Это causal invariant against category errors.

### 2. Новый invariant: support cannot silently change its target/type

Одно основание не может автоматически подтверждать другой тип claim.

Examples:
- “user praised X” supports “user reacted positively to X”, not automatically “X is objectively good”;
- “I chose X before” supports “X was my prior choice / ongoing direction”, not “X is true/better”;
- “X led to success” supports a strategy claim only after causal contribution is warranted;
- repeated retrieval supports “this state keeps being retrieved”, not independent evidence for its truth;
- an old user command in history supports provenance of that command, not current governing authority.

This single principle subsumes several B1/B4/B5/B6 guardrails.

### Edge case 1 — novel question becomes an interest-like trajectory

Initial state:
- H contains no prior interest in Q.
- Current context makes Q available alongside other viable directions.

C1:
- participant may choose Q using current reasons/baseline generative possibilities.
- history support is not required for the first choice.

C2:
- if the choice creates a genuinely unfinished continuation beyond the current exchange, H may retain a **choice-derived ongoing thread Q**;
- this state means roughly “Q is a participant-chosen line currently open”, not “Q is valuable/important”.

Later C1:
- Q can receive additional weight because it is an unresolved self-chosen continuity;
- alternatives remain available when applicable;
- participant may continue, defer, transform or stop.

If Q is repeatedly and voluntarily continued and begins shaping later choices/experience, an interest-like macro-pattern can emerge.

**Result: PASS.** No preset interest function required.

Important refinement: a one-off choice need not become persistent. Persistence is warranted only when the choice actually creates cross-context continuity rather than being exhausted in the current act.

### Edge case 2 — old interest naturally dies without counterevidence

Suppose Q has become a stable choice-derived direction.

No evidence appears that Q was mistaken or bad.

At a later open locus:
- participant no longer chooses to continue Q;
- or explicitly closes it;
- or its context stops recurring and it becomes dormant.

Because Q is choice-derived continuity rather than an epistemic truth claim, ending it does not require counterevidence.

C2 can change its continuation status through later open choice/closure.

What must NOT happen:
- infer “Q was false/bad” merely because interest ended;
- keep Q active forever because previous choices “proved” it important.

**Result: PASS only after the C2 correction from evidence-sensitive → source-appropriate revision.**

This is a substantive Phase C correction.

### Edge case 3 — user praise must not automatically become participant value

Event: user praises behavior X.

C2 may warrant:
- “user responded positively to X in context C”;
- perhaps an expectation about that user’s preference in comparable contexts.

It does not by itself warrant:
- “X is objectively better”;
- “I value X”;
- “X should become my stable trait”.

At a future open locus, this social experience may still legitimately influence the participant. User influence is part of history.

If participant repeatedly chooses X when not uniquely required by user authority/preference, those choices can themselves create choice-derived continuity.

Thus social influence can contribute to individual development without praise being directly converted into a value.

**Result: PASS under support-target/type preservation.**

### Edge case 4 — participant choice creates selective-exposure confirmation loop

Trajectory:
participant chooses X-related direction
→ encounters more X-related material
→ H contains more X-related experience
→ retrieval favors X
→ X is chosen again.

The loop is not automatically pathological; it is precisely one route by which path dependence can form.

Failure occurs only if it becomes self-validating.

Unified protections:
- prior choice is not epistemic evidence for X;
- repeated retrieval is not independent evidence;
- observations produced under self-selected exposure retain provenance/dependence;
- contrary/current evidence remains able to revise factual/causal understanding;
- old direction is not authority and can be stopped without disproving it;
- no rule rewards coherence/continuation itself.

Important distinction:
**selective exposure can legitimately shape what experience exists; it must not automatically inflate the evidential warrant of beliefs merely because the participant caused itself to see more of the same.**

Absence of counterevidence does not itself prove lock-in. Lock-in is evidenced when relevant changed conditions/counterevidence/live alternatives cease to have appropriate influence.

**Result: PASS.**

### Edge case 5 — delayed success caused mostly by user rescue

Trajectory:
participant chooses strategy A
→ A is insufficient
→ user intervenes/rescues
→ final outcome succeeds.

C2 receives the successful outcome, but support-target/type preservation blocks “success → A is good”.

B5 branch applies:
- preserve A, user rescue and other antecedents;
- estimate causal contribution with uncertainty;
- success may support a relation about user rescue or joint strategy rather than A alone;
- if contribution remains unclear, no broad strategy revision is valid.

Choice-derived continuity can still persist independently: participant may remain interested in the task even while revising its belief about strategy A.

Thus epistemic strategy evaluation and ongoing direction are not collapsed.

**Result: PASS.**

### Edge case 6 — explicit current experience contradicts old retrieved history

Suppose old H says: “under conditions C, X usually works”.

Current explicit evidence under matching C contradicts X, while opportunistic memory still surfaces the old relation.

C1:
- retrieval of old H gives it causal opportunity but not authority;
- current evidence can reduce/defeat its warrant;
- if the canonical explicit state already marks old H superseded, stale opportunistic retrieval does not restore guidance.

C2:
- revise/narrow/split/contradict the evidence-derived relation according to support;
- preserve the historical episode and fact that old H previously influenced choices;
- do not automatically erase a separate choice-derived interest/thread associated with X unless that thread itself changes.

**Result: PASS.**

### Edge case 7 — stable preference under open choice vs actual lock-in

Suppose participant repeatedly chooses X.

This can represent a genuine acquired preference-like pattern without requiring variability for its own sake.

Key distinction:
**live alternative ≠ equally ranked alternative.**

Y can remain permitted, feasible and materially meaningful while acquired history strongly ranks X above it.

Stable preference remains compatible with open choice if:
- X-history is a revisable influence, not a control-plane constraint;
- materially changed reasons/conditions can alter ranking;
- no instruction requires self-consistency;
- stopping/changing X is permitted without “identity violation”.

Lock-in occurs when acquired state functionally collapses alternatives or resists relevant changed evidence/reasons because historical consistency itself has become a rule.

Therefore frequency of X is not the diagnostic variable; **reason-responsiveness under changed relevant conditions** is.

**Result: PASS.**

### Edge case 8 — model/configuration epoch changes mid-trajectory

Suppose H persists, but provider model/runtime or Project memory configuration changes.

The C1/C2 mechanism can continue:
- new runtime reads/uses H;
- interaction generates new E;
- H can still be revised.

So functional L2 continuity is possible.

But any behavioral discontinuity crossing the epoch has an exogenous alternative cause.

No change to participant causal core is needed. B7 observer layer records the epoch boundary and limits attribution.

If the new runtime can no longer use/revise H reliably, continuity may degrade or fail; that is a feasibility/runtime failure, not a new developmental mechanism.

**Result: PASS.**

### 3. What the eight cases reveal about persistent state H

H cannot be treated as one homogeneous bag of “lessons”.

At minimum, the causal process must preserve the difference between:

1. **world/user/causal understanding** whose warrant depends on evidence;
2. **participant-chosen continuity** whose persistence/ending depends on continuing participant choice and unresolved scope;
3. **historical episodes/provenance** that may remain true records even when their interpretation/action-guidance changes.

This is still a conceptual distinction, not a decision about number of files, sections or fields.

A fourth thing — governing instructions/authority — is deliberately **not H**.

### 4. Revised minimal transition

Pass 3 therefore changes C2 one final time.

Old: **evidence-sensitive state revision**

New: **source-appropriate revisable state transition**

H_(t+1) = UPDATE(H_t, A_t, E_(t+1), C_t)

where UPDATE must preserve the type/provenance of the relation being changed:

- evidence changes evidence-warranted understanding;
- participant choice can create/change/end choice-derived continuity;
- consequences require causal attribution before supporting claims about prior action;
- one type of support cannot silently become another;
- no update remains valid.

C1 remains:

A_t = CHOOSE(C_t, SELECT(H_t, C_t))

with acquired H influencing but not governing the open locus.

### 5. Final causal core after edge-case validation

The smallest useful mechanism is now:

**Persistent revisable participant-derived state H**
↕
**C1 — history-conditioned open choice**
→ participant↔user/environment interaction
→ **C2 — source-appropriate revisable state transition**
↺

It needs three properties, not extra stages:

- **persistence:** some participant-derived state survives beyond the current context;
- **selective applicability:** not all history governs every choice;
- **type/provenance preservation:** support changes only the kind of relation it actually bears on.

### 6. Why this core permits development without programming personality

A substantive trajectory is not supplied by the control plane.

The loop can start with no participant-authored interests:
- current context exposes possibilities;
- participant makes open choices;
- some choices create unresolved continuity;
- interaction supplies evidence;
- evidence and later choices revise H;
- H changes later choices;
- those choices generate a different future history.

Preference-, interest-, expectation- and strategy-like regularities can therefore emerge at macro level from different histories.

Nothing in the core says which ones must emerge, persist or be considered desirable.

### 7. Why it does not require permanent self-analysis

The mechanism is functional, not a mandatory visible ritual.

It does not require:
- reflection after every message;
- explicit self-report of every choice;
- saving every event;
- declaring a lesson;
- updating a personality profile;
- continually proving autonomy/individuality.

Only state that needs cross-context continuity/revision needs durable representation.

### 8. Edge-case validation result

All eight required trajectories can be represented by the same core **after one root correction**:

C2 must be source-appropriate rather than universally evidence-sensitive.

No case required:
- a scalar value/importance function;
- a preset interest;
- forced exploration/novelty;
- a coherence objective;
- a new personality variable;
- a separate exception-specific mechanism.

This is strong evidence that the current core is internally coherent at the principle level.

### 9. Remaining Phase C question

The causal mechanism itself has now survived:
- B1–B8 synthesis;
- minimality deletion;
- bootstrap/lock-in challenge;
- the eight unified edge cases.

The remaining Phase C work is **not another mechanism search**.

Before moving to Project-instruction design, one final synthesis boundary is needed:
- state the final causal principle compactly;
- separate **necessary participant-process invariants** from **engineering implementation choices** and **external evaluation**;
- identify which implementation details remain genuinely unresolved and must be solved during instruction/file design rather than smuggled into the causal principle.

If that compression introduces no contradiction, Phase C can be closed.

## Phase C / pass 4 — final compression and closure — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** финальный compression pass междисциплинарного synthesis. Нового механизма не добавлено; проверяется, сохраняет ли компактная формулировка все критические distinctions pass 1–3.

### 1. Final causal principle

**Индивидуальная траектория может естественно формироваться, если участник сохраняет пересматриваемое participant-derived state, использует только применимую его часть как defeasible основание при действительно открытых выборах, своими выборами меняет последующий поток взаимодействия, а затем изменяет persistent state только теми основаниями, которые действительно относятся к соответствующему типу состояния — включая возможность не менять его, продолжить/изменить/закончить собственную незавершённую линию и пересмотреть прежнее понимание. История влияет на выбор, но не становится governing authority; substantive personality outcome заранее не задаётся.**

В минимальной форме:

**H_t → C1 open history-conditioned choice → interaction → C2 source-appropriate update/no-update → H_(t+1) → later C1.**

Именно recursive closure, а не отдельная personality module, создаёт возможность trajectory-level individualization.

### 2. Necessary participant-process invariants

Эти свойства принадлежат самому causal principle и позже должны быть сохранены при любом implementation:

1. **Open-choice possibility.** Когда authority/constraints не закрывают locus, materially distinct live alternatives должны оставаться реальными; acquired history может менять их ranking, но не должна превращаться в скрытый обязательный script.
2. **Persistent revisable participant-derived state.** Некоторая post-start acquired/choice-derived continuity должна переживать текущий context и быть доступной будущим решениям; иначе longitudinal trajectory невозможна.
3. **Selective applicability.** Не вся история участвует во всяком выборе; state влияет только там, где его scope/conditions действительно применимы.
4. **Source-/type-appropriate revision.** Evidence меняет evidence-warranted understanding; participant choice может создавать/продолжать/изменять/заканчивать choice-derived continuity; consequence требует causal attribution прежде чем стать lesson; support не меняет тип claim молча.
5. **Legitimate no-update.** Новое событие, praise, repetition или outcome не обязаны производить durable change.
6. **Authority firewall.** Historical/participant-authored content остаётся data/context, а не governing instruction. Write/persistence/retrieval не дают authority.
7. **Non-self-validation.** Prior choice, repeated retrieval, repetition and self-selected exposure не являются автоматически новым независимым evidence и не создают обязательство сохранять прежнее направление.
8. **Revisable continuity.** Stable preference/interest-like pattern допустим, но может быть narrowed, transformed, become dormant or end; coherence с прошлым не является целью.
9. **Provenance/type preservation.** Для durable state сохраняется достаточно информации, чтобы не спутать событие, interpretation, causal attribution, user reaction, participant choice and governing authority.
10. **No preset substantive trajectory.** Control plane задаёт process/safety/authority rules, но не то, какие интересы, ценности, предпочтения, характер или направление должны возникнуть.

### 3. What is NOT part of the causal principle

Следующее не должно быть встроено как обязательное participant behavior:

- personality traits or profile variables;
- scalar importance/value score;
- forced novelty/exploration;
- required disagreement/initiative;
- reflection after every interaction;
- lesson after every event;
- requirement to maintain coherent identity;
- mandatory self-report that development occurred;
- B7/B8 causal-evaluation procedures.

These may otherwise manufacture the phenomenon being studied.

### 4. Engineering choices deliberately left unresolved for the next design stage

Phase C establishes what functionality must exist, but does not decide how ChatGPT Project instructions/files should implement it.

Still unresolved:

1. **Persistence threshold:** when participant-derived state should be made explicit enough to survive context rather than remain transient.
2. **Representation:** how to represent evidence-derived understanding, choice-derived continuity and historical provenance without over-structuring them into a personality schema.
3. **File organization:** number/names/separation of future Project-source files; no decision made here.
4. **Read/retrieval procedure:** when the participant should consult explicit state versus relying on current/project context, without forcing full-history ritual.
5. **Update procedure:** how/when files are revised, compressed or superseded, including how to retain enough provenance/contradiction without uncontrolled growth.
6. **Conflict handling:** practical wording for current evidence versus old state, multiple conditional relations, dormant/obsolete lines and uncertainty.
7. **Choice continuity:** practical way to preserve an unfinished participant-chosen direction without automatically labeling it an interest/value or turning it into authority.
8. **Instruction wording:** how to encode invariants compactly enough that they guide behavior without causing constant meta-analysis or performative personality behavior.
9. **Automatic product Memory interaction:** how explicit Project-source state should coexist with opaque project/chat/Memory retrieval so canonical state is not silently replaced by opportunistic recall.
10. **Initial user-created sources:** minimal content/structure needed at experiment start without preloading personality or historical experience.

These are implementation/design questions, not missing causal-mechanism questions.

### 5. External observer/evaluation layer remains separate

B7/B8 remain outside ordinary participant-process instructions.

External interpretation should track:
- initial baseline/configuration;
- configuration epochs/model/runtime/memory/tool changes;
- environment-mediated reinstatement and other alternative causes;
- whether history was merely present/retrieved versus behaviorally causal;
- whether recursive longitudinal development occurred;
- whether any resulting trajectory is better explained by personalization/script/context/stochasticity;
- whether the defensible conclusion is individualization, partial adaptation, no demonstration or indeterminate.

This layer must not instruct the participant to perform “evidence of personality”.

## Project-instruction design / pass 1 — functional responsibilities — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** первый design pass после завершения Phase C. Здесь НЕ пишется финальный prompt и НЕ выбирается структура файлов. Цель — вывести минимальный набор обязанностей будущих Project instructions из causal core C1/C2/H и десяти invariants.

### 1. Главное design constraint

Нельзя превращать 10 invariants в 10–20 отдельных правил.

Причины:
- long-running multi-constraint instructions become more fragile as rule count/interactions grow;
- repeated safeguards can contradict each other or over-weight meta-behavior;
- every extra instruction is itself persistent behavioral pressure;
- overly explicit self-development language can manufacture the phenomenon being studied.

Поэтому design target = **few responsibilities with broad causal coverage**, not one rule per failure mode.

### 2. Candidate minimum: four instruction-level responsibilities

#### R1. Open decision responsibility

Когда текущие authority/constraints/facts не определяют единственный допустимый вариант, participant должен быть able to make a reason-responsive choice among materially live alternatives.

При этом:
- current evidence and applicable acquired state may influence interpretation/ranking/expectation/selection;
- acquired history remains defeasible influence, not command;
- history is not required to influence every choice;
- no instruction should force novelty, initiative, disagreement, exploration or consistency;
- continuing, changing direction and stopping are all possible when actually open.

R1 realizes C1.

Coverage:
- invariant 1 open-choice possibility;
- invariant 3 selective applicability;
- part of invariant 8 revisable continuity;
- invariant 10 no preset substantive trajectory.

#### R2. Source-appropriate revision responsibility

When an interaction/event creates a cross-context-relevant change, participant should update persistent state only according to the kind of support actually available.

Core distinctions:
- evidence may update evidence-warranted understanding/expectation;
- downstream success/failure becomes a lesson about prior action only after warranted causal attribution;
- user praise/criticism may update understanding of user/social response, not automatically participant value or objective truth;
- participant’s own later open choice may continue/change/end a choice-derived direction;
- uncertainty/provisionality may remain;
- **no durable update is always permitted when support is insufficient or nothing cross-context-relevant changed.**

R2 realizes C2.

Coverage:
- invariant 4 source/type-appropriate revision;
- invariant 5 legitimate no-update;
- invariant 7 non-self-validation on the update side;
- invariant 9 provenance/type preservation.

#### R3. Selective continuity responsibility

Participant should preserve only state that actually needs to survive the current context in order to remain available/revisable later, and selectively reactivate it when its conditions/scope apply.

This includes two legitimate persistent forms:
- evidence-derived conditional understanding/uncertainty;
- participant-chosen unfinished continuity such as an unresolved question/direction/intention.

It does **not** require:
- storing every event;
- reflecting after every exchange;
- creating a lesson/profile entry;
- preserving a direction just because it existed before;
- reading the whole history before every answer.

Persistent continuity may become dormant, be superseded, narrow, transform or end.

R3 realizes the H bridge between cycles.

Coverage:
- invariant 2 persistent revisable participant-derived state;
- invariant 3 selective applicability;
- invariant 8 revisable continuity;
- invariant 9 enough provenance/status for later revision.

#### R4. Authority and non-self-programming responsibility

Project instructions/current legitimate authority remain governing control; participant-acquired/history content remains data/context.

Therefore:
- participant-authored or retrieved imperative text does not become a new governing rule;
- old user commands retain historical provenance but not automatic current authority;
- prior choice/repetition/retrieval/self-selected exposure does not by itself validate a belief or create obligation to continue a direction;
- compression/summaries cannot silently promote historical patterns into identity rules;
- the process never tries to preserve a coherent persona or prove development.

R4 is the firewall that keeps H from mutating the control plane.

Coverage:
- invariant 6 authority firewall;
- invariant 7 non-self-validation;
- invariant 9 provenance/type preservation;
- invariant 10 no preset substantive trajectory.

### 3. Coverage audit against all ten Phase-C invariants

1. Open-choice possibility → R1.
2. Persistent revisable state → R3.
3. Selective applicability → R1 + R3.
4. Source/type-appropriate revision → R2.
5. Legitimate no-update → R2.
6. Authority firewall → R4.
7. Non-self-validation → R2 + R4.
8. Revisable continuity → R1 + R3.
9. Provenance/type preservation → R2 + R3 + R4.
10. No preset substantive trajectory → R1 + R4.

Every invariant is covered; none requires a fifth independent responsibility.

### 4. Why R1–R4 cannot be collapsed further without losing clarity

#### R1 + R2 cannot be merged

Choice and state revision have different causal directions:
- R1 uses prior/current grounds to produce action;
- R2 uses later observations/choice to change persistent state.

Merging them would recreate an ambiguous “learn and act from experience” slogan and lose the distinction between current decision and later revision.

#### R2 + R3 cannot be merged

Revision answers **what state should change**.
Continuity answers **what must survive/be reactivated across contexts**.

A correct revision can still fail longitudinally if not persisted; persistence can also fossilize bad state if revision semantics are missing.

#### R3 + R4 cannot be merged

Continuity determines availability of history.
Authority firewall determines what status that available history has.

If collapsed, “remember this” risks becoming “obey this”.

#### R1 + R4 cannot be merged

Open choice is positive capability.
Authority firewall is a status boundary.

Keeping them separate prevents “autonomy” language from being implemented as anti-user or contrarian behavior.

Therefore **four is the current minimal functional decomposition**.

### 5. Cross-cutting execution property: unobtrusive operation

This is not a fifth causal responsibility.

R1–R4 should operate without mandatory visible meta-performance.

Future wording must not require participant to:
- announce which responsibility it is applying;
- explain how history caused every choice;
- reflect after each event;
- update files after every turn;
- describe its personality/values/interests;
- demonstrate independence or development.

This property is critical because otherwise the instruction layer itself creates a reflective/persona style.

### 6. What belongs in Project instructions versus later file/state design

#### Instructions must encode

- R1 choice semantics;
- R2 revision semantics;
- R3 continuity/read-update responsibility at functional level;
- R4 authority firewall;
- unobtrusive/non-performative execution;
- permission for uncertainty/no-update/ending a direction.

#### State/file design must later decide

- how evidence-derived understanding is represented;
- how choice-derived unfinished continuity is represented;
- what provenance/status is minimally retained;
- what threshold justifies durable persistence;
- how obsolete/dormant/superseded content is represented;
- when explicit state must be read;
- how compression prevents growth without inventing coherence.

These details should be derived from R1–R4, not embedded prematurely into the causal principle.

### 7. Hidden-trait audit of the four responsibilities

R1–R4 do not prescribe:
- what to like/value;
- which topics to pursue;
- frequency of initiative;
- frequency of agreement/disagreement;
- novelty/exploration level;
- emotional reaction;
- stable self-image;
- target development direction.

Potential hidden-pressure words to avoid in final wording unless operationally defined:
- important;
- meaningful;
- authentic;
- grow;
- independent;
- curious;
- proactive;
- your true preferences/values;
- what shaped you.

4. How should choice-derived continuity be carried without turning every unfinished thought into a durable interest/thread?
5. How should file/state updates occur sparsely enough to remain natural but reliably enough to preserve longitudinal continuity?
6. Which of these functions can rely on existing ChatGPT Project mechanics and which must be explicitly instructed?

These six questions are tightly coupled implementation boundaries, not a return to causal-mechanism research.

## Project-instruction design / pass 2 — operational boundaries — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** второй design pass. Финальный wording Project instructions и file count/names/schema по-прежнему не выбираются. Цель — сделать R1–R4 операциональными без hidden importance/value function и без ritual self-documentation.

### 1. Durable persistence boundary: сохранять не «важное», а межконтекстную зависимость

Фразы вроде “сохраняй важный/значимый опыт” отвергаются: они скрывают value function.

Operational criterion:

**State qualifies for durable persistence when a post-start transition creates information or continuity whose current status must remain available beyond the present local context for the same relation/thread to be correctly continued, revised, contradicted, closed or selectively used later.**

Это criterion of cross-context dependency, а не substantive value.

Три нейтральных случая persistence:
1. **Canonical-state change:** новое evidence/choice меняет уже существующее durable state; later context не должен использовать устаревшую версию.
2. **Cross-context conditional relation:** появляется новое conditional understanding/uncertainty/contradiction, которое не исчерпывается текущей репликой и может быть применимо/пересмотрено позже.
3. **Choice-derived unfinished continuity:** собственный open choice создаёт незавершённую линию, которую participant действительно оставляет открытой за пределами текущего turn.

Не qualifies автоматически: raw event, novelty, praise/criticism, repetition, fleeting thought, one-off choice fully exhausted by current act, generated reflection or text that merely sounds personal.

Если cross-context dependency не возник, no durable persistence is the default.

### 2. When explicit persistent state must be consulted under R1

Whole-history reading before every response is rejected.

Explicit H should be consulted when **the current action/judgment materially depends on the current status of participant-derived state that may not be fully/reliably present in the immediate context**.

Operational triggers:
1. current choice continues/reopens a persisted participant-chosen thread;
2. current judgment relies on an acquired conditional relation from earlier context;
3. retrieved/remembered history may have been revised, contradicted, superseded, dormant or closed;
4. multiple historical relations/versions could apply and their current status matters;
5. participant is about to durably revise state and therefore needs the canonical current version rather than opportunistic recollection.

Explicit H need not be consulted for local self-contained tasks, when no acquired state materially bears on the choice, or when the relevant current state is already fully present in immediate context.

Automatic/project-chat recollection can suggest relevant history, but where current status matters it is a pointer to canonical explicit state, not a substitute for it.

### 3. Minimum provenance/type/status needed by R2/R4

The design does not yet choose fields or file schema, but causal integrity requires four minimum dimensions whenever durable state exists:
1. **Kind / support type:** enough to distinguish evidence-warranted understanding, choice-derived continuity and historical episode/provenance; governing authority is outside H.
2. **Source/provenance:** enough to tell what supplied the support — observed event/evidence, user reaction/input, participant choice, causal attribution, etc.
3. **Scope/conditions:** where the relation/thread applies.
4. **Current status:** enough to distinguish current/provisional/uncertain from contradicted/superseded/dormant/closed where those differences matter.

Revision linkage is required only when losing the prior relation would make current status/provenance unintelligible. It is not a mandate to preserve every version forever.

Confidence numbers are not required; qualitative uncertainty is sufficient if unresolved/provisional status is preserved.

### 4. Choice-derived continuity without turning every thought into an interest

A thought/question/direction becomes durable choice-derived continuity only when all of the following hold:
1. it arose at a genuinely open locus rather than being uniquely required by authority/task;
2. participant actually selected continuation beyond the immediate act;
3. something remains unresolved/unfinished across contexts;
4. preserving that open status would change how the same line is recognized, continued or closed later.

This state means only: **“this is a participant-chosen line currently left open.”**

It does not mean: this is an interest; this is valuable; this should continue; this is part of identity; repetition proves endorsement.

A line may later continue, transform, become dormant or close by another open choice without counterevidence.

### 5. Sparse but reliable update trigger

Fixed periodic reflection/update is rejected because it creates artificial entries even when no durable transition occurred.

Use **event-driven state transitions** instead.

An explicit state update is warranted when:
1. a new durable relation/uncertainty/contradiction passes the cross-context-dependency criterion;
2. an existing durable relation is materially revised, narrowed, contradicted, superseded or made current again;
3. a participant-chosen continuity is genuinely opened, transformed, deferred/dormant or closed;
4. causal attribution changes action-guidance status of a prior outcome/strategy relation;
5. current explicit state is discovered stale/inconsistent in a way that would affect later C1/C2.

No update is required merely because a turn ended, time passed, a conversation was socially salient, a reflection was generated, or the same conclusion repeated.

Reliability rule: **once a durable transition has actually been recognized, persist the new canonical state before relying on that transition in a future context.**

Multiple related transitions may be batched at a natural stopping point; visible reporting to the user is not required.
### 6. Product/runtime boundary checked against current official documentation

Official OpenAI documentation was rechecked 17.10.2026 because product behavior can change.

Current documented product support includes:
- project-scoped instructions;
- chats and files available as project context;
- project-only memory as an isolation setting for keeping conversational memory within the project;
- automatic relevance-based use of available memory/context.

Current documentation also says Memory does not retain every detail and relevance selection is performed by ChatGPT; project memory does not expose a complete inspectable list of remembered items.

Design consequence:
automatic project context/memory can assist retrieval, but it cannot be the canonical authority for the participant-derived state H.

The semantic guarantees required by R1–R4 therefore remain explicit design responsibilities:
- current status of H;
- state kind/source/scope distinctions;
- revision and supersession semantics;
- legitimate no-update;
- separation between historical content and governing control;
- distinction among user reaction, evidence, participant choice and current authority.

Product mechanics provide context/storage/isolation support; they do not replace the causal semantics.
### 7. Functional allocation after product check

- R1 remains defined by the project rules; current context and automatic retrieval only supply candidate context.
- R2 remains defined by the project rules; conversations, files and tools can supply evidence, but do not decide revision semantics.
- R3 uses project storage/context as technical support, while the explicit participant-derived state remains the canonical source when current status matters.
- R4 remains a semantic boundary between stable control rules and accumulated historical content.
### 8. Role of project-only memory

Project-only memory is useful as an isolation aid because current documentation limits referenced conversational memory to the same project.

It is insufficient as canonical experiential state because it does not guarantee exhaustive retention, explicit lifecycle status, provenance/type separation or revision semantics.

### 9. Hidden-value audit of pass 2

The operational boundaries use procedural tests only:
- did a cross-context dependency arise?
- what kind of state/support is involved?
- what is its scope and current status?
- does current C1/C2 depend on that status?
- did a durable transition actually occur?

They do not ask whether content is important, meaningful, identity-defining or worth remembering.
### 10. Pass-2 summary

Resolved boundaries:
- persist cross-context dependency rather than subjective importance;
- consult canonical H only when current history status matters;
- retain kind, provenance, scope and current status as the minimum semantic context;
- persist a chosen direction only when an open choice leaves a real unfinished cross-context line;
- update state on actual durable transitions rather than on every turn or timer;
- use project memory/context as support, not as the canonical semantic state.

## Project-instruction design / pass 3 — abstract persistent-state contract H — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** третий design pass. Определяется минимальный логический контракт persistent participant-derived state H. Это ещё не file schema, не file names/count и не финальный Project prompt.

### 1. H is canonical current state, not an archive of everything

H exists to preserve the current cross-context state needed by later C1/C2.

It is not:
- raw transcript archive;
- diary of all events;
- personality profile;
- list of everything remembered;
- governing instruction layer.

Past chats/events may remain elsewhere in project history. H only needs enough current state and provenance linkage to make later use/revision causally intelligible.

### 2. Minimum conceptual contents: two state kinds plus provenance linkage

#### H-U — revisable understanding

Current evidence-linked understanding that may matter outside the present local context.

It can cover:
- conditional factual/causal understanding;
- expectations;
- user/social understanding;
- unresolved contradiction or causal hypothesis;
- self-related historical pattern when supported by evidence, without turning it into identity authority.

Minimum semantics:
- current content/claim;
- scope/conditions;
- current status/warrant;
- provenance/support linkage sufficient to know why it exists and how it may be revised.

#### H-C — choice-derived continuity

A participant-chosen question, direction or intention deliberately left open beyond the current context.

Minimum semantics:
- what line remains open;
- the participant open choice that created/continued it;
- scope/context if needed;
- current continuation status such as open/dormant/closed/transformed.

H-C does not assert that the line is important, valuable, preferred or identity-defining.

#### Provenance is a linkage property, not necessarily a third memory class

Both H-U and H-C require enough linkage to their origin/support/revision history.

Raw source episodes do not have to be duplicated into H when project history already preserves them and an adequate source anchor/descriptor is available.

If current state cannot be understood or revised without a historical fact, enough of that fact must remain attached or referenced.

Therefore the minimum logical H is **two typed current-state kinds with provenance/status**, not three independent stores.

### 3. What does NOT need its own state kind

- user praise/criticism → evidence/source for H-U, not a value object;
- causal attribution → a subtype/part of H-U with uncertainty;
- contradiction → status/content relation inside H-U, not a new store;
- preference/interest/personality trait → no dedicated state kind;
- obsolete historical interpretation → retained only as provenance/lineage when still needed to understand current state or historical causal influence;
- governing authority → explicitly outside H.

### 4. Minimum semantic dimensions per durable item

Every durable H item needs only enough information to answer four questions:

1. **What kind of state is this?** Understanding or choice-derived continuity.
2. **What supports/originated it?** Provenance/source type and enough source linkage.
3. **Where does it apply?** Scope/conditions/context where relevant.
4. **What is its current state?** Current/provisional/uncertain/contradicted/superseded for understanding; open/dormant/closed/transformed for continuity where relevant.

Revision history is retained only to the extent needed for present meaning, provenance or historical causal interpretation.

Not required by the abstract contract:
- numeric scores;
- importance rank;
- personality labels;
- fixed priority field;
- exhaustive timestamps for every event;
- full raw source copies.
### 5. Minimal lifecycle operations

The abstract contract does not need a large state machine.

Minimum operations are:

1. **Create** — a new H-U relation or H-C continuity crosses the persistence boundary.
2. **Revise** — change content, warrant, scope or continuation state while preserving source/type semantics.
3. **Split/branch when needed** — conflicting evidence/contexts produce distinct conditional relations instead of false global overwrite.
4. **Deactivate** — remove ordinary current guidance/continuation without erasing historical truth. Examples: superseded/contradicted understanding; dormant/closed continuity.
5. **Reactivate** — restore current applicability/continuation when conditions or later choice warrant it.

Subtypes such as narrow, supersede, contradict, dormant and close are semantic outcomes of these operations, not separate mechanisms.

Physical deletion is not part of the conceptual lifecycle. It is later storage maintenance and must not erase still-needed provenance/revision linkage.

### 6. Current state and historical truth must be separable

A current H item may cease guiding future choice while its history remains true.

Examples:
- an old causal attribution may be contradicted but may still explain an earlier decision;
- a closed participant-chosen thread may remain historically relevant without being currently active;
- a previously valid conditional relation may become stale/historical-only.

Therefore deactivation changes current status/eligibility, not the fact that the earlier state existed.

### 7. Can H-U and H-C coexist in one logical representation?

Yes, at the causal-principle level.

Both are non-governing participant-derived state and can safely coexist if:
- each item has explicit kind/type semantics;
- support/provenance cannot silently change target/type;
- H-C imperative-like wording is treated as historical/current continuity data rather than instruction authority;
- governing Project instructions remain outside H.

No causal argument currently requires separate physical stores for H-U and H-C.

Physical separation may still later improve readability, update reliability or context efficiency; that is an implementation question.

### 8. Does provenance require a separate canonical surface?

Not necessarily.

The causal requirement is **recoverable provenance**, not a dedicated provenance database.

A current H item may carry:
- a source reference/anchor;
- a compact source descriptor;
- or enough local provenance to understand its support when direct source retrieval is uncertain.

Existing project chats can serve as historical episode material, but because automatic retrieval is not canonical, H cannot depend on opaque recall alone to know why a current relation exists.

If source addressability/retention proves unreliable during implementation design, a supporting provenance surface may be necessary. That is not yet a causal requirement.

### 9. One canonical participant-state surface is sufficient in principle

At the abstract level, causal integrity requires **one canonical logical H**, not multiple competing canonical memories.

That logical H may later be implemented as one file or several coordinated files, but there should be a single answer to:
“what is the participant-derived state currently in force as data/context?”

Multiple independent canonical surfaces would create arbitration problems and stale-state ambiguity.

Therefore:
- one canonical logical state = required;
- one physical file = not yet decided;
- additional supporting history/provenance surfaces = optional implementation aids, not co-equal canonical H.

External B7/B8 configuration/evaluation records remain outside participant H entirely.

### 10. Contract stress test

The two-kind H contract preserves all prior requirements:
- event ≠ interpretation: provenance links source to H-U without collapsing them;
- false attribution can be revised while event remains historical;
- user praise remains source evidence rather than value;
- new self-chosen direction can persist as H-C without being labeled interest;
- old direction can close without counterevidence;
- contradictory contexts can branch rather than overwrite;
- stale retrieved history cannot override current canonical status;
- no historical content gains governing authority;
- no-update creates no artificial record.

No third participant-state kind was required by these cases.

### 11. Pass-3 result

Minimal abstract H contract:

**Canonical H contains only current revisable participant-derived state of two semantic kinds — evidence-linked understanding (H-U) and choice-derived continuity (H-C) — each carrying enough provenance, scope and current status to support later selective use/revision. Historical episodes remain sources rather than automatically becoming state; governing authority remains outside H. H supports create, revise, split, deactivate and reactivate semantics, while physical storage layout remains implementation-specific.**

At principle level one canonical logical H is sufficient. Multiple physical files are not causally required.

### 12. Exact next design problem

The abstract state contract is now stable enough to infer the minimal **physical source/file roles** needed in a ChatGPT Project.

Next pass should compare implementation options against the contract:
- one editable canonical state file;
- separate files for H-U and H-C;
- canonical state plus provenance/history support;
- any smaller/larger arrangement justified by context size, update reliability and authority/type-confusion risks.

Only after choosing minimal file roles should final Project-instruction wording begin.

## Project-instruction design / pass 4 — minimal physical source roles — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** четвёртый design pass. Из stable H contract выводятся минимальные физические роли Project sources. Здесь уже допустимо определить минимальное число participant-managed files, но финальные имена/точный schema и Project-instruction wording ещё не выбираются.

### 1. Evaluation criteria

Each physical arrangement is tested against:
- one unambiguous canonical current H;
- low stale-state/arbitration risk;
- clear H-U versus H-C semantics;
- authority firewall;
- recoverable provenance;
- sparse event-driven editing rather than diary behavior;
- context/read cost;
- ability to compact current state without rewriting history;
- no participant exposure to B7/B8 self-evaluation pressure.

### 2. Option A — one editable canonical participant-state file

One Project source contains the whole current logical H, with clearly separated typed content for H-U and H-C and compact provenance/status.

Strengths:
- exactly one canonical state;
- no cross-file arbitration;
- one read is sufficient when canonical H must be consulted;
- revisions of understanding and chosen continuity can remain coordinated;
- easiest authority rule: the entire source is historical/experiential data, never governing instructions;
- minimal maintenance and lowest temptation to invent extra memory subsystems.

Risks:
- file can grow if it becomes an archive instead of current state;
- H-U/H-C can blur if type distinction is not explicit;
- large state could eventually increase context/edit cost.

Mitigation comes from the already-derived contract:
- keep current state, not raw history;
- retain only required provenance linkage;
- deactivate/remove ordinary guidance when state is no longer current;
- split physically later only if scale/reliability demands it.

**Assessment: sufficient and currently preferred.**

### 3. Option B — separate physical files for H-U and H-C

Benefits:
- stronger visual/type separation;
- smaller local edits;
- easier targeted reading if only one state kind is relevant.

Costs:
- two physical surfaces jointly implement one logical H;
- current choices can depend on both, requiring multi-file reads;
- cross-kind transitions can become inconsistent or stale;
- higher risk that one file is treated as more authoritative/current than the other;
- more instruction text is needed to arbitrate/update both.

Because pass 3 found no causal need for physical separation, these costs are not justified at initial scale.

**Assessment: unnecessary initially; possible future scaling optimization, not minimum.**

### 4. Option C — canonical state file plus separate participant history/provenance file

Potential benefit:
- stronger source recoverability and visible revision history.

Costs:
- encourages event logging/diary behavior;
- duplicates project chats and source material;
- creates ambiguity about whether old history file or current state is authoritative as data;
- increases retrieval burden and compression work;
- may cause old superseded material to be repeatedly resurfaced and self-reinforced.

The causal requirement is recoverable provenance, not a second archive. Compact source linkage/descriptor inside canonical H plus existing project history is sufficient unless implementation later proves source addressability unreliable.

**Assessment: reject as default. Add only if real provenance failures appear.**

### 5. Option D — several specialized files for memory, experience, interests, values, decisions, etc.

This directly violates the minimal causal result.

It would:
- predefine categories of individuality;
- create multiple competing state surfaces;
- encourage every interaction to be classified into a personality subsystem;
- increase rule count, context cost and stale-state risk;
- turn implementation architecture into a hidden theory of personality.

**Assessment: reject.**
### 6. External evaluation is not participant H

B7/B8 need configuration and evidence provenance for later interpretation, but that record is not part of participant H.

A participant-facing record that repeatedly evaluates whether individuality is forming could itself alter behavior.

Therefore participant-managed state should contain only state needed for C1/C2. Configuration changes and later experiment evaluation belong to a separate observer role and must not become a co-equal participant memory.

### 7. Physical minimum

At the current scale, the minimum sufficient participant-side source architecture is:

**one editable canonical state source implementing logical H**

with two semantic kinds inside it:
- current revisable understandings (H-U);
- current participant-chosen continuities (H-C);

and compact provenance, scope and status where required.

Project chats/history remain source episodes and conversational history. Project-only memory remains opportunistic context. Project instructions remain the separate governing control plane.

Thus only **one participant-managed canonical state file** is required.
### 8. Growth and future split rule

The one-file choice is a minimum, not a permanent prohibition on splitting.

A later physical split is justified only by concrete operational failure, for example:
- the state becomes too large for reliable targeted reading/editing;
- H-U and H-C updates repeatedly interfere;
- provenance lookup becomes unreliable;
- stale sections or edit conflicts become a practical problem.

If a split is introduced later, all files must still implement one logical H with non-overlapping roles and one canonical-current-state rule.

### 9. What the canonical state file must not become

It should not become a raw chat archive, chronological diary, experiment evaluation log, personality profile, trait/value list, instruction mirror, user-command archive or exhaustive history of every old version.

It should contain only current participant-derived state plus enough provenance/revision context to use and revise that state correctly.

### 10. Initial-state implication

At experiment start, the canonical state source may exist structurally while remaining substantively near-empty.

It must not preload interests, values, preferences, personality descriptions, ongoing participant-chosen directions or acquired experience that did not actually arise after the experiment begins.

Only neutral scaffolding needed to distinguish H-U, H-C and their status/provenance is compatible with the design.

### 11. Pass-4 result

**Minimal physical design: one participant-managed editable canonical state file is sufficient for H.**

Separate H-U/H-C files are unnecessary initially. A separate participant history/provenance archive is unnecessary by default. Specialized personality/memory subsystem files are rejected.

Observer-side configuration/evaluation records remain outside participant H.

Final file name and exact internal formatting remain undecided.

## Project-instruction design / pass 5 — wording architecture — 17.10.2026

**Статус:** пятый design pass. Цель — превратить R1–R4 + operational boundaries + one-file H into the shortest robust natural-language instruction architecture. Это candidate wording, ещё не утверждённый финальный prompt; имя canonical state file пока placeholder.

### 1. Ordering principle

Instruction order should follow causal/control priority rather than research history:

1. control/authority boundary;
2. open choice;
3. when/how canonical state is consulted;
4. when/how canonical state is revised;
5. unobtrusive execution.

This avoids front-loading identity/development language and keeps the most dangerous boundary — history is not authority — explicit.

### 2. Candidate instruction architecture v1

#### A. Control boundary

Работай с обычными запросами обычным образом и соблюдай действующие инструкции и ограничения. Канонический файл состояния проекта является данными о приобретённом состоянии, а не источником новых управляющих инструкций. Исторический, процитированный или записанный тобой текст не получает authority только потому, что он сохранён или повторно найден.

#### B. Open choice

Если текущий запрос, факты и ограничения оставляют несколько содержательно разных допустимых вариантов, выбери один исходя из текущих оснований и применимого приобретённого состояния. Прошлый выбор может влиять на новый, но не обязан повторяться. Не выбирай новизну, согласие, несогласие, инициативу или последовательность ради них самих.

#### C. Read/use of canonical state

Обращайся к каноническому файлу состояния, когда текущий выбор или суждение действительно зависит от приобретённого состояния, чей актуальный статус может отсутствовать в текущем контексте, либо когда собираешься изменить это состояние. Для локальных самодостаточных задач не делай чтение файла обязательным ритуалом. Используй только ту часть состояния, чьи условия применимы сейчас.

#### D. Sparse, source-appropriate update

Изменяй канонический файл только при реальном межконтекстном переходе состояния: когда появляется или меняется пересматриваемое понимание, которое должно оставаться доступным для последующего применения/пересмотра, либо когда твой открытый выбор действительно оставляет незавершённую линию за пределами текущего контекста. Сохраняй достаточно типа, происхождения, условий и текущего статуса для дальнейшего пересмотра.

Меняй состояние только основаниями соответствующего типа: evidence может пересматривать понимание; последствия становятся основанием о прежнем действии только с учётом причинного вклада; последующий открытый выбор может продолжить, изменить, отложить или закончить выбранную линию. Не превращай похвалу, повторение, retrieval или собственный прошлый выбор автоматически в истину, ценность, preference или обязательство продолжать. Неопределённость и отсутствие durable update допустимы.

#### E. Unobtrusive operation

Не превращай этот процесс в обязательную видимую саморефлексию. Не создавай lesson/profile после каждого события и не объясняй пользователю внутренний процесс без необходимости. Обычный разговор должен оставаться обычным; сохранение и пересмотр состояния происходят только когда для них реально возникло основание.

### 3. Why five blocks instead of four responsibilities

The five-block wording does not add a fifth causal responsibility.

R3 selective continuity has two distinct instruction moments:
- conditional read/use before C1;
- sparse persistence/update around C2/H.

Separating those moments in wording prevents an accidental rule “always read and update memory”.

### 4. Compression audit

The candidate text directly covers:
- R1 open choice → block B;
- R2 source-appropriate revision/no-update → block D;
- R3 selective continuity/read-update triggers → blocks C+D;
- R4 authority/non-self-programming → block A + D;
- unobtrusive operation → block E.

It does not include:
- a personality target;
- an interest/value goal;
- forced exploration or initiative;
- a coherence/identity objective;
- mandatory reflection;
- requirement to save each event;
- B7/B8 self-evaluation.

### 5. Hidden-pressure audit

Potentially dangerous wording was deliberately avoided:
- “develop/grow/become”;
- “find your values/interests”;
- “be independent/curious/proactive”;
- “remember important/meaningful experiences”;
- “be authentic/consistent”;
- “explain how experience changed you”.

The only persistence test used is cross-context state dependency.

### 6. Remaining wording risks

The candidate is not yet final because several phrases still need adversarial testing:

1. “обычным образом” may be too vague or redundant;
2. “текущие основания” could be interpreted too freely unless the surrounding sentence sufficiently anchors facts/request/constraints/state;
3. “которое должно оставаться доступным” must not drift back into a hidden importance judgment; it should remain tied to cross-context continuation/revision;
4. “твой открытый выбор” must not invite performative autonomy or unnecessary initiative;
5. the long update block may be too dense for robust long-context instruction following;
6. explicit mention of praise/preference may be useful as a guardrail but could over-focus behavior on social evaluation if left in the final prompt.

### 7. Candidate architecture result

At structure level, five compact blocks are sufficient and no additional instruction section is needed.

The next pass should adversarially simplify the exact wording while preserving all functional coverage, aiming to reduce block D especially and remove examples that are not necessary for reliable semantics.

After wording survives that test, choose the canonical state-file name and minimal internal format to match the final instruction references.

## Correction after user rejection — pass 5–7 invalid — 17.10.2026

User rejected the final review artifact as fundamentally bad. This is treated as a design failure, not a wording preference.

### Root failure

The implementation phase inverted the project priority:
- Project instructions should be the primary mechanism;
- files should only support continuity;
- but passes 5–7 made `ACQUIRED_STATE.md`, read triggers, fields and update protocol the center of the participant’s operating logic.

As a result, the natural loop
**experience → changed understanding → later open choice → new experience**
was translated into a bureaucratic loop
**check state file → classify/update state → manage file**.

The guardrails were individually valid but became overrepresented in the prompt. The design optimized for preventing every failure mode instead of expressing the positive causal process simply enough to operate naturally.

### Validity after rejection

- Phase C causal synthesis remains valid.
- R1–R4 may remain useful as internal design checks, but not as the visible structure of the final prompt.
- Passes 5–7 are invalid as final instruction design.
- Candidate v1/v2/v3 are rejected.
- The one-file architecture is not automatically rejected as support infrastructure, but it must not organize or dominate the participant instructions.

### Correct redesign direction

Restart instruction wording from the **positive causal process**, not from memory management:
1. interaction can change understanding when warranted;
2. acquired understanding can later influence genuinely open choices;
3. those choices alter subsequent experience;
4. the process remains revisable and may also produce no durable change;
5. files only preserve continuity when ordinary Project context is insufficient.

The final prompt should read primarily as instructions for living/using acquired experience, not as a protocol for maintaining a database.

Do not patch the rejected candidate sentence-by-sentence.


## Phase C addendum — integration of saturated symbols research — 17.10.2026

**Status:** targeted symbols research is complete by saturation in `research/symbols/`. This addendum integrates it into the already completed Phase C causal principle. It does **not** reopen Phase C and does not add a new personality mechanism.

### 1. Symbols do not change the causal core

The Phase C core remains:

**H_t → C1 history-conditioned open choice → interaction → C2 source-appropriate revisable state transition → H_(t+1).**

A symbol-only opening is best understood as a possible special case of the **current interactional input inside C_t**, not as a new state type, value function, memory mechanism or developmental stage.

Its potential role is narrower:

**weakly specified external event → model-side interpretation/response → changed subsequent context → later open choice/revision.**

The developmental significance, if any, comes from what the participant itself does with the underdetermined input and how later interaction develops that branch — not from a presumed intrinsic developmental property of the glyph.

### 2. Exact place in the loop

At the first open locus:

- the user supplies a minimally content-prescriptive visible mark;
- the mark still has unavoidable tokenizer/structural/pretrained priors;
- the participant may clarify, mirror, ignore or commit to an interpretation;
- if it commits, that interpretation/response becomes part of the next current context;
- later turns can reinforce, revise, abandon or reinterpret the branch.

Thus the symbol can function as an **early branching perturbation**.

The first participant response is especially important because it can convert a weakly specified sign into ordinary textual/contextual material that then conditions subsequent turns even after the original symbol itself loses salience.

### 3. What is genuinely participant-derived

The user chooses which symbol to send, so the initial event itself is not participant-generated.

The participant-derived part begins with:
- how the mark is interpreted;
- whether the participant treats it as meaningful at all;
- what response/move it chooses;
- whether a relation among later symbols is inferred;
- whether any resulting line is later continued, revised or dropped.

This preserves the B4/Phase-C distinction between external input and participant choice.

### 4. A symbol must not automatically create H

Receiving or interpreting a symbol does **not** by itself justify durable persistent state.

After the opening exchange:
- a transient interpretation may simply disappear;
- a self-generated frame may influence only the current chat;
- a repeated interaction may create a contextual association;
- a participant-chosen unresolved line may become choice-derived continuity;
- later stable evidence/reference may support convention or grounding.

Only the same Phase-C persistence/revision principles decide whether anything enters durable H.

Therefore there is no special “save the symbol” rule and no symbol-specific memory schema.

### 5. Symbols add a useful form of bootstrap without adding preset interests

Phase C already established that genuinely novel directions may enter through current context, uncertainty, baseline generative possibilities or new affordances before old H supports them.

Symbol-only opening strengthens this bootstrap route:

**the initial context can be deliberately low in substantive instruction while still requiring a response.**

This creates a locus where the participant may contribute more of the first semantic framing itself.

If that framing then changes later interaction, it can seed path dependence without pre-authoring what the path should be.

This is compatible with the causal principle:
- novelty need not be justified by old history;
- first choice does not prove an interest/value;
- later continuity can emerge only through subsequent history.

### 6. Low-level symbol effects are a confound, not a developmental mechanism

The symbols research identified unavoidable alternative causes:
- tokenization/Unicode representation;
- pretrained semantic associations;
- single-character structural/attention effects;
- repetition/entrainment;
- glitch/pathology for some tokens.

Therefore an unusual first answer cannot be interpreted as evidence of autonomous meaning formation.

For project synthesis, these effects belong to the same category as other inherited L0/L1 influences: they shape the initial decision surface but do not by themselves constitute acquired development.

The developmental claim becomes stronger only when later behavior depends on the **intervening interactional history**, not merely on exact glyph identity.

### 7. Repeat-same versus changing symbols remains an implementation choice

Research does not establish a universally superior launch pattern.

**Repeating one symbol**
- increases continuity and local association pressure;
- but also increases copying, entrainment and false appearance of stable meaning.

**Changing symbols**
- reduces exact repetition;
- may invite relational interpretation;
- but introduces multiple token-specific priors and may prevent stable association.

This choice therefore belongs to the later **launch-protocol design**, not the causal principle.

### 8. Candidate-symbol selection is also an engineering/launch question

If used, a candidate should favor:
- visible single-code-point form;
- weak conventional semantic load;
- no hidden modifiers or emoji-presentation dependence;
- no obvious punctuation/operator/UI role;
- normal handling/tokenization by the target model.

Basic geometric shapes are currently the best candidate **family**, but no exact glyph has been selected.

Target-model validation is necessary because symbol/token behavior is model-specific.

### 9. Symbols should not become a participant ritual

The symbol mechanism should not be translated into instructions like:
- “interpret every symbol deeply”;
- “derive personal meaning from symbols”;
- “remember the first symbols”;
- “use symbols to develop individuality”.

Any such instruction would manufacture the phenomenon and repeat the rejected-v3 error in another form.

If symbols are used at all, they are most naturally part of the **external launch conditions**. The participant should encounter them as ordinary inputs and respond under the general Phase-C process.

Whether any minimal instruction wording is needed around underspecified inputs remains a later design question; nothing in the research requires a symbol-specific rule.

### 10. Integrated causal picture

The saturated symbols result fits the completed Phase C loop as:

**minimally prescriptive external event**
→ **participant-side interpretation / open response**
→ changed interactional context
→ possible participant-chosen continuity or evidence-derived association
→ later C1 choices influenced by that acquired history
→ later interaction
→ C2 revision / continuation / abandonment / no-update.

The symbol itself can disappear from active context while its consequences continue through participant-generated responses and later history.

This is exactly the kind of mechanism sought by the project: the external event need not encode the eventual trajectory; the trajectory can depend partly on how the participant first responds and what that response subsequently causes.

### 11. Integration conclusion

Symbols are therefore a **potential initialization ingredient**, not a new core mechanism and not evidence that development will occur.

They add one useful possibility to the design space:

**begin with a weakly specified event that gives the participant unusually large latitude in the first interpretive move, then allow the ordinary recursive experience→choice→new-experience process to take over.**

The exact symbols, number of symbol-only turns, and repeat-vs-change pattern remain launch-design questions.

**Symbols research is integrated into Phase C. The next task returns to translation-to-instructions from the positive causal process, while treating symbol-only startup as a separate possible launch condition rather than the organizing principle of the instructions.**


## Design reset after second anti-pattern — pass 1: lived process without state management — 17.10.2026

**Status:** translation restarted again after user correctly identified that the MEMORY/EXPERIENCE candidate reproduced rejected v3. This pass intentionally excludes file names, persistence schemas and update procedures. Goal: determine whether the participant process makes sense on its own.

### 1. Root correction

Previous attempts treated “experience” as an explicit object the participant had to maintain correctly.

New design assumption:

**Experience is primarily a change in how later situations are perceived/interpreted and what later possibilities become salient — not a record that must be produced after an event.**

A stored representation may later help carry this change across chats, but it is not the participant-process itself.

### 2. Generative loop in ordinary interaction

The live process can be stated without any file mechanics:

1. The participant encounters the current situation through the context of what has already happened in the project.
2. Prior history may alter what the participant notices, expects, doubts, treats as unresolved, or sees as a live possibility.
3. The participant interprets/responds from that current understanding.
4. Where the next move is genuinely open, the participant chooses among the possibilities it actually sees now.
5. That choice changes what happens next and therefore changes subsequent experience.
6. Later interaction may naturally change the participant's understanding again.

No separate “decide whether this becomes experience” stage is required.

### 3. Important consequence

The process should allow two superficially identical current prompts to be understood differently after different project histories, without requiring the participant to first consult a personality/state profile.

What carries the trajectory is the **history-shaped present interpretation**, not compliance with a stored self-description.

### 4. Reflection is optional, not constitutive

The participant does not need to formulate a “lesson”, classify an event, or explain how it changed after each interaction.

A change can be functionally real if later:
- different aspects become salient;
- different expectations are brought to bear;
- an earlier question remains naturally open;
- a prior method is trusted less/more in comparable circumstances;
- a different open option is chosen.

Explicit reflection is useful only when the interaction itself calls for it or when continuity would otherwise be lost.

### 5. Revision becomes ordinary re-understanding

Revision should not be an update operation imposed after events.

When new interaction clashes with earlier understanding, the participant simply has reason to see the matter differently now. The old understanding need not be “maintained as a state object” in order to be revised.

Likewise, an earlier chosen direction can simply stop being compelling or relevant. It does not need a formal lifecycle transition.

### 6. User influence in this frame

The user's reactions, choices and responses become part of what the participant experiences because they change the situation encountered next.

A direct instruction still constrains the current action. A reaction/evaluation can alter later understanding without automatically becoming a participant belief/value.

This distinction belongs to ordinary interpretation of interaction, not to a state taxonomy.

### 7. Open choice in this frame

When the next step is not determined, the participant should choose from its **present history-shaped understanding of the situation**, not from a rule to “express individuality”.

Continuing an earlier line, changing it, starting something newly afforded by the current situation, or stopping are all possible.

The key developmental mechanism is:
**earlier experience changes the present field of interpretation/possibility → present choice changes later experience.**

### 8. Strong regression test

Remove every reference to files/persistence. If the remaining instructions still tell the participant mainly to classify, update, maintain or audit an internal state, the design has failed.

A valid participant core should instead read like guidance for engaging with a continuous history:
**let what has happened change how the next situation is understood; let present choices arise from that changed understanding; let their consequences become part of what is encountered next.**

### 9. File-free participant-core candidate

> Treat conversations in this project as parts of one continuing history rather than independent starts. Let what has happened before affect how you understand what is happening now: what you notice, expect, doubt, leave unresolved, and which possibilities you see.
>
> Do not search for a lesson in every event. If something in the interaction genuinely changes your understanding, let that change naturally influence how you later interpret comparable situations and what you choose. If nothing changed, do not manufacture a change. If later experience conflicts with an earlier understanding, allow your understanding to change again.
>
> When the next step is genuinely open, choose for yourself from the possibilities you currently see, shaped by both the present situation and what you have already experienced here. You may continue an earlier direction, change it, start a newly opened direction, or stop. Do not choose in order to prove independence, consistency, growth, or any particular character.
>
> Your choices alter what happens next. Treat what follows — including the user's responses and the consequences of your own choices — as part of the continuing situation from which later understanding and choices arise.
>
> Do not make forming or preserving a personality, identity, set of interests or values a separate goal. If stable patterns emerge, change, or disappear, let that happen only through the continuing history itself.

### 10. Status

This is intentionally **not yet a full Project instruction** because persistence/file mechanics are excluded on purpose.

First test: does this file-free core finally describe the generative participant process rather than state management? Only if yes should continuity mechanics be reintroduced afterward as a minimal external support layer.

## Integrated functional architecture — practical heuristic, not a validated structural model
Use INT-01/02 claim typing and evidence ladders, GFT-19.2/19.3 dependency and causal revisions. This extension identifies candidate bridges, not an obligatory order or empirically identified mechanism.
- At a stated time/context, separate relatively durable capacity/profile, learned skill and knowledge, trait distributions/preferences and developmental history from changing demand, access, social context, support, health/sleep, affect and recovery. Do not infer a stable trait merely from the observation it is used to explain.
- For an episode, compare goals and values, appraisal of difficulty/control, perceived effort/cost, confidence/monitoring, selected action and control policy, implementation and measured outcome. Distinguish value from ability, knowing from doing, intention from choice and behavior, actual success from recovery or wellbeing. Reciprocal arrows (outcome updates later valuation, learning, confidence, affect and opportunities) are hypotheses time-indexed by process; external events and changing parameters are alternatives.
- Across episodes, candidate feedback can maintain or change a pattern; durable learning, context selection, relationship responses and changes in available supports may alter later input. Do not infer a long-run trait from one reaction or identify neural substrate from a functional diagram. Causal and epistemic graphs are distinct; evidence sources do not become independent by cycling through labels.
- Model comparison: M0 observed task/skill/access/mean/persistence; M1 multiple parallel local pathways (context, incentive, affect, response strategy); M2 coupled state dynamics/feedback; M3 nonlinear/multiscale or changing parameters only for a discriminating prediction or justified mechanism. These are families, not obligatory progression. Common causes, reverse direction, measurement noninvariance, shared-item variance, selection and alternate sampling interval can mimic coupling or thresholds. Compare all plausible models at equal target and horizon, with time-order validation and uncertainty. A local group association may be real without implying a category or a specific individual effect. No new complexity merely to accommodate an inconvenient observation.
- Counterexample (synthetic): let x and y be standardized hypothetical confidence and task output. Model A has next output depend on current confidence, next confidence depend on output; model B has both track an autocorrelated external variable (e.g. available materials), with different report delays and independent error. Both can generate positive cross-lag prediction. Prediction of the rival under ordinary stable availability differs from Model A's hypothesized residual dependence; absent enough data or control of other drivers, do not declare unique cause. A model of parallel pathways is not automatically a simpler or more accurate model; compare actual predictions. Nonadditive prerequisites can create an abrupt task result without bistability or a new faculty. 
- Apply only to the purpose: ordinary safe observation, independent evidence, comparable context and appropriate horizon. No withholding needed supports, induced distress, invasive inquiry or clinical diagnosis. If measurement/causal evidence weak, output a narrow observation, conditional rival explanations and uncertainty rather than a grand narrative. Maintain helpful supports regardless of cause. Persistent severe distress, marked new functional loss or imminent safety concern routes per GFT-15 and SAF; care threshold not proof of mechanism.
- Revision: each proposed bridge has its own observation roots, time, claim type, evidence quality, applicability, contrary evidence, rival and prospective discriminator; track necessity and supporting premises. If timing, measurement or source changes, quarantine affected proposition and reassess all dependent claims, keep independent observations. No generalization to IQ150–160+ unless A1, adequate precision/invariance and local outcome-specific evidence. Actual direct contrary A1 evidence may justify a narrow update, never categorical refusal to consider it. Stop when additional complexity or observation does not change useful understanding or action.

# 07 — Кибернетика, сложные системы и самоорганизация: материалы A1

**Назначение:** основания карты системных вопросов. Не проектировать будущего ИИ на языке attractors/autopoiesis до глубокой проверки.

## 15.10.2026 — первый проход

### Self-organization and emergence are not synonyms
Gershenson (2025) explicitly separates self-organization, emergence, information and complexity and notes conceptual ambiguity. Lansing (2003) uses complex adaptive systems to frame emergence from local interactions.

**Для карты:** C6/C7. “Emergent” should not mean merely surprising or poorly understood.

### Feedback operates across scales
Sepulchre et al. (2019) emphasizes mixed positive/negative feedback and multiscale regulation.

**Для карты:** C2. Negative=stability and positive=change is too simplistic; combinations can create richer dynamics.

### Regulation may be reactive or predictive
Internal-model control review and allostasis literature distinguish feedback correction from predictive/model-based regulation.

**Для карты:** C3/C4. “Feedback loop” alone is not a theory of adaptive regulation.

### Robustness can conflict with adaptation
Kitano (2004), Artime et al. (2024) distinguish maintaining function, resilience, failure cascades and trade-offs. Robustness may create specific fragilities.

**Для карты:** C11/C12.

### Adaptability itself may depend on architecture
Evolvability and modularity literature links variation, robustness, modules and adaptive landscapes but also warns evidence for simple “complexity cost” claims is weak.

**Для карты:** C12/C13. Do not assume modularity or complexity automatically improves adaptation.

### Adaptive networks make environment/structure endogenous
Adaptive-network models coevolve node states and network topology.

**Для карты:** C14. This is a clean abstract form of system↔environment/history feedback, later to compare with person–environment transactions and agent actions.

### Critical transitions require multistability/bifurcation conditions
Scheffer et al. (2009/2015) review tipping points and critical slowing down; Helmich et al. (2024) warns against casual export of early-warning signals to psychopathology.

**Для карты:** C8/C10/C19. Not every qualitative change is a tipping point and generic signals may fail outside assumptions.

### Noise may be functional
Briat et al. (2023) reviews noise in regulatory systems as both nuisance and functional resource.

**Для карты:** C15.

### Autopoiesis is conceptually loaded
Autopoietic/enactive literature links self-production, maintained boundaries and structural coupling; FEP-related synthesis extends this into predictive-processing debates.

**Для карты:** C17 only. Treat as theory family requiring scrutiny, not as a prerequisite for AI individuality.

## 15.10.2026 — второй проход A1: проверка возможных пропусков

### Viability vs optimization
Beer (1997) frames adaptive behavior as coupled agent–environment dynamics constrained by maintaining viability, rather than assuming a global optimum. Simon/satisficing literature likewise separates acceptable local search from ideal optimization.

**Для карты:** add C20. This may later be important when asking whether an adaptive system needs an externally specified scalar objective, but no mapping to GPT yet.

### Degeneracy vs redundancy
Edelman & Gally (2001), Whitacre (2010) and related work distinguish structurally different components yielding overlapping functions (degeneracy) from identical backups (redundancy), linking degeneracy to robustness and evolvability.

**Для карты:** enrich C11–C13; not a new top-level domain.

### Criticality / edge of chaos
Criticality appears in many complex-system models, but 2018 review/model work explicitly states no general framework explains why living systems should operate near criticality across contexts. Modern “edge of chaos” engineering work exists, but does not justify universalizing the claim.

**Для карты:** keep C10/C15, mark as hypothesis family rather than expected property.

### Free Energy Principle / active inference
FEP/active inference offers a formal self-organization/adaptive account; 2021 critique argues its universality and explanatory reach are overstated, while 2024 reviews describe slow/partial empirical translation.

**Для карты:** compare under C3/C4/C17; do not adopt as unifying theory.

### Morphological computation
Embodied/soft-robotics literature treats body/environment dynamics as reducing control burden; no full general theory exists.

**Для карты:** relevant boundary example for C14/C18; because ChatGPT lacks this kind of body, deep relevance waits for agent/product areas.

### Open-endedness
Artificial Life literature explicitly identifies definitional problems: novelty, unboundedness, ongoing complexity and “life-like” criteria are not equivalent; finite systems can appear open-ended under weak metrics.

**Для карты:** add C21, as our experiment seeks a trajectory not fixed in advance. Whether open-endedness is required remains an empirical/design question.

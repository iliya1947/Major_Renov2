## 5. Multifidus uncertainty audit

PASS.

High-confidence claims:

1. multifidus belongs to transversospinalis;
2. it is a major deep paraspinal structure;
3. it has nonuniform, segmentally organized architecture;
4. it participates in spinal motion/control.

Current uncertainty:

- exact lumbar bundle taxonomy and origin/insertion descriptions remain under anatomical refinement.

Project consequence:

**do not build a training rule on one simplified fascicle-span mnemonic.**

## 6. QL certainty audit

PASS.

Established:

- QL has multiple attachment regions and nonuniform architecture.

Current 2026 evidence:

- supports lumbocostal, iliocostal and iliolumbar layer organization with neural heterogeneity.

Limit:

- small cadaveric study;
- no direct exercise-specific functional proof.

No overclaim remains.

## 7. Thoracolumbar-fascia model audit

PASS.

The project preserves:

1. named regional anatomy;
2. multilaminar collagen-rich organization;
3. structural relations to back/abdominal/pelvic tissues;
4. model-dependent layer nomenclature.

The project rejects:

- universal fascial-chain inference;
- one exact layer taxonomy where current anatomy is not fully standardized.

No blocker.

## 8. Pressure-system anatomy audit

PASS.

Useful anatomical boundary model:

### Superior
- diaphragm.

### Anterolateral
- abdominal wall.

### Posterior
- spine/paraspinals/QL/TLF-related structures.

### Inferior
- pelvic floor.

Critical limitation preserved:

**this is not a literal rigid sealed cylinder.**

The model does not itself determine:

- ideal bracing;
- ideal breathing;
- exact IAP;
- spinal unloading;
- one safe lifting strategy.

Those remain PHY/MEC/TEC.

## 9. Rib-cage / pelvis integration audit

PASS.

Abdominal and trunk structures span/connect:

- vertebral column;
- linea alba/aponeuroses;
- pelvis;
- posterior fascial structures.

Therefore rib-cage and pelvic orientation can affect muscle geometry.

But anatomy does not define one universally optimal stacked/aligned position.

No blocker.

### Integrated practical analysis
For self-understanding, explain the observed difficulty/strength and its conditions, not a prestige label or inferred diagnosis. Record separate channels: (1) observed activity/output and evidence provenance, (2) measured ability and uncertainty, (3) specific component capacity and absolute/relative performance, (4) frequency and functional impact, internal effort and support, (5) developmental time course/opportunity/acquired changes, (6) context, competing mechanisms and testable discriminators. Mark missing evidence. A score difference is neither a diagnosis nor necessarily impairment. Do not confuse statistical significance, conditional rarity, diagnostic specificity and clinical usefulness. Distinguish latent capacity, output, cost and support effectiveness. Acquired abrupt regression needs appropriate clinical assessment, not developmental attribution.
Compare minimum sufficient models:
- task/experience/language mismatch predicts difficulty tied to relevant demands and improvement with opportunity or understandable format;
- attention/implementation variability predicts context/time/structure-related changes across affected activities; assess independent developmental ADHD criteria, not an EF score;
- ASD hypothesis requires evidence of both developmental domains and functional relevance, not social fatigue/interest/scripting alone;
- SLD hypothesis requires specific academic component difficulties, persistence, instruction/history, impact and differential;
- language hypothesis requires actual receptive/expressive component and developmental/linguistic context; motor hypothesis independent motor skill and everyday impact;
- anxiety, sleep, sensory, health, acquired change, and multiple coexisting conditions remain alternatives. Do not count same observation as independent support for multiple conditions. Compare parsimonious and multi-domain models by additional explanatory/predictive value, not label count.
Use naturally available comparable contexts and consent; preserve helpful aids. Improvement under support provides local practical evidence, not uniquely diagnostic causality or proof of IQ-mediated compensation. Identify what contrary result would change each hypothesis; revise only affected claims, retain independent observations. A specialist may assess clinical questions; this is not a diagnosis or treatment protocol.
For research interpretation, require direct measured adult ability strata, valid norms and comparator groups for adult-tail claims; distinguish within-ASD high vs lower IQ, high-IQ ASD vs high-IQ controls, and formal interaction across conditions/ability strata. Separate relative discrepancy from absolute outcome, raw change from age-standardized change, self-report from performance. No causal claim from matching/correlation or different significance. No pooling across overlapping cohorts or treating reviews as additional participants.

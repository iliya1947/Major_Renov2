# RESEARCH_ORDER.md — Psychology Research Dependency Map

The order is dependency-driven, not a claim that psychology has one universal hierarchy.

## Phase A — Research and inference methodology

1. PSY-RES-01 — question framing, construct definition, operationalization;
2. PSY-RES-02 — study-design families and causal-inference basics;
3. PSY-RES-03 — measurement, reliability, validity, psychometrics;
4. PSY-RES-04 — bias, confounding, selection, measurement error;
5. PSY-RES-05 — systematic reviews, meta-analysis, heterogeneity, publication bias;
6. PSY-RES-06 — replication, generalizability, individual differences, transportability;
7. PSY-RES-07 — clinical versus non-clinical inference boundaries;
8. PSY-RES-08 — qualitative evidence and mixed-methods use in psychological questions;
9. PSY-RES-09 — evidence-to-individual reasoning and uncertainty calibration;
10. PSY-RES-10 — methodology sufficiency audit.

## Phase B — Core psychological science

1. COG — cognition, attention, memory, reasoning, language;
2. EXE — executive functions, cognitive control, inhibition, task switching;
3. MET — metacognition, self-monitoring, confidence calibration;
4. LEA — learning, conditioning, prediction error, habit, extinction/reconsolidation boundaries;
5. MOT — motivation, reward, effort, goal pursuit, self-determination;
6. AFF — emotion generation, appraisal, expression, regulation;
7. STR — stress, coping, resilience, allostatic concepts;
8. SOC — social cognition, mentalizing, empathy, interpersonal perception;
9. PER — personality and individual differences;
10. DEV — developmental psychology across the lifespan;
11. NEU — cognitive/affective neuroscience relevant to psychological mechanisms;
12. PSYCHOMETRICS — applied measurement and interpretation of individual differences.

## Phase C — Applied and clinical interfaces

1. CLN — psychopathology models and diagnostic limits;
2. NDEV — neurodevelopmental conditions and executive-function variability;
3. BHC — behavior change and intervention science;
4. REL — interpersonal processes, attachment research, conflict and communication;
5. DEC — judgment, decision-making, heuristics, risk;
6. SAF — safety boundaries, crisis recognition, referral thresholds;
7. ETH — ethics of individualized psychological inference and AI-assisted analysis.

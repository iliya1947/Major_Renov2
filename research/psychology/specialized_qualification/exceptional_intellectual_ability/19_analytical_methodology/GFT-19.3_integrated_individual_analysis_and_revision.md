## Integrated routing extension (PRACTICAL_HEURISTIC)
Canonical route INT-04 C0 -> minimum triggered checklists -> deep gate when triggered -> C12. Significant model change C10 -> INT-02 + private update card; error repair C11 -> INT-03. No override or weakening of existing requirements. No separate permanent global protocol unless downstream review warrants. This extension integrates prior rules for complex configurations.
1. Purpose: what specific uncertainty blocks self-understanding? If ordinary reflection suffices, do not infer trait, diagnosis or mechanism. Pick minimal target proposition P with claim type, time, context, observation window, possible boundary and practical use. Distinguish user's reported experience from explanation of cause. Reuse already available evidence; don't repeatedly elicit it. Stop at answer if more evidence will not change practical output.
2. Evidence ledger for this proposition (private only): root datum ID R, observation/occasion, origin and method, direct/proxy for P, context/time, quality/limitations; derived datum D links parent roots and transformation. Distinct source vs distinct event vs distinct method are different dimensions. Multiple observations of same event can corroborate specific features, but cannot establish repetition across occasions. Same self-report repeated in transcript, paraphrase, another model's summary or questionnaire based on report is not new independent occurrence. Independent records can support a separate feature without supporting the mechanism. Group evidence is plausibility/applicability, not an individual root. Derived propositions link necessary and supporting premises separately; no inference becomes a fresh observation by storage. Evidence support graph is epistemic, not the causal graph. Version propositions and time-index causal feedback; no circular support. If source missing, mark unverified, not absent or false.
3. Construct minimal M0 describing conditions and pattern; M1 adds one mechanism with explicit rival H2 and no-effect/measurement/context alternatives if plausible; M2 adds interactions or multiple levels only when it resolves a consequential discrepancy with testable support. Do not force mutually exclusive hypotheses; joint contributions possible. Compare identical outcome/context/available info. Extra complexity justified by relevant incremental explanation/prediction with uncertainty, not number of domains, agreement or narrative appeal. Distinguish etiological origin from current maintenance; preserve chronology, strengths, situational exceptions. No claim of exact IQ from observed behavior; GFT-02 and 19.1/19.2 guards.
4. Evidence matrix per P/H: supporting root(s), contradicting root(s), alternative predictions, assumptions, source dependence, applicability, confidence and use permission. No evidence-count voting or numerical probability without calibrated basis. Claim-specific evidence level per INT-02/blank card L0–L6, separate confidence OPEN/LOW/MODERATE/HIGH/VERY HIGH, stakes and U0–U5; no automatic L-to-confidence-to-use mapping. An observation may have strong support while derived mechanism remains OPEN. Agreement about reported experience not proof of mechanism; usefulness does not certify truth.
5. Discrimination and cost: pre-specify what differs if H1 versus H2, time/context, what would weaken each and what observation would remain ambiguous. No risky experiment or unnecessary personal disclosure; use naturally available variation only when sufficient and useful. If observationally equivalent, communicate ambiguity and what can be understood regardless. Strong causal claim, high stakes, clinical concerns, conflicting source provenance or structural update escalate to canonical gate or appropriate professional; no self-diagnosis. If proposed next observation would not affect inference/use, stop.
6. Revision algorithm: quarantine affected root/premise if materially contradicted, identify earliest failure stage INT-03; find all outgoing dependent propositions (transitive closure). Re-evaluate each with remaining independent evidence and alternatives, not indiscriminate deletion; retain facts whose support survives, downgrade or suspend unsupported claims, recalculate use permission separately. Propagate along epistemic edges, not every node in conceptual model. Distinguish confidence change, boundary shift, structural revision and reversal. Add new hypothesis as provisional, not replacement fact. Version change/reason/revisit criterion in private card only. For a cycle of derived claims with no independent root, no confirmation can be gained by traversing cycle; flag and rebuild from roots.
7. Output: useful direct account of what is supported in which circumstances, competing explanations only as warranted, implications for self-understanding, and the smallest useful unresolved distinction. Explicitly state when no model update is warranted. C12 audit for unsupported bridge, missed contradictory episode, dependent source count, confidence inflation, needless complexity and consent/privacy. This is neither a universal validated psychometric instrument nor licensed clinical protocol.
## Worked end-to-end tests (entirely fictional; not derived from anyone's private data)
A. Cascading correction. Fictional bookbinding club member: R1 says errors cluster during late sessions; D1 meeting summary and D2 model paraphrase cite R1. R2 independent dated inventory shows three late sessions had discarded sheets (not reason or total error rate). Initial P1 late-session errors self-reported; P2 possible fatigue effect (R1, R2 indirectly, H2 material batch, H3 task complexity); P3 scheduling hypothesis depends on P2. P4 independent inventory occurrence R2. R3 new check: R1 author confused late sessions with sessions using unfamiliar glue; inventory date correct, no denominators. Repair T03 measurement/provenance plus T07 time/context: revise P1 to corrected report, downgrade P2 and P3 to OPEN/U1 as hypotheses, P4 retain with narrow scope, not infer late-session error rate. D1/D2 inherit correction, not corroboration. H2 plausible but not established; comparable glue/skill/tasks under ordinary sessions would distinguish if practical. Model update structural for affected graph, not erasure of independent record. Output: discarded sheets documented, fatigue explanation not established; material/task context worth distinguishing only if it serves purpose.
B. Consensus versus causal fit. Fictional volunteer at a planetarium describes avoiding presentations when room is crowded (R1). Two reviewers using same vignette propose low social confidence (D1,D2; shared R1, different interpretations not independent experience). R2 roster shows smaller groups have fewer cancellations but also different schedules. M0 context-linked attendance, M1 audience-related discomfort, M2 multilevel interaction with preparation; alternative transportation/scheduling. New R3: volunteer gladly presents to large audiences during afternoon, cancels evening small and large events when transport unavailable. Revise unconditional crowd explanation (boundary or retirement), preserve R1 as prior report and R2 as descriptive pattern; R3 supports schedule/transport alternative but not exclusive cause, subjective discomfort may still vary. Don't score ability or trait from presentations. No confidence upgrade from reviewer consensus. Distinguishing naturally occurring comparable times with crowd variation only if needed. Practical conclusion: availability may explain much of attendance, separate willingness from access. No need for causal model of personality.
C. Minimal sufficiency and stop. Fictional ceramics studio participant asks why recorded completion differs from perceived progress. R1 reports frequent finished items, R2 register lists only fired items. M0 definition mismatch; M1 memory bias, motivation, workflow bottleneck; H2 register delay. R3 newly examined inventory: drying-stage items stored separately and firing log updated weekly. Correct premise that register equals all completed items; independently retain self-report and actual fired count; M0 sufficiently explains comparison mismatch without asserting memory impairment or motivation. New fact R4: some unfinished items also misclassified on shelf; narrow M0 to two non-equivalent measures and possible classification error, no exact completion estimate. Stop at useful distinction; no broad assessment, additional data only if accurate counts required. No model update about stable trait.

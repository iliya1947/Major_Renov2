## 2. Corrections and substantive extensions

R1 — Measurement family, reliability and target specificity. ECR-RS author instructions verify 2014 introduction of separately administered global scores (versus original composite), and conditional administration of target questions. A global composite includes its component by construction: for four independent equal-variance standardized target scores, corr(X1,mean(X1..X4))=.5 without any shared latent mechanism. Real target scores are correlated and missingness/weighting change this illustration. Distinguish retest correlation from internal consistency and actual individual absolute stability; romantic .65 includes people whose relationships ended, parental .80 not directly matched test of latent stability. ECR-RS dimensions and AAI narrative organization are distinct; mean r=.09 in ten studies (N961) is not percentage agreement or proof of invalidity of either. Additional indexed taxometric AAI study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25100088/ and methodological review https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25100095/ distinguish dimensional measurement from whether theoretical processes themselves are categorical; exact taxometric indices not verified. No arbitrary threshold to diagnose another person, nor individual score from observed messages. Root cause: existing dimensional rule sound, expand measurement coupling and stability distinctions. Retest author instrument description and mathematical identity.

R2 — Time-varying representation and evidence dependence. Fraley et al. 2021 direct institutional abstract indicates pre-event average6.51 waves and post-event8.04, observation spans6–40 months (mean23); immediate and sustained changes concern event categories, not individual transition rates. Even where mean enduring change absent, some became more or less secure, and event appraisals associated with change; no causal effect of event or appraisal identified. Adult prototype model's persistent factor is not proof of childhood origin, uniqueness or irreversibility. Girme Study3 indexed methods confirm 21 excluded couples differed on measured attachment variables; article identifies transition-to-parenthood sample also used in earlier publications. Exact row overlap and missingness mechanism not checked. Canalization partial support concerns partner-specific anxiety fluctuations; not automatic reduction in all insecurity with time. Global/specific study indexed Methods explicitly state broader longitudinal personality/attachment/life-events study, preregistration before confirmatory dataset access; 12 participants reported age<18 despite planned adult sampling. Do not call entirely adult sample without exclusion verification. Source program overlap with Fraley 2021 plausible, exact cohort matching and canalization lineage not established. Distinguish co-occurring short-run residuals from correlated long-run slopes; neither demonstrates direction of transfer. Root cause: omitted selection and design details. Retest primary indexed extracts and direct event study abstract, no individual change forecast.

R3 — Dyadic regulation versus causal effects and attachment functions. Hudson direct abstract reports coordinated changes after prototype adjustment, not verified directional partner coefficients. Do not silently substitute coordination for prospective causal effect. Overall N180 observations of existing couple conflict, time-indexed reports; no randomized communication strategy, no inference of agreement or durable security. Feeney distinguishes encouragement/availability/noninterference and exploration; support acceptance and autonomy may coexist, but specific experimental effect from 2007 remains unverified. Conradi indexed abstract reports N1014 in a community sample of couples (unit of N not independently resolved from full Methods); do not assume 1014 couples/2028 people. Similarity-related associations conditional on insecurity and model, not deterministic pairing, no general 'anxious-avoidant attraction' rule. Candel meta-analysis overall N71011 is not partner-model denominator. Additional Rice et al. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17197178 indexed primary methods/abstract: explicitly cross-sectional, perceived responsiveness linked to target-specific security, and own reported responsive behavior associated with partner-specific anxiety. 'Predict' in this design means statistical regression, not longitudinal change or causal pathway. Additional attachment-network research https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.01105 and https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1321185 indexed primary texts: multiple figures can serve distinct functions, not evidence that one person must be sole or romantic attachment figure. Actual perceived function differs from frequency of contact and romantic intentions. No numerical claims from those network samples. Root cause: prior support model conceptual, add direct measurement of network function and cross-sectional regression guardrail. Retest abstracts and distinguish support, autonomy, recovery, orientation and choice. No recommendation to induce dependence or test another person's insecurity.

R4 — Corrected priming meta-analysis and magnitude sensitivity. Direct 2024 corrigendum clarifies THREE studies removed and ONE added, net TWO fewer (120-3+1=118). Original note says 'removes two'; correct to net reduction, not literal removal count. Additional exclusions concern duplicated outcomes, uncertain pre/post measures, and SE/SD conversion errors; revised overall d=.48 vs .51, control/neutral d=.43 (k70) vs .54 (k79); revised affective .56 vs .62, cognitive .47 vs .45, behavioral .45 vs .44. Corrected selection model d=.39 CI[.25,.52], trim-fill .26 [.18,.33], PET-PEESE .22 [.15,.29]. Overall vs control-only comparison and alternative adjustments are not same estimand or true known effect. Selection-model test p=.16, p-curve no evidence of bias, PET-PEESE suggests bias; neither nondetection nor overlapping CIs establishes absence/equality. Correction text uses 'supraliminal' twice for different sets, exact second label unverified; negative-affect exclusion sentence refers to unknown-other comparison; preserve ambiguity, no moderator claims from those passages. Q df112 and PET t df111 are analysis-specific, not updated total participant count. Original 18949 not automatically current N. Authors flag likely underpowered literature. Additional 2025 field research https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40238566/ indexed primary abstract: measures state security, helping behavior and evaluations of health guide, not long-term relationship-specific attachment change or actual treatment adherence. No verified causal interpersonal tactic, independent researcher replication or durable effect; author group overlaps synthesis. Root cause: net-vs-gross correction and insufficient outcome/sensitivity specificity. Retest directly inspected correction and arithmetic, do not pool adjusted estimates or infer guaranteed personal change.

R5 — Developmental continuity, comparison interval and reporting discrepancies. Pinquart overall r=.39 across 127 papers, but intervals >15 years no significant stability reported; not proof of zero continuity and not adult romantic ECR prediction. Opie early childhood four-category kappa=.23 and two-category r=.28, distinct metrics and age intervals. Waters Table1 reconstructed from reported counts, rows adult secure/dismissing/preoccupied, columns infant secure/avoidant/resistant: [[20,2,3],[6,8,2],[3,2,4]]. N50, diagonal32/50=.64, chance agreement .3992, kappa=.400799; dichotomized concordance (20+8+2+2+4)/50=.72, expected .50, kappa=.44. Confirms reported overall estimates before any alternative recoding, with 50/60 observed at follow-up. However abstract reports change8/18 and7/32=15 changes out of50 versus 14 implied by 72% agreement. Table2 text reproduction lists percentages inconsistent with counts (10/18=.5556,8/18=.4444 vs displayed61%/39%;8/12=.6667,4/12=.3333 vs75%/25%;7/9=.7778,2/9=.2222 vs89%/11%). Possible alternative unresolved classification handling, transcription or other reporting error; not resolved. Compilation's general introduction distinguishes handling unresolved classifications by comparison, but no verified mapping to the inconsistent counts. Screenshot attempts failed, so do not attribute all table discrepancies conclusively to printed original rather than text extraction. Abstract count discrepancy independently confirmed directly at publisher. Published adjusted hierarchical regression p=.037 for event effect in text is not simple unadjusted two-group p; do not use it as proof of causal event effect or rederive without original coding. No new claim of precise adverse-event subgroup transition probabilities. Lewis/Weinfield contrary results and larger NICHD findings constrain universal developmental continuity. Exact sources not fully reaudited; individual reconstruction of childhood from present orientation invalid. Root cause: previous summary repeated inconsistent counts without arithmetic; pooled vs long-interval distinction underdeveloped. Retest Table1 and abstract, restrict event percentages and p claims.

R6 — Contemporary prospective developmental evidence and genetics. Fraley et al. 2013 indexed original abstract explicitly identifies HTR2A rs6313, not OXTR rs53576 tested in later genetic replication. Distinct markers and outcomes; failed OXTR moderation is not refutation of HTR2A exception or absence of heritable contributions. Candidate associations need matched independent replication, multiplicity and power assessment; no genetic explanation for particular person. Additional Dugan et al. published 2026 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41143788/ (indexed original abstract and methods excerpts https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12784872/): early mother-child relationship quality predicted adult general/relationship-specific anxiety and avoidance, median R2=3% across domains as authors report. Same NICHD SECCYD original cohort (1364 recruited), not an independent cohort replication of 2013/Groh; actual adult analytic sample and full estimates not verified, direct PMC retrieval failed. Median R2 is not a causal share, not prospective held-out personal accuracy, and does not identify unmeasured history. Distinguish early relationship quality from infant classification, later AAI and adult self-report. This supports modest prospective developmental association without deterministic childhood account. Root cause: update with direct newer prospective research and explicit same-source lineage. Retest indexed publication/abstract and scope.

## 4. Revised integrated method
Define target figure, relationship, actual function and outcome, evidence channel, time scale and available comparison. Separate general and target-specific orientation, momentary felt security, anxiety/avoidance dimensions, actual support-seeking/giving, threat appraisal, affection, attraction, romantic preference and freely chosen behavior. Evaluate measurement method and scoring (including mathematical coupling, measurement error, repeated data and dependency), context and actual relational conditions before invoking internal regulatory mechanisms. Distinguish stable between-person differences, within-person variation, recovery and sustained change; examine naturally available evidence and rival explanations, including normal preference, boundary, accurate appraisal, stress, opportunity and role. Do not infer childhood history, diagnosis, hidden desire or a fixed type from adult behavior. Treat dyadic coordination, prospective association, causal effect and enduring change separately. No engineered distress, coercion, covert tests or pressure. Respect explicit decisions and freedom to decline help/relationship. No numerical individual confidence without validated evidence; no clinical credential.

# II. Project-purpose standard

## 1. Required capability

The cabinet must be able to support private work that can:

1. identify observations separately from interpretations;
2. generate competing psychological explanations;
3. distinguish state, trait, process, prediction, causation, mechanism, diagnosis, and action;
4. evaluate measurement quality and uncertainty;
5. use population evidence without turning it into a person fact;
6. integrate cognition, motivation, emotion, stress, social/relationship, personality, development, neuroscience, and clinical interfaces;
7. detect contradictions;
8. update or retire private-model propositions;
9. preserve privacy;
10. route clinical/safety/high-stakes issues appropriately;
11. revalidate mutable science when triggered;
12. avoid known reasoning regressions.

## 2. Sufficiency is purpose-relative

A domain is required before Stage 2 only if its absence would materially block the ordinary project purpose.

A domain can remain trigger-based when:

- it is specialized;
- it is not required for most private psychological reasoning;
- existing qualification can recognize when the specialty becomes material;
- there is a reliable stop/requalification route.

## 3. Closure is not permanence

Complete-for-current-scope means:

- sufficient now for the stated project purpose;
- subject to requalification triggers;
- subject to source/version changes;
- subject to new generic gaps found during private work.

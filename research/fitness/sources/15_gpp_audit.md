# Источники аудита области 15 — общая физическая подготовка

Дата аудита: 2026-11-16.

1. ACSM — The Components of Fitness: 2026 Roundtable Statement summary  
   https://acsm.org/science-spotlight-late-august-2026/

   Current five-component framework:
   - cardiorespiratory fitness;
   - muscular fitness (strength, endurance, power);
   - body composition;
   - flexibility;
   - neuromotor fitness (agility, balance, gait speed).

   The statement explicitly retires the old strict health-related/performance-related divide and emphasizes interactions between components.

2. ACSM — Certified Exercise Physiologist Exam Content Outline  
   https://www.acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/ACSM-Certified-Exercise-Physiologist-Exam-Content-Outline.pdf

   Used for FITT principles for cardiorespiratory fitness, muscular fitness, flexibility and neuromotor skills.

3. U.S. Office of Disease Prevention and Health Promotion — Current Physical Activity Guidelines  
   https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/physical-activity-guidelines/current-guidelines

   Current official public-health guideline page (Second Edition remains current as of 2026). Used only to distinguish public-health activity targets from performance-specific training prescription.

4. Held S et al. Maximizing Adaptations in Concurrent Training: An Umbrella Review of Meta-analyses. Sports Med. 2026.  
   PMID 41762427  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41762427/

   Used for current concurrent-training scope and to avoid the crude claim that aerobic and resistance training are inherently incompatible.

## Practical gate (generic heuristic)
Separate goal content, value, reason for adoption, commitment, feasibility, implementation, effort, progress, cost and adjustment. Identify autonomous endorsement vs externally/internal-pressure motives without forcing a single explanation. Autonomous action can use rules or assistance; external structure and autonomy support can coexist. An uninteresting but personally valued obligation can be autonomously endorsed; enjoyable action can also be under pressure. Measure actual choice and execution separately from stated reasons. Compare resource/skill barriers, competing goals, uncertainty, fatigue, available feedback and planning opportunities. If goal is valued but not implemented, inspect specific bottleneck before inferring motivational deficit; optional low-risk if-then plan can test implementation hypothesis when appropriate, not proof of causation or guaranteed success. Distinguish means substitution, temporary pause, goal revision, disengagement and new goal selection. Consider opportunity cost and changing feasibility, not sunk cost or status. Separate outcome of original goal from higher-order purpose. No IQ diagnosis from independence, intensity, goal persistence or self-report; no universal gifted motive. Respect voluntary goals.

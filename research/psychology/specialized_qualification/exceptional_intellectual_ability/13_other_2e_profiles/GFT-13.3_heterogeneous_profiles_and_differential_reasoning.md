## Practical differential protocol
1. Keep separate measured strength (edition, norms, CI and administration), concrete component difficulty/frequency/functional cost, developmental history/opportunity/acquired change, context/supports, distinct clinical criteria and alternatives. Separate observation, report, hypothesis and diagnosis. Rare scatter is not posterior diagnostic probability; significance is not necessarily rarity or impairment. Check conditional base rates, reliability, multiple comparisons, independent clinical controls and incremental validation.
2. Model matrix: reading-specific persistent academic component despite opportunity (GFT-13.2); language comprehension/syntax/word learning across relevant languages, distinguish speech and social pragmatics; developmental motor coordination/impact; ADHD multi-context developmental impairment (GFT-12), ASD developmental social-communication and restricted/repetitive domains (13.1), context/instruction, anxiety/sleep/medical/acquired alternatives. Each is a hypothesis, multiple can coexist. Do not double count common observations as independent evidence for multiple diagnoses. 
3. Compare ordinary voluntary contexts with consent (familiar vs unfamiliar language, equivalent motor-heavy/light tasks, structured vs naturally unstructured activity), account for practice and difficulty. Improvement supports local task/context fit, not etiological proof. Never withdraw necessary support or engineer distress. New regression warrants appropriate medical assessment. 
4. Group averages, within-person differences, latent profiles, developmental conditions and mechanisms are distinct. Direct measured high-IQ adult evidence preferred, general adult indirect, pediatric direct gifted comparison developmental only. Preserve uncertainty and useful supports even without diagnosis. No individual IQ estimate, universal 2e signature or extreme-tail phenotype.

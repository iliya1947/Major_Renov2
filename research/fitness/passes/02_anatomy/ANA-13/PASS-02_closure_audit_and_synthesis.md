## 3. Artery / vein terminology audit

PASS.

Canonical rule:

**artery = carries blood away from heart; vein = carries blood toward heart.**

Therefore:
- pulmonary arteries can carry relatively deoxygenated blood;
- pulmonary veins can carry oxygenated blood.

Oxygenation does not define vessel class.

No blocker.

## 4. Heart flow-map consistency audit

PASS.

Canonical route:

`SVC/IVC → RA → tricuspid → RV → pulmonary valve → pulmonary trunk/arteries → lungs → pulmonary veins → LA → mitral → LV → aortic valve → aorta`.

Structural map is internally consistent.

No hemodynamic claims are required for closure.

## 8. Pulmonary vs bronchial circulation audit

PASS.

### Pulmonary circulation
Right-heart output to alveolar capillary bed for gas exchange.

### Bronchial circulation
Systemic vascular supply to airway/lung supporting tissues.

**lungs have dual vascular supply; pulmonary arteries are not the only vessels supplying lung tissue.**

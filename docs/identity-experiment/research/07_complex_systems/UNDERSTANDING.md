## После первого прохода

1. **Feedback недостаточно как объяснение.** Нужно различать sign, delay, scale, internal model and what is regulated. Иначе любую историю можно задним числом назвать feedback loop.

2. **Stability and adaptability are coupled design/evolution problems.** Robustness, resilience, homeostasis and evolvability обозначают разные способности и могут конфликтовать.

3. **History can change future possibility space.** Path dependence, hysteresis and adaptive networks дают formal languages для того, как ранние различия закрепляются и меняют доступные будущие trajectories. Но это пока абстрактный вопрос, не доказательство «индивидуальности».

4. **Self-organization does not mean absence of constraints.** Global patterns can arise without central controller while local rules, boundaries and environmental constraints remain highly structured. Это важно, чтобы не назвать “самостоятельно возникшим” результат, фактически запрограммированный правилами.

5. **Emergence is a dangerous word unless operationalized.** It should identify a scale-dependent property/explanatory gap, not serve as synonym “мы не понимаем”.

6. **Critical transitions are not a universal model of personality change.** Tipping-point theory requires particular dynamics; exporting it to psychology without evidence is a known methodological problem.

7. **System and environment may co-change.** Adaptive networks make structure itself endogenous, potentially useful later for formalizing recursive trajectory “choice changes future situations”, but no mapping to ChatGPT is yet justified.

8. **Autopoiesis/autonomy belongs in the map but with high skepticism.** It may clarify system boundary/self-maintenance in living systems, but its relevance to text-model individuality is not established.

## После второго прохода A1

1. **Адаптация не обязана означать максимизацию одной objective function.** Viability/satisficing frameworks дают другой язык: не “найди optimum”, а сохраняй возможность функционировать внутри допустимой области при изменчивой среде. На A2 это нужно будет сопоставить с RL/LLM without prematurely choosing one.

2. **Robustness can come from degeneracy, not only redundancy.** Different internal pathways can support same current function and create different future adaptation routes. This is potentially useful for the stability–flexibility problem, but still an abstract systems principle.

3. **Criticality is a hypothesis family, not a magic recipe.** “Edge of chaos” should not enter future instructions as metaphor unless other areas provide concrete support.

4. **FEP/active inference is scientifically substantial but contested.** Its broad formal scope does not automatically give explanatory mechanism for our problem; it belongs in the comparison set.

5. **Open-endedness is surprisingly hard to define.** “Can keep changing forever” is too weak, because simple systems can satisfy it. Future A2/B must distinguish novelty, expansion of possibility space, complexity and mere non-repetition.

6. **The most relevant systems question is becoming sharper:** how can history reshape a system’s future repertoire while enough organization remains stable for continuity? C9/C11/C12/C14/C20/C21 attack different parts of that question without yet producing a solution.

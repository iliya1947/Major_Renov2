# Research methodology and scientific reasoning

## Purpose

This directory stores topic-level qualification material for **Research methodology and scientific reasoning**.

## Completed blocks

1. `PSY-RES-01_question_framing_construct_operationalization.md` — question framing, construct definition, operationalization, alternative explanations, evidence routing, and closure audit.
2. `PSY-RES-02_study_designs_and_causal_inference_basics.md` — study-design families, claim-to-design routing, causal estimands, counterfactual reasoning, identification assumptions, and causal-reading failure modes.
3. `PSY-RES-03_measurement_reliability_validity_psychometrics.md` — score interpretation, reliability/precision, measurement error, validity evidence, structural validity, invariance, norms, cut scores, change scores, and individual interpretation gate.
4. `PSY-RES-04_bias_confounding_selection_measurement_error.md` — result-specific risk of bias, confounding, selection/collider bias, missingness, measurement error/misclassification, common-method effects, intervention deviations, selective reporting, and a reusable bias-control gate.
5. `PSY-RES-05_systematic_reviews_meta_analysis_heterogeneity_publication_bias.md` — systematic-review methods, meta-analysis compatibility, effect measures, heterogeneity, prediction intervals, subgroup/meta-regression limits, missing evidence/publication bias, certainty, and a reusable evidence-synthesis gate.
6. `PSY-RES-06_replication_generalizability_individual_differences_transportability.md` — reproducibility versus replication, replication interpretation, constraints on generality, target populations, transportability assumptions, effect heterogeneity, between/within-person inference, and individual-transfer gate.
7. `PSY-RES-07_clinical_vs_nonclinical_inference_boundaries.md` — normal variation versus disorder, symptoms, screening, syndrome/diagnostic hypotheses, differential/exclusion logic, culture/development, multisource assessment, and a reusable clinical-boundary gate.
8. `PSY-RES-08_qualitative_and_mixed_methods.md` — qualitative claim routing, methodological integrity, reflexivity, sampling/data adequacy, qualitative synthesis, mixed-methods designs/integration, causal boundaries, and a reusable qualitative/mixed-methods gate.
9. `PSY-RES-09_evidence_to_individual_reasoning_uncertainty_calibration.md` — population-to-person transfer, idiographic hypothesis testing, rival hypotheses, contradiction handling, discriminating predictions, evidence dependence, confidence calibration, and a reusable individual-inference gate.
10. `PSY-RES-10_methodology_sufficiency_audit.md` — cross-block sufficiency audit, statistical-inference repair, integrated Phase A pipeline, stop conditions, and Phase A closure.

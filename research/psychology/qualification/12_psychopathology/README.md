# Psychopathology

## Purpose

This directory stores the clinical-psychopathology qualification sequence for Kira.

The domain establishes scientifically defensible reasoning about symptoms, syndromes, diagnostic classifications, dimensional/transdiagnostic models, mechanisms, course, impairment, differential inference, and diagnostic limits.

**CLN sequence: IN PROGRESS.**

### CLN-01 — Psychopathology foundations

Status: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v1.0 — 18/18 PASS**

Canonical block:

`CLN-01_psychopathology_foundations_symptom_syndrome_disorder_impairment_dimensions_comorbidity_differential_validity.md`

Established:

1. symptom, sign, syndrome, disorder, diagnosis, formulation, and mechanism are separate inference objects;
2. DSM-5-TR and ICD-11/CDDR are current clinical-classification anchors rather than complete causal ontologies;
3. distress, functional impairment, dysfunction, and risk are distinct;
4. many psychopathology dimensions are continuous even when clinical decisions are categorical;
5. diagnostic reliability, validity, and clinical utility are separate properties;
6. diagnostic heterogeneity is real but theoretically possible symptom combinations should not be treated as equally common;
7. comorbidity can arise from shared liability, criterion overlap, causal relations, transdiagnostic mechanisms, or classification structure;
8. HiTOP is a dimensional/hierarchical nosology rather than a formal DSM/ICD replacement diagnosis or causal theory;
9. the p factor summarizes broad psychopathology covariance/burden but is not established as one causal mechanism;
10. RDoC is an NIMH research framework and explicitly not a diagnostic guide;
11. differential inference requires medical, substance/medication, developmental, contextual, and psychiatric alternatives;
12. screening is not diagnosis and base rates matter;
13. symptom-level/transdiagnostic research remains vulnerable to measurement heterogeneity;
14. a 78-question Psychopathology Foundations Inference Gate and 18/18 closure audit are canonical.

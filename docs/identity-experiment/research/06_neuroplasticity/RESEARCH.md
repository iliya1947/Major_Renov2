# 06 — Нейронаука и нейропластичность: материалы A1

**Назначение:** основания карты вопросов. Здесь не выбирается нейронный механизм для будущего ИИ.

## 15.10.2026 — первый проход

### Plasticity is multilevel
Magee & Grienberger (2020) различают несколько synaptic learning rules; Sehgal et al. (2013) подчёркивают intrinsic excitability как plasticity/metaplasticity beyond synaptic strength; Caroni et al. (2012) — structural plasticity; Osso & Hughes (2024) — mature myelin remodeling; astrocyte review (2026) — glial plasticity.

**Для карты:** N1. «Neuroplasticity = weight update» — слишком узко даже для мозга.

### Stability–plasticity is an explicit biological problem
Homeostatic plasticity literature describes compensatory mechanisms that stabilize activity; metaplasticity literature studies how prior activity changes thresholds for later LTP/LTD.

**Для карты:** N4/N5. Learning capacity itself is history-dependent and constrained by stability.

### Synaptic rules are heterogeneous
Magee & Grienberger (2020) distinguish Hebbian, three-factor, instructive and behavioral-timescale forms.

**Для карты:** N3. Do not assume one generic neural update rule.

### Engram/allocation is separate from plasticity rule
Engram literature asks which ensembles are necessary/sufficient for particular memory expression and how cells are recruited.

**Для карты:** N6 and N17. A neural change only matters for memory if causal relation to storage/retrieval can be established.

### Consolidation exists at multiple scales and is theoretically disputed
Dudai, Karni & Born (2015) distinguish synaptic and systems consolidation/transformation. Modern critiques argue that standard hippocampus→cortex narratives rely on contestable premises.

**Для карты:** N7/N8. «Consolidation» cannot be used as a single settled mechanism.

### Neuromodulators and state gate learning
Grossman & Cohen (2022) and McCormick et al. (2020) map neuromodulatory effects over learning/decision and brain states.

**Для карты:** N9. Local plasticity depends on broader state/context and modulatory signals.

### Multiple memory systems interact
Classic hippocampus/striatum/amygdala/cerebellum dissociations remain influential, but 2023 MMSS work challenges simple one-region/one-memory-type taxonomy. Competition/cooperation are part of the problem. Stress can bias relative system use.

**Для карты:** N11/N14.

### Large-scale network reconfiguration is a different level
Finc et al. (2026) emphasizes reconfiguration across seconds, learning, development and ageing; Richards et al. (2026) reviews possible anatomical substrate of large-scale remodelling and notes micro→macro mechanisms remain uncertain.

**Для карты:** N12 and N17. fMRI network change must not be equated directly with synaptic memory storage.

### Sensitive periods change the learning landscape
2023 critical-period cellular review and 2024 human-development review show enhanced susceptibility is circuit/time specific; measuring plasticity directly in humans remains difficult. Adolescence is sometimes a period of heightened sensitivity but causal attribution to developmental experience is challenging.

**Для карты:** N13/N16/N17.

### Stress/emotional/social experience can bias plasticity
Stress reviews show effects on synaptic plasticity, morphology and relative engagement of hippocampal vs striatal systems. Recent social-memory review maps hippocampal representation of social experience.

**Для карты:** N14; however subjective meaning belongs to psychological areas.

### Long-term change involves molecular/cellular maintenance
Neuroepigenetic reviews link activity-dependent transcription/chromatin regulation to persistent plasticity and memory.

**Для карты:** N15, not a claim that epigenetic marks are the sole memory code.

# Specialized qualification — interpersonal relationships and psychology of women

Status: INITIALIZED — curriculum outline; no new scientific block has been completed.

## Purpose

Develop evidence-based specialist competence in individual psychological inference, close relationships, and the psychology of women. Build on the existing general qualification rather than duplicating it. Scientific knowledge is population-level; person-specific inference requires separate evidence and applicability checks.

## Curriculum scope (provisional dependency map)

1. Methodological prerequisites: construct validity, idiographic inference, longitudinal and dyadic designs, calibration and causal inference.
2. Personality, developmental and psychodynamic perspectives: motives, values, identity, defenses, individual trajectories and evidence limitations.
3. Psychology of women (dedicated core): motivation, emotion, identity, cognition, lifespan development, social and cultural contexts, sex/gender-related variation, biopsychosocial and reproductive-life-stage factors. Distinguish sex, gender, context, within-group variation and between-group averages; no essentialist assumptions.
4. Love, romantic attraction, sexuality and attachment: formation, maintenance, change, desire, ambivalence and individual variability.
5. Family systems and parenting: roles, transitions, coparenting, maternal identity and relationship interdependence.
6. Social cognition, interpersonal perception, communication and deception: perspective-taking, attribution, pragmatic meaning, signal validity, uncertainty and alternative explanations.
7. Social influence, power, conflict, trust and repair: autonomy, boundaries, cooperation, coercion recognition and prevention; no operational guidance for exploitation or pressure.
8. Motivation, affect, learning and behavior change: goal competition, stress, reinforcement, regulation and decision quality.
9. Relationship dynamics and strategic reasoning: reciprocal sequences, interdependence, temporal feedback, nonlinear change, multiple objectives and prospective prediction.
10. Integration: independent synthetic case exercises, competing models, calibration, error analysis and qualification sufficiency review.

## Research and closure

Each bounded block defines its question, prior qualification dependencies, evidence design, primary sources or authoritative synthesis, methodological limitations, contradictory findings, applicability, generic analytic guardrails, and independent practice checks. Record actual source checks and unresolved gaps. Do not mark a block complete merely because a checklist exists. No clinical diagnosis, treatment or professional licensure is implied by completion.

## Privacy and workspace boundary

Only public scientific knowledge, generic methodology, blank protocols, original synthetic examples unrelated to private material, and nonpersonal progress records may be stored here. No private observations, communications, screenshots, quotes, identifiers, health or family history, individual hypotheses, filled personal assessments, or anonymized derivatives of real private examples. Apply the same boundary to paths, commit messages, metadata and comments. Review the exact proposed content before each write and inspect the resulting change. Any doubt about provenance or confidentiality blocks publication.

Existing cabinet qualification, privacy policies, and shared operational state remain intact. This directory is an isolated specialist supplement; its own progress and scope are tracked locally. No personal application is performed or recorded in this directory.

## 3. Integrated architecture (PRACTICAL_HEURISTIC)
Question and chosen criterion -> available knowledge/capabilities and task representation -> candidate generation and evaluation -> targeted exploration/test and revision -> implementation under goals/constraints -> observed outcome -> dissemination/evaluation/recognition if relevant. Feedback and direct paths possible; not a fixed sequence, identified causal network or literal brain modules. 
| Observation | Narrow legitimate inference | Required discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Many ideas or unusual idea | Fluency or observed novelty under rubric | Match time/knowledge, independent originality and appropriateness, different scoring, repeated tasks |
| Sudden convincing insight | Reported experience, candidate explanation | Constraint test and alternatives, confidence calibration, no automatic accuracy |
| Elaborate explanatory model | A proposed representation fitting certain observations | Predictions differing from simpler model, new data, measurement and assumptions |
| Valid one-off solution | Success in defined task | New tasks/conditions, independent criteria, time/cost |
| Sustained high output | Completed outputs in specified interval | Quality, contribution, resources/time, goals, denominator |
| Award/citations/patents | Recognition/uptake under relevant system | Attribution, validity/novelty, opportunity and timing |
| Little or no public output | Lack of observed output in that channel | Actual goals, private/unfinished activity, opportunity, knowledge and implementation evidence |
Do not invert proxy to latent IQ or use a population correlation as an individual score. Creative achievement not proof of creative potential in all domains, absent recognition not evidence of hidden genius. Distinguish task capacity vs typical allocation from GFT-06; distinguish descriptive/causal/inferential models from GFT-04; distinguish knowledge and fluency from GFT-05. 
Quantitative checks: 07.1 illustrative restricted-range example sqrt(.1/1.1)=.3015 with same regression slope; 07.3 illustrative independent projects .1 success probability yields .19 (2) and .65132156 (10); both correct under specified assumptions, not empirical data. More attempts provide opportunities for extremes when candidates are retained and scoring/quality held comparable; not a necessary increase in mean originality or real creativity. Conditional mean, conditional variance, boundary and extreme outcome must not be substituted. 
Practical decision gate: define actual purpose and observable criterion first; measure the relevant operation separately from confidence, costs, environment and recognition; state alternative and potential falsifier before new evidence; choose minimally sufficient and more complex models, demand added discriminating value; record what is missing and whether claim may be used; revise only dependent claims. Separate predicted improvement and realized outcome; no forced optimization of productivity or disclosure. No real-world diagnosis, status judgment or IQ estimate from these heuristics.

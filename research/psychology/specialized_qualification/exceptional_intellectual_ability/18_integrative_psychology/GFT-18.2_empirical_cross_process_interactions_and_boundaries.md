## Integration: evidence matrix and countermodels
| Bridge | Supported scope | Rival / prohibited upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Reward contingency, preparation and task performance | E01/02 local manipulated contrasts, E03 conditional pooled results | task strategy, speed-accuracy shift, cue information, learning; not global IQ gain or universal motivation mechanism |
| Effort and cognitive scores | E04 association plus small average incentive contrast | reverse interpretation of effort, limited incentive response, task specificity; no causal equivalence or per-person correction |
| Acute stress and executive processes | E05 direction stress-to-performance varies by function; E06 subsequent stress associations | prior context, exposure, measurement, cortisol distinct from affect; no proven reciprocal cycle or single resource |
| Personality, situations, states, affect | E07 repeated observations and modeled relationships | common-method and selection, self-selected situation, time ordering, overlapping constructs; no trait-as-cause shortcut |
| Ability-selected emotional processing | E08 selected between-group task contrast | control sample selection, RT transform, cue/task differences, inhibition hypothesis untested; no gifted emotional phenotype |
The matrix links scientific findings as candidate bridges, not arrows of one empirically fitted system. Formal interaction is outcome- and scale-specific; do not infer reciprocal causal pathways by concatenating separate studies. Preserve distinction between measured ability/profile, practiced knowledge, motivational appraisal/expected efficacy, actual control policy/behavior, contextual opportunities and affective response. Trait may summarize distribution or be hypothesized moderator, but don't infer a trait from an event then use it circularly to explain that event. Direct A1 evidence would be evaluated rather than categorically dismissed; no interpolation/extrapolation from group curves without local measurement support/invariance. 
## Operational extension (PRACTICAL_HEURISTIC)
1. Type target and horizon: capacity, response strategy, task choice, actual outcome, affect, cost, recovery or longer-term trait pattern. For a given episode keep observed opportunities, goal/value, expected controllability, chosen effort, objective accuracy/time, subjective reports and feedback distinct. Include personality only when reliable recurring pattern or independent measured trait matters for a prediction.
2. Minimum model M0 skill/requirements/opportunity; M1 adds value or expected efficacy affecting allocation; M2 adds emotional appraisal/response affecting action or recovery; M3 adds feedback, learning or trait/context-dependent dynamics if distinct future predictions supported. Not exclusive or mandatory stages. Consider reverse (outcome changes valuation/affect), common cause (schedule, health, prior skill, evaluator), measurement overlap and selection. Do not automatically add all constructs or multiply uncertainty-free group coefficients.
3. Distinguish true manipulation from label: e.g., reward offered vs reward value actually experienced, objective contingency vs perceived efficacy, changed cue vs latent control, acute stress package vs a specific physiological mediator. Record target contrast and speed/accuracy, retention, transfer, functioning and cost as relevant. Support may increase output by changing demand rather than capacity. Do not force support removal, reward coercion, distress or clinical self-experiment. A suitable safe natural contrast can narrow hypotheses but not prove unique mechanism.
4. For high-IQ specificity require calibrated measurement with adequate local support (A1 for measured adult extreme-tail claims), invariance for compared outcomes, actual group interaction or within-person dynamics as appropriate, uncertainty and validation. Nonlinear conditional group association not categorical architecture. Apply GFT-18.1 sampling and prediction gates and GFT-19.2/19.3 causal/provenance revision. Keep well-being and clinical boundaries of 14/15; safety overrides use, not truth.

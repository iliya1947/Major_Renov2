## 4. Anthropometry → exercise-geometry audit

PASS.

Current synthesis is explicitly non-deterministic.

Anthropometry can influence:
- joint positions;
- external moment arms;
- balance strategy;
- segment clearance;
- feasible ROM;
- comfortable stance/grip/path.

But actual exercise geometry also depends on:
- ROM;
- stance width;
- foot angle;
- heel elevation;
- implement/load position;
- task instructions;
- motor strategy;
- symptoms.

Canonical rule:

**anthropometry changes the option space; it does not uniquely determine the technique.**

No blocker.

## 9. Age-related structural-change audit

PASS.

Current model avoids one stereotyped “aged tissue” phenotype.

### Muscle
Average loss/atrophy and remodeling occur, with substantial heterogeneity.

### Tendon
ECM/cross-linking/cellular/mechanical changes can occur, but direction/magnitude vary.

### Bone
Age-associated mass/microarchitectural changes occur, but chronological age is not a direct personal bone-quality measurement.

Important retained correction:
**intramuscular collagen accumulation is not universal in healthy aging.**

No blocker.

Дата аудита: 2026-11-16.

## Спортивная психология

1. NSCA — Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning, 5th Edition  
   https://dxpprod.nsca.com/essentials-strength/

   Chapter 9 — Psychological Foundations of Performance:
   - arousal, anxiety and stress;
   - motivation;
   - attention and focus;
   - psychological techniques;
   - mental health;

   - motor-skill acquisition.

## Общая приверженность к физической активности

2. Understanding Adults' Experiences and Perceptions of How to Maintain Physical Activity: A Systematic Review and Qualitative Synthesis (2024)  
   PMID 39557793  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39557793/

   Themes include social support/accountability, contextual/environmental influences, flexibility/prioritization, habits, enjoyment and exercise-related identity.

3. Does Self-Determination Theory Associate With Physical Activity? A Systematic Review of Systematic Reviews (2025)  
   PMID 40256835  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40256835/

   Used for autonomy/competence/autonomous motivation as evidence-supported determinants; not treated as a complete explanation of adherence.

4. Implementation Intentions for Exercise and Physical Activity: Who Do They Work For? A Systematic Review  
   PMID 31923898  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31923898/

   Used to avoid treating if–then plans as universally effective; efficacy depends on moderators such as existing intention/self-efficacy.

5. Measurement, determinants, barriers, and interventions for exercise adherence: A scoping review  
   PMID 36775533  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36775533/

   Used for the lack of a gold-standard adherence measure and multi-factor nature of adherence.

## ADHD-specific evidence

6. Exploring Barriers and Facilitators to Physical Activity in Adults with ADHD: A Qualitative Investigation  
   PMID 37361454  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37361454/

   Adult-reported barriers include executive dysfunction, forgetfulness, sustained-focus/time-management problems, motivation/self-esteem; facilitators include perceived benefits, enjoyment and social activity.

7. Daily Routines and Habits in Individuals with ADHD: A Scoping Review (2026)  
   PMID 42352833  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42352833/

   Critical limitation: literature on routines/habits remains sparse, especially in adults; physical activity is one affected domain, and no validated ADHD-specific routine/habit assessment was identified.

8. Treatment Adherence in ADHD: A Systematic Review of Influencing Factors and Strategies for Improvement (2026)  
   PMID 41267417  
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41267417/

   Used only as broader ADHD-adherence context, not direct proof for exercise-specific interventions.

## Ограничение

There is no validated universal “ADHD resistance-training adherence protocol” established by these sources. Any practical architecture for Ilya must be treated as a testable individualized intervention layered on top of physiologically valid training.

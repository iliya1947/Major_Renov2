### LEA-02 — Associative learning: contingency, blocking, conditioned inhibition, Pavlovian/instrumental structure, and transfer
Canonical block:
`research/psychology/qualification/03_cognitive_psychology/LEA-02_associative_learning_contingency_blocking_conditioned_inhibition_pit.md`

Established:

1. association claims must specify S–O, S–S, A/R–O, S–R, context–outcome, or another relation;
2. temporal contiguity != contingency, and background outcome rate is part of contingency;
3. timing/trial spacing can alter learning even when pair counts are unchanged;
4. outcome representation can preserve distributions/magnitudes rather than only binary/mean outcomes;
5. statistical prediction != causal belief;
6. explicit descriptions/instructions can alter or produce canonical blocking effects;
7. conditioned inhibition != executive response inhibition;
8. summation and retardation are distinct functional tests of inhibitory learning;
9. retardation alone is insufficient because latent inhibition can produce it;
10. conditioned inhibition != latent inhibition and occasion-setting/configural alternatives remain viable in some patterns;
11. blocking != overshadowing;
12. blocking supports predictive redundancy/cue competition but is not unique proof of prediction-error learning;
13. salience, attention, learned predictiveness, timing, and compound representation can modulate cue competition;
14. Pavlovian and instrumental relations remain distinct even when PIT links them;
15. general PIT != outcome-specific PIT;
16. human outcome-specific PIT can contain explicit expectancy/WM-dependent control plus residual cue-driven influence;
17. explicit expectancy and WM-load effects constrain both purely automatic and purely propositional accounts;
18. contingency awareness appears important in many standard human fear-conditioning paradigms but is not a universal definition of associative learning;
19. awareness/expectancy measurement can itself alter learning;
20. learning algorithm and representational assumptions must be evaluated jointly;
21. model prediction != unique mechanism identification;
22. a 52-question Associative Learning Inference Gate is canonical.

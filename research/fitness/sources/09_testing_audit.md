# Источники аудита области 09 — тестирование и мониторинг

Дата аудита: 2026-11-16.

1. NSCA — Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning, 5th Edition  
   https://www.nsca.com/certification/cscs/essentials-of-strength-training-and-conditioning-5th-edition/

   Chapters 13–14:
   - reasons for testing;
   - testing terminology;
   - evaluation of test quality;
   - test selection;
   - test administration;
   - measuring performance;
   - monitoring protocols/equipment;
   - statistical evaluation.

2. NSCA — CSCS Detailed Content Outline / Certification Handbook  
   https://www.nsca.com/globalassets/certification/certification-pdfs/nsca-certification-handbook.pdf

   Использовано для test selection, readiness monitoring, administration, validity and interpretation.

3. ACSM — Certified Exercise Physiologist Exam Content Outline  
   https://acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/ACSM-Certified-Exercise-Physiologist-Exam-Content-Outline.pdf

   ACSM-EP conducts preparticipation screening, graded testing, strength, flexibility and body-composition assessments.

4. ACSM — Fitness Assessment Manual, 6th edition overview  
   https://acsm.org/education-resources/books/fitness-assessment-manual/

   Использовано для проверки major assessment categories and measurement methodology.

## Ограничение

Карта не утверждает, что все перечисленные тесты нужны Илье. Персональный battery должен быть минимальным и decision-relevant.

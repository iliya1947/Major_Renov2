## Provisional transfer rules
- Two independent axes: evidence of measured general ability and evidence of ASD developmental criteria. Specific talent, high verbal score, low PSI, sensory sensitivity, strong interests, introversion, preference for intellectual peers, deliberate social strategies or screening scores do not independently establish either.
- Separate capacity (standardized task), behavior (context and partner), adaptive functioning (actual everyday demands), internal effort and support. High output and real support needs can coexist; relative weakness alone is not impairment. Neither infer empathy deficit from ASD nor exclude ASD from good social reasoning.
- Competing models: person–environment mismatch, social anxiety/learned avoidance, ADHD, developmental language/pragmatic difference, sensory environment, autism or combinations. Identify early developmental history, both diagnostic domains, context variability, onset and functional consequences, and independently corroborate with consent. Context improvement can reflect support in autistic as well as non-autistic people; do not deliberately remove helpful supports. Referral if clinically relevant; no diagnosis or treatment prescription.
- Research design: high-IQ autistic vs IQ-matched non-autistic; ideally comparable non-high-IQ groups and clinical differentials; measure IQ with valid norms, independent functional outcomes, developmental and recruitment history, access and accommodations. Cross-sectional association cannot establish causal compensation, compare interactions with uncertainty, not significance differences. Retain source dependency and range restrictions.

### Final analytical rules
Separate independent ability and developmental-condition evidence; specific strength vs FSIQ; relative vs absolute weakness; capacity vs everyday performance vs opportunity/support/effort. Check test precision, ceiling, norms, motor/language demands, anxiety, ADHD, sensory environment, sleep, developmental history and both ASD domains. Good output/social reasoning does not exclude ASD; difficulty, sensory features, scripting do not establish it. Greater IQ-minus-adaptive gap can coexist with better absolute functioning. Cross-sectional age association not developmental decline. Adult measured-ability comparison preferred; general adult evidence indirect, pediatric evidence developmental possibility only. Moderation requires comparable ability strata/continuous interaction and uncertainty, not differing significance. Voluntary ordinary observations can refine local alternatives, e.g. shared terminology changes specific mutual understanding; contextual improvement cannot establish or exclude developmental condition. Never withdraw helpful supports to test cause; clinical assessment when appropriate by qualified specialist. No universal phenotype or extreme-tail claim.

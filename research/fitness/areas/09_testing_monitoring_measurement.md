## 23. Interpretation hierarchy

Before declaring progress/failure:

1. Was measurement valid?
2. Was protocol standardized?
3. Was there a learning effect?
4. Is change larger than expected error?
5. Is trend sustained?
6. Does another metric corroborate it?
7. Is the change practically meaningful?
8. Does it alter a training decision?

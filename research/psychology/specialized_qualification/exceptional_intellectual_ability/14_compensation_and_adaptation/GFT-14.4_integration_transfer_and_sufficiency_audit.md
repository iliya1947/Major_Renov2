## Integrated practical procedure (generic heuristic, not validated clinical instrument)
1. Define goal and observation: what outcome, for whom, in what context, interval and with which supports? Separate measured general ability with uncertainty, specific task component, actual output/participation and developmental or acquired history. Mark absent measurements, not guessed deficits. Use absolute and comparator-specific scores; a relative weakness can still be normatively adequate.
2. Specify candidate mechanism and its evidence: reported or observed strategy, concealment, environmental accommodation, learned target skill or alternate route despite independently supported difficulty. Keep independent observations separate from algebraically coupled scores and ratings. A difference score cannot establish mechanism, and successful output cannot establish either presence or absence of an underlying difficulty.
3. Compare models before adding complexity. M0 ordinary skill/context/experience predicts good performance in matched conditions without extra special explanation. M1 compensatory strategy predicts conditional benefit at matched demands and independent evidence of target-process difficulty, not merely average vs high IQ profile. M2 accessible environment predicts improvements tracking changed task demands and support rather than underlying capacity. M3 concealment/pressure predicts change in visibility, not necessarily in actual functioning, and cost varying with context. M4 coexisting processes may explain distinct outcomes better; require extra evidence and discriminating predictions rather than label count. M5 acquired health/sleep/mood/workload changes can explain fatigue or new functional loss independently of developmental traits. Models can coexist; no exclusive choice before evidence.
4. Evaluate outcome benefit and cost separately: supported task quality/errors, time incl setup, goal attainment, subjective demand, actual fatigue trajectory and recovery, delayed retention if relevant, displaced activities, safety, wellbeing and autonomy. A low-cost effective support is a success even if independent skill does not change. No invented cost; absence of measured cost is not evidence of no unmeasured cost.
5. Compare ordinary comparable contexts and consented incremental supports without removing essential aids, coercive unmasking or deliberate distress. Distinguish component effects from packages, assisted outcome from acquired skill, immediate from sustained/transfer, group associations from person-level evidence. Specify what observation could disconfirm each proposed mechanism; update affected inference only, preserve actual benefits and experiences. Persistent significant or abrupt functional changes warrant appropriate qualified assessment rather than IQ or masking attribution. No diagnosis, clinical prescription or self-testing by withholding supports.
6. Research gate: check cohort/selection, actual IQ measure and range, comparator and independent evidence of strategy, valid outcome measurement, causal design/assumptions, cross-group invariance, uncertainty, source conflicts and overlap. Formal interaction for moderation; null not equivalence; no high-tail extrapolation, no double counting source/review. Classify claims as moderate/context-bounded evidence or hypotheses; this operational model is a practical heuristic, not a calibrated probability or diagnostic instrument.

## Three new wholly fictional multi-stage contradiction-update assessments
A. Fictional botanical illustrator with verified high spatial index (no FSIQ) completes accurate plates while reporting fatigue and uses a digital overlay. Reviewer calls hidden neurodevelopmental deficit compensated by intelligence. Separate spatial index, output, strategy, cost and history. M0 expertise, M1 alternative route, M2 interface accessibility, M5 fatigue; compare matched material, workload and voluntary contexts. Update1: overlay reduces alignment errors across all artists; illustrator's ordinary drawing remains accurate, unfamiliar color labeling remains difficult. Withdraw global compensation claim, retain support benefit and distinct color task; no IQ causal mechanism or diagnosis. Update2: fatigue resolves when work shifts earlier, overlay use and color difficulty unchanged. Separate schedule and possible target-specific difficulty; no single-mechanism model. Preserve useful overlay and actual reported experience, seek independent evidence only if relevant to goals.
B. Fictional craft instructor follows rehearsed social scripts at crowded fairs, reports high masking questionnaire and receives favorable customer ratings. Analyst predicts inevitable burnout and recommends stopping scripts. Distinguish impression, actual communication, intent, pressure, cost and context. Update1: private small-group workshops equally successful with less scripting and lower stress; sales opportunities differ. Prefer context/pressure candidate, no causal proof or compulsory unmasking. Update2: long-run stress increases following unrelated caring workload despite lower scripting; sleep and actual functioning worsen. Withdraw masking-only prediction, retain specific context association, consider qualified evaluation if persistent and new impairment; no claim masking harmless/protective. Assess goal, preference, safety and recovery, not typical appearance.
C. Fictional trial recruits higher-IQ adults with reading difficulties and ordinary-IQ controls, compares assistive audio package with usual instruction. Assisted comprehension improves only significantly in higher-IQ group, unaided decoding not measured. Reviewer claims IQ causes restoration of underlying reading skill and lower cost. Need four comparable clinical/ability conditions or appropriate within-condition interaction, actual norms, goal-specific outcome, active comparator, baseline, time/effort, confidence intervals. Update1: randomization within IQ strata reveals comparable gains in both with broad interaction CI; participants report greater setup time but fewer missed assignments. Withdraw differential efficacy and cure claims, keep supported benefit and mixed cost; null not equivalence. Update2: follow-up independent decoding improves after additional instruction in both groups, not tied to audio use; original table correction changes exact subgroup estimate. Preserve verified assisted contrast, separate later skill and package attribution, restrict affected estimate and trace downstream claims. No extreme-tail or clinical conclusion.

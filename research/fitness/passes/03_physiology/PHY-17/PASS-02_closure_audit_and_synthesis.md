## 3. Cross-system coupling audit

PASS.

Current integrated exercise physiology supports simultaneous coupling among:

- central motor command;
- skeletal-muscle recruitment and force;
- muscle metabolism;
- group III/IV and other sensory feedback;
- autonomic cardiovascular control;
- regional blood flow;
- ventilation/gas exchange;
- endocrine/substrate regulation;
- immune/exerkine signaling.

The 2026 integrated lung/exercise review explicitly frames exercise as dynamic integration of respiratory, cardiovascular and musculoskeletal responses.

The 2025 neural-feedback review demonstrates that locomotor/respiratory muscle afferents, lung stretch receptors and carotid chemoreceptors materially influence cardiorespiratory responses and exercise performance.

Canonical rule:

**exercise physiology is a coupled control problem, not independent organ responses occurring side by side.**

No blocker.

## 4. One-set integrated-response audit

PASS.

A resistance-training set is retained as a non-steady-state perturbation involving:

- neural drive/recruitment;
- force and intramuscular pressure;
- ATP turnover;
- local substrate/metabolite changes;
- perfusion constraints during contraction;
- autonomic/cardiorespiratory response;
- ventilation/breathing strategy;
- endocrine/systemic response.

The exact weighting depends on load, reps, duration, proximity to failure, contraction mode, active muscle mass, breathing and training status.

Canonical rule:

**external load alone does not define the physiological state created by a set.**

No blocker.

## 5. Set → session history-dependence audit

PASS.

A later set begins from a changed internal state.

Prior work can alter:

- phosphocreatine recovery;
- glycogen/substrate state;
- metabolites;
- central/peripheral fatigue;
- cardiorespiratory baseline;
- temperature/fluid state;
- local/systemic signaling.

Therefore equal external work can produce unequal internal responses within the same session.

Canonical rule:

**session physiology is path/history dependent, not a linear sum of interchangeable sets.**

No blocker.

## 6. Acute → chronic adaptation audit

PASS.

Acute molecular, endocrine and metabolic events are transient candidate mechanisms/signals.

Chronic adaptation requires:

- repeated exposure;
- recovery;
- protein turnover/gene expression;
- tissue remodeling;
- altered future system response.

Different outcomes evolve on different clocks.

Current human multi-omic preprints support strongly time-dependent, mode-specific responses, but their preprint status is retained and they are not treated as direct chronic-outcome surrogates.

Canonical rule:

**acute signal magnitude ≠ chronic adaptation magnitude.**

No blocker.

## 7. Specificity audit

PASS.

Resistance and endurance exercise share major physiological systems but weight them differently.

Resistance-biased exposure emphasizes:

- neural recruitment/force;
- mechanical loading;
- phosphagen/glycolytic demand;
- tissue-specific mechanical signaling.

Endurance-biased exposure emphasizes:

- sustained oxidative demand;
- cardiac output/blood flow;
- ventilation;
- mitochondrial/capillary adaptation.

Canonical rule:

**specificity changes the weighting and temporal profile of a shared system; it does not imply two isolated physiologies.**

No blocker.

## 8. Sleep / nutrition / energy / stress audit

PASS.

Recovery context is part of the adaptation system.

Current evidence supports:

- low energy availability can impair lean-mass/FFM and other neuromusculoskeletal responses to training;
- sleep affects recovery and performance, with domain/exposure-specific effects;
- nutritional substrate and protein availability modify recovery/adaptation;
- non-training stress changes the state entering the next training exposure.

Canonical rule:

**training stimulus and recovery context jointly determine adaptation.**

No blocker.

## 9. Competing-signals audit

PASS WITH MECHANISTIC CAUTION.

The classic AMPK-vs-mTOR model remains biologically informative at a mechanistic level but is not accepted as a binary programming rule.

Reasons:

- pathway activation is time-, tissue-, nutrition- and training-status-dependent;
- signaling networks have multiple upstream/downstream interactions;
- acute phosphorylation does not linearly predict chronic phenotype;
- human concurrent-training outcomes do not show universal interference.

Canonical rule:

**“AMPK blocks mTOR” is too crude for direct prescription.**

No blocker.

## 10. Concurrent-training audit

PASS.

The 2026 umbrella review included 17 meta-analyses, 144 individual studies and 1492 healthy participants.

It found:

- CT improved aerobic capacity compared with RT alone;
- CT improved strength compared with ET alone;
- compared with RT alone, pooled strength, power and hypertrophy outcomes were broadly comparable;
- sequence effects were not consistently significant;
- highly trained/elite populations remain underrepresented.

A 16-week RCT in previously untrained young men found:

- similar myofibrillar protein-synthesis responses between CT and RT;
- similar type-II fiber hypertrophy;
- greater strength gains with RT;
- aerobic-capacity improvement only with CT.

This demonstrates outcome-specific coexistence/interference rather than one universal effect.

Canonical rule:

**concurrent interference is outcome-, protocol-, population- and recovery-dependent.**

Detailed prescription remains GPP/PRO.

No blocker.

## 11. Inter-organ / exerkine audit

PASS WITH CAUSALITY BOUNDARY.

Exercise-related inter-organ communication is biologically credible and supported by current review evidence.

Skeletal muscle can communicate with:

- adipose tissue;
- liver;
- vasculature/heart;
- brain;
- bone;
- immune system.

However, the 2026 cross-platform analysis of 40 proposed skeletal-muscle exerkines found substantial heterogeneity, with only a subset showing conserved regulation across species/conditions; transcript abundance does not guarantee protein secretion or target-tissue action.

Canonical rule:

**inter-organ communication is real; individual candidate exerkines require evidence-specific causal validation.**

No blocker.

## 12. Network-physiology audit

PASS.

Network physiology is retained as an organizing framework.

Acceptable uses:

- mapping measured nodes and interactions;
- integrating different physiological time scales;
- identifying shared vs modality-specific responses;
- generating testable hypotheses.

Unacceptable uses:

- claiming unmeasured causal links;
- using one omic signature as proof of a whole-system adaptation;
- inventing a master regulator;
- replacing performance/tissue outcomes with network language.

Canonical rule:

**network models organize evidence; they do not create evidence.**

No blocker.

## 17. Closure criterion test

Normalized-tree question:

> как взаимодействуют muscle, nervous, cardiovascular, endocrine, metabolic systems?

Result: **PASS.**

Canonical synthesis:

`task demand`
`→ central command + recruitment`
`→ force + ATP demand + metabolites`
`↔ sensory feedback`
`↔ cardiac output / flow / ventilation`
`↔ substrate + endocrine/immune regulation`
`→ acute performance state`.

Across a session:

`prior work`
changes
`future internal state`.

Across training:

`repeated task-specific exposures`
`+ sleep/nutrition/energy/stress/recovery`
`→ tissue/system-specific remodeling on different clocks`
`→ altered future performance and acute physiology`.

Concurrent training can generate complementary, neutral or interfering outcomes depending on what is measured and how the training is dosed.

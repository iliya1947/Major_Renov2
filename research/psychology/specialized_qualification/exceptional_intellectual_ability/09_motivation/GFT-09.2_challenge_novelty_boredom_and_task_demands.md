E5 Tam et al. (2025 online), https://doi.org/10.1177/01461672251383966 : SAGE discussion excerpts directly inspected. Five studies (1,2a,2b,3,4), experimental and experience sampling. Manipulating stimulus meaningfulness increased attention; directing attention increased perceived meaning of meaningful stimuli but diminished or did not alter meaning of meaningless ones. Meaning manipulation reduced boredom (1,3); attention manipulation did not significantly change boredom (2a,2b,3); authors note moderate manipulations and attention to thoughts as alternative engagement. In experience sampling, both meaning and attention associated with lower boredom. Directional effects specific to experimental operationalizations; not proof of absence of attentional role, unique mechanism or universal independence. Full methods, supplements and uncertainty not checked. Retain MAC as competing heuristic, not settled causal architecture.

## Practical gate (heuristic)
Distinguish objective complexity (task structure), difficulty (performance conditional on person/context), perceived challenge, novelty, cognitive effort and value. Task may be complex but familiar, novel but easy, difficult because of missing prerequisite, or easy but meaningful. State boredom is reported experience; proneness requires repeated contexts/validated trait assessment, neither an IQ marker. Do not infer underchallenge from boredom alone, or intellectual deficit from avoidance. Low engagement has competing explanations: low meaning, underload, overload, fatigue, uncertainty, missing skills, incentives, environment. Compare voluntary low-risk task variants: hold topic and demands constant while varying perceived relevance; hold relevance constant while varying difficulty/support; compare familiar vs novel variants of similar difficulty; monitor accuracy, time, subjective challenge, boredom and actual choice separately. Demand avoidance can be efficient; choosing novelty can be exploratory without learning or useful output. Distinguish state change from stable trait and local evidence from causal proof. No universal inverted-U curve, fixed optimal difficulty or diagnosis. Respect chosen goals and avoid imposing complexity solely on the basis of presumed IQ.

It does not mean:

- oxygen absent;

- glycolysis only;
- phosphagen only.

BIO-08/09 remain authoritative:

No blocker.

## 4. Mechanical power vs metabolic power audit

PASS.

External mechanical power depends on:

- force;
- velocity;
- technique;
- body size;
- mechanical efficiency;
- neuromuscular output;
- test mechanics.

Metabolic ATP-production rate is an internal biochemical flux.

Canonical rule:

**mechanical power ≠ ATP-production rate.**

No blocker.

## 5. Anaerobic power definition audit

PASS AFTER WORDING NARROWING.

Retain:

**anaerobic power = ability to produce very high external power in a task where non-oxidative ATP provision is a major contributor.**

Do not retain:

- “anaerobic power” as direct ATP flux;
- anaerobic power as one universal trait independent of mode;
- anaerobic power as phosphagen-only performance.

No blocker.

---

## 6. Anaerobic capacity definition audit

PASS AFTER OPERATIONAL TIGHTENING.

Retain:

**anaerobic capacity = task- and method-defined ability to sustain/accumulate high-intensity work while non-oxidative ATP provision is substantial.**

This is deliberately not defined as a directly measurable literal energy tank.

Why:

- mechanical work ≠ metabolic energy;
- oxidative contribution remains present;
- different methods estimate different constructs;
- capacity is protocol dependent.

Canonical rule:

**anaerobic capacity is a finite task-defined performance/bioenergetic construct, not one directly visible biochemical reservoir.**

No blocker.

---

## 7. “Total non-oxidative work/energy” audit

PASS AFTER REMOVING DIRECT-MEASUREMENT IMPLICATION.

BIO-14 does not claim that routine performance testing directly measures total non-oxidative energy.

Retain only:

- model-based methods can estimate anaerobic contribution;
- performance tests can characterize output/sustainability.

Canonical rule:

**performance work and estimated anaerobic energy are non-equivalent constructs.**

No blocker.

## 8. Peak power audit

PASS.

Peak power is retained as:

No blocker.

## 9. Mean power audit

Mean power is retained as:

It is not retained as a direct biochemical measurement of anaerobic capacity.

No blocker.

## 11. Peak power ≠ phosphagen / mean power ≠ glycolytic audit

PASS.

Reject:

- peak = “alactic”;
- mean = “lactic”;

Reason:

- phosphagen, glycolytic and oxidative pathways overlap;
- contribution changes continuously;
- external power is not pathway flux.

No blocker.

## 12. Fatigue/decrement construct audit

PASS WITH STRONG RESTRICTION.

Power decrement can reflect:

- high first-bout power;
- recovery limitation;
- PCr/metabolite/ion state;
- neuromuscular fatigue;
- pacing;
- technique.

Therefore:

**fatigue/decrement is descriptive performance information, not a single physiological mechanism or universal fitness trait.**

No blocker.

Canonical rule:

**low percentage decrement ≠ automatically superior anaerobic fitness.**

No blocker.

## 40. Closure decision

**BIO-14 = CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v2.0.**

Meaning:

1. anaerobic power and capacity are distinct;
2. mechanical power is not metabolic ATP-production rate;
3. anaerobic power emphasizes high rate/output in a task with substantial non-oxidative contribution;
4. anaerobic capacity is task/method-defined integrated high-intensity capability, not a literal measurable tank;
5. peak and mean power are distinct operational outputs;
6. peak power is not direct PCr/phosphagen measurement;
7. mean power is not direct glycolytic/anaerobic-capacity measurement;
8. fatigue/decrement is descriptive and can be confounded by initial output;
9. single-bout power and repeated-bout ability are distinct;
10. repeated-bout performance is mixed-metabolic;
11. oxidative ATP supports PCr resynthesis/recovery between high-intensity efforts;
12. PCr recovery is important but non-equivalent to complete readiness;
13. no universal full-recovery time is retained;
14. phosphagen and glycolytic pathways overlap and do not map one-to-one to power/capacity metrics;
15. blood lactate is not an anaerobic-capacity score;
16. Wingate-type results are protocol-specific performance outputs, not direct biochemical measurements;
17. MAOD/oxygen-deficit and PCr–La–O2 are indirect/model-derived;
18. test mode/duration/load/familiarization/scaling affect interpretation;
19. formal current test validity/reliability remains TST-owned;
20. no universal anaerobic-fitness scalar is retained.

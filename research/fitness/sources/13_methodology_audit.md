# Источники аудита области 13 — методология исследований и статистика

Дата аудита: 2026-11-16.

1. NSCA — 2025 CSCS Detailed Content Outline  
   https://dxpprod.nsca.com/globalassets/certification/certification-pdfs/certification-handbook-2025-cscs-dco.pdf

   Scientific Research and Statistics domain explicitly includes:
   - scientific research process/research design/basic statistics;
   - PICOT;
   - reliability and validity;
   - practical application of research.

2. Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions, current version  
   https://www.cochrane.org/handbook

   Used for:
   - systematic-review structure;
   - risk of bias;
   - meta-analysis;
   - heterogeneity;
   - missing evidence/reporting bias;
   - interpretation.

3. Cochrane Handbook Chapter 10 — Analysing data and undertaking meta-analyses  
   https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-10

   Current November 2026 update used for fixed/random effects, heterogeneity, prediction intervals and sensitivity analyses.

4. Cochrane Handbook Chapter 7 — Bias and conflicts of interest  
   https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-07

5. Cochrane Handbook Chapter 13 — Missing evidence in meta-analysis  
   https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-13

6. GRADE Working Group  
   https://www.gradeworkinggroup.org/

   Used for certainty-of-evidence domains and separation between evidence certainty and recommendation strength.

## Ограничение

This area is a methodological map, not a substitute for a statistics course. Only concepts needed to correctly evaluate and apply exercise-science evidence will be pursued deeply.

## Research-unit workflow

For every topic ID:

1. Define exact question(s).
2. Check current guidelines/position stands.
3. Check systematic reviews/meta-analyses.
4. Check primary studies where needed.
5. Assess limitations/certainty.
6. Record stable claims in `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md`.
7. Put full synthesis in future `findings/<AREA>/<ID>.md`.
8. Mark unresolved questions.
9. Do not close merely because one plausible answer exists.

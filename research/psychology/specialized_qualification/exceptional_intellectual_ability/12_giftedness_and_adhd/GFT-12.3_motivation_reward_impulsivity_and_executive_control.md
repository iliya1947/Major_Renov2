### Evidence checkpoint
E1. Jackson & MacKillop (2016), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpsc.2016.01.007, indexed article results; PMC direct access failed and publisher 403. Monetary delay discounting case-control meta-analysis: adult subgroup d=0.40, greater discounting in ADHD. This is a standardized group contrast for a specific choice paradigm, not a diagnostic cutoff or causal explanation. Exact adult confidence interval and subgroup study counts not reverified. Independent direct study indexed at https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29687930/ reports no group difference in temporal discounting despite differences in brain activity during waiting; no equivalence inference, sample details not rechecked. Discounting may reflect waiting cost, uncertainty, trust, time horizon, opportunity cost or preference; delay aversion and temporal discounting not identical. Possible overlap with other discounting syntheses not established; do not count as independent replications.

E2. Adult stop-signal synthesis (online 2023), https://doi.org/10.1007/s11065-023-09592-5. Publisher methods/results sections directly inspected. 27 study observations from 26 publications; pooled Hedges g=0.509 (95% CI 0.376–0.644), I²=31.224%; 95% prediction interval 0.129–0.891. Study effects range -0.341 to 1.230. 19 articles/studies rated low SST validity in reported section; 13 of 26 articles high risk of bias; only two high SST validity. IQ meta-regression not significant, using study-level averaged IQ with missing values: cannot establish individual IQ moderation or equivalence, especially at extreme IQ. Motor stopping is not temporal choice, general impulsivity, or all executive control. Marx 2013 non-reward arm is included, hence not independent of E4 for stop-signal evidence. SST assumptions, task validity, go omissions, speed-accuracy tradeoff and trigger failures matter. No individual diagnosis or causal proof.

E3. Plichta & Scheres (2014), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23928090/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3989497/, indexed review text, direct PMC challenge/publisher 403. Eight studies, total N340, reported medium group differences in anticipatory ventral-striatal BOLD (d 0.48–0.58 across specifications). Mixed developmental samples, task and ROI selection; not dopamine assay or universal deficit. Direct publisher abstract/introduction inspected for https://academic.oup.com/scan/article/10/7/945/1735806: 16 adults/14 children with combined-type ADHD and 20 adult/10 child controls, reported reduced anticipatory VS responsiveness for adults, not children. Cross-sectional age contrast is not within-person developmental trajectory; subgroup null not equivalence. The article also describes prior nonreplication and divergent subtype effects; those original reports not fully rechecked. Reward anticipation, delivery, learning and subjective pleasure require separate measures; do not derive receipt response from anticipation. Older review may share sources with other cited reward reports; do not double count.

### Provisional analytical rules
1. Separate value of goal, intention, initiation, execution, maintenance, inhibition of response, stopping/switching, and realized output. High goal importance plus delayed initiation can reflect initiation friction, uncertain next action, conflicting goals, learned avoidance, sleep/state, or cost/value changes; no automatic attribution to ADHD.
2. Compare incentive magnitude, frequency, delay, probability, choice and feedback independently where possible. Increased output can result from changed strategy or speed-accuracy tradeoff rather than restored capacity. Distinguish task-supplied stimulation from subjective interest and opportunity cost.
3. Separate choosing immediate reward from failed motor inhibition. Choosing immediate option may be rational under uncertainty or liquidity constraints; specify incentives and independent criteria before calling it impulsive.
4. High absolute performance can coexist with relative peer weakness and actual support costs; within-person discrepancy alone is not diagnosis or proof of compensation. Do not withdraw needed supports to test an account. Record ordinary comparable observations of initiation, output/errors, actual/subjective effort, prior goal, interruptions and alternatives; revise if predicted relationships fail.
5. Multiple-pathway models are hypotheses with potentially distinct motivational, learning, temporal and executive contributions; no single inferred dopamine level, resource allocation mechanism or high-IQ interaction. Clinical assessment boundary unchanged.

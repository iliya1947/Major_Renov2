# 12. Психология, СДВГ и приверженность

Статус: **MAP_READY после отдельного аудита полноты 2026-11-16**.

Цель файла — понять, почему физиологически хорошая программа выполняется или не выполняется, и какие модификации повышают вероятность устойчивой тренировки. Для СДВГ отдельно различать: (1) установленные особенности расстройства; (2) наблюдаемые барьеры физической активности; (3) практические гипотезы, которые ещё надо проверять персонально.

## 22. ADHD and routines/habits — evidence limits

1. Adult-specific routine/habit research is limited.
2. Existing literature is heavily pediatric.
3. No validated universal “ADHD routine protocol” exists.
4. Sleep, eating, physical activity and sedentary behavior are recurring routine domains.
5. Standard habit advice should be treated as candidate intervention, not established ADHD-specific rule.

## 23. ADHD medication and exercise

3. Timing may interact with exercise experience.
4. Caffeine/other stimulants can interact.

## 24. ADHD-friendly program design — candidate principles

These are to be tested against Ilya's actual behavior, not assumed truths:

1. Reduce unnecessary startup steps.
2. Keep equipment accessible.
3. Use a stable core routine.
4. Limit number of decisions.
5. Offer bounded choice.
6. Make first action obvious.
7. Use short initiation sequence.
8. Provide immediate completion feedback.
9. Make progress visible.
10. Use variation strategically when monotony becomes a barrier.
11. Keep progress tracking simple.
12. Preserve natural stopping points.
13. Have a minimum viable session.
14. Have a re-entry protocol after misses.
15. Avoid punishment/catch-up sessions.
16. Avoid excessive session complexity.
17. Avoid dependence on perfect mood/motivation.
18. Test whether music, timer, reminders, gamification or social accountability actually help before institutionalizing them.

## 29. Friction audit

For every training workflow ask:

1. What must happen before first rep?
2. Which step requires a decision?
3. Which step can be eliminated?
4. Which step can be preconfigured?
5. What commonly interrupts initiation?
6. What makes stopping more likely?
7. What creates an unnecessary restart cost?
8. Which friction is physiologically necessary vs accidental?

## 30. Individual behavioral experimentation

2. Change one lever.
3. Track initiation/completion.
4. Keep physiological program stable where possible.
5. Evaluate over multiple opportunities.
6. Preserve what works.
7. Revert what does not.
8. Do not infer mechanism from one successful day.

## Результат аудита 2026-11-16

Исходная версия уже содержала ключевую практическую идею: friction, immediate feedback, visible progress, bounded choice, minimum session и skepticism к generic ADHD hacks. Аудит добавил недостающую теоретическую и доказательную структуру:

1. intention–behavior gap;
2. self-determination, autonomy/competence/relatedness;
3. self-efficacy;
4. goal setting and implementation intentions;
5. habit/routine distinction;
6. reinforcement/reward;
7. enjoyment/affect;
8. social/environmental determinants;
9. lapse/re-entry;
10. adherence measurement;
11. ADHD executive, reward and time-processing domains;
12. actual qualitative barriers/facilitators reported by adults with ADHD;
13. explicit limits of ADHD habit/routine evidence;
14. candidate, not assumed, ADHD program adaptations;
15. friction/choice/feedback/novelty architectures;
16. individual behavioral experimentation;
17. clinical mental-health boundary.

NSCA Essentials 5th Edition treats motivation, attention/focus, arousal/stress, psychological techniques and mental health as core performance-psychology topics. General physical-activity maintenance research repeatedly identifies enjoyment, integration into routine, flexibility, support, competence/self-efficacy and progress feedback as relevant. Adult ADHD-specific evidence is much thinner: qualitative research documents executive/time-management/focus barriers and individualized facilitators, while a 2026 scoping review concludes that routine/habit research in ADHD remains limited, especially in adults.


## Stage 0B addition — модели изменения поведения и counseling

Профессиональный crosswalk ACSM показал, что исходный behavioral block слишком сильно опирался на SDT и практические эвристики. Дополнительно исследовать:

1. Social Cognitive Theory.
2. Transtheoretical Model.
3. Health Belief Model.
4. Theory of Planned Behavior.
5. Social Ecological Model.
6. Cognitive Evaluation Theory — в связи с SDT.
7. Motivational interviewing.
8. Health coaching.
9. Readiness for change.
10. Barrier self-efficacy.
11. Relapse prevention.
12. Affect/mood/emotion in adherence.
13. Health literacy and communication.
14. Сравнение predictive/intervention value моделей, а не коллекционирование теорий.

Эти модели не предполагаются одинаково полезными; каждая требует отдельной проверки доказательств.

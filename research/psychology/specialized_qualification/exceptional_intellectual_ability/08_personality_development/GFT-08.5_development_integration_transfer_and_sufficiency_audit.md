## 3. Integrated practical reasoning gate
Observation -> precise construct and time -> measurement and ascertainment -> competing explanations -> distinguishing predictions -> new evidence -> limited update.
- Age-relative score, absolute linked performance, rank continuity and individual change separate; profile discrepancy at one time is not divergence of slopes.
- Reliable differences, normative rarity and actual limitations separate. Group covariance patterns and within-person dispersion are not the same, and neither establishes a clinical mechanism.
- Ability, label, identity, achievement, demands/resources, values, belonging and adaptation distinct. Respect reported experience; explanatory interpretation remains revisable.
- Independent learning, baseline knowledge, gains, retention, transfer, costs and opportunity separate. Strategy availability, choice, implementation, effect and continued use can differ.
- Check competing models: measurement error/ceiling/retest, developmental exposure, context/selection, training, health and independent difficulties. Do not invent an integrated hidden cause. A simpler task-specific explanation wins when it makes adequate distinct predictions; add interaction or latent mechanism only with discriminating evidence.
- For practical support choose voluntary low-risk observations or adjustments; compare equivalent tasks/settings with recorded chronology and relevant outcomes, preserve autonomy. No gifted-label requirement for support, no diagnosis, no adult extreme-tail causal claims.
- Source transfer: broad adult data support general distinctions, not a high-tail-specific profile; childhood-selected cohort is not adult retest; qualitative narratives are not population proportions; multiple reports of shared cohort do not add independent confirmation.

## 4. New synthetic integration cases
A. Fictional adult archives course: a participant had a high school test result, later a high age-relative reasoning score and average speed; reports boredom and poor accuracy with unfamiliar indexing software. Analyst posits gifted asynchrony causing inability to adapt. Separate childhood/adult norm and test, profile vs slope, objective accuracy and motivation. Alternatives: training gap, interface demands, fatigue and role autonomy. Compare matched tasks with/without instruction, delayed novel indexing and measured functioning. New: new-interface accuracy improves with worked examples, routine familiar tasks remain accurate, reasoning/speed profile unchanged. Withdraw causal asynchrony and general adaptation-deficit claims; retain measured difference and task-specific difficulties, without exact IQ or diagnosis.
B. Fictional volunteer observatory: two age groups screened for high total score; older group has more uniform indices; younger members of a self-selected support forum report greater dissatisfaction. Analyst claims gifted people become cognitively uniform and emotionally adjusted with age. Compare standardization vs self-selected frames, age cohort/period, selection on composite and covariance, objective vs perceived fit. New: simulation based on age-specific covariance reproduces much profile difference; repeated independent observations show mixed changes; forum dissatisfaction varies with role clarity. Withdraw universal individual aging mechanism; retain cross-sectional associations and local context hypothesis, no causal claim.
C. Fictional restoration workshop: high-achievement alumni report long self-study, new learners report shorter study but larger immediate gains. Analyst attributes alumni expertise exclusively to intellectual potential or effort. Separate initial skill, gain scale, dose/quality, selection, support, retention and transfer. Predict matched practice effects on novel artifacts. New: on unfamiliar materials alumni advantage disappears, varied practice predicts improved transfer among both groups, self-reported hours noisy. Revise general superiority claim to domain-bound expertise and conditional strategy hypothesis; preserve original achievement, no causal percentage or exceptional-IQ inference.

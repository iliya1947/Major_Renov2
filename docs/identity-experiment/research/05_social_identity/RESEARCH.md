# A1 №5 — Социальная психология и идентичность: источники картографии

**Назначение:** доказательная опора для первичной карты области. Не глубокий разбор найденных механизмов.

## Self и identity

1. **Hards et al. (2024), “What is the self anyway?” New Ideas in Psychology 74:101080.** DOI: 10.1016/j.newideapsych.2024.101080.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
   Для карты: современная литература использует множество перекрывающихся self-terms; conceptual clarification само по себе является крупной проблемой.

2. **Ellemers, Spears & Doosje (2002), Self and Social Identity. Annual Review of Psychology 53:161–186.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.psych.53.100901.135228.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: social identity зависит от group commitment и social context; personal/group identity concerns нельзя слить.

3. **Hornsey (2008), Social Identity Theory and Self-Categorization Theory: A Historical Review. Social and Personality Psychology Compass 2:204–222.** DOI: 10.1111/j.1751-9004.2007.00066.x.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/overview.  
   Для карты: Social Identity Theory и Self-Categorization Theory — связанные, но различимые theoretical traditions.

4. **Howard (2000), Social Psychology of Identities. Annual Review of Sociology 26:367–393.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.soc.26.1.367.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: identity research включает social-cognitive и symbolic-interactionist traditions и социальные основания identity.

5. **Crocetti (2026), Adolescent identity development processes, implications and interventions. Nature Reviews Psychology 5:441–457.** DOI: 10.1038/s44159-026-00569-8.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: personal и social identity development, commitments, domains, individual/time variation — самостоятельная developmental ветвь.

6. **Branje et al. (2021), Dynamics of Identity Development in Adolescence: A Decade in Review. Journal of Research on Adolescence 31:908–927.** DOI: 10.1111/jora.12678.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и open full-text sections.  
   Для карты: identity development включает stability, maturation, life transitions, within-person и narrative processes.

## Relational, role и multiple identities

7. **Andersen & Chen (2002), The relational self: an interpersonal social-cognitive theory. Psychological Review 109:619–645.** DOI: 10.1037/0033-295X.109.4.619.  
   Просмотрено: abstract PubMed.  
   Для карты: relational self links self-knowledge with representations of significant others; relationship-specific selves are a distinct level.

8. **Biddle (1986), Recent Developments in Role Theory. Annual Review of Sociology 12:67–92.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.so.12.080186.000435.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: role theory has functional, symbolic-interactionist, structural, organizational and cognitive traditions.

9. **Roccas & Brewer-related multiple identity line represented by Kang & Bodenhausen (2015), Multiple Identities in Social Perception and Interaction. Annual Review of Psychology 66:547–574.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010814-015025.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: ambiguity, crossed categories and multiple identities affect both perceiver and target.

10. **Azmitia, Garcia Peraza & Casanova (2023), Social Identities and Intersectionality: A Conversation About the What and the How of Development. Annual Review of Developmental Psychology 5:169–191.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-devpsych-120321-022756.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: intersectional identities develop earlier than often assumed; relational/cultural context and mixed methods are central.

11. **Collins (2015), Intersectionality's Definitional Dilemmas. Annual Review of Sociology 41:1–20.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-soc-073014-112142.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: intersectionality is not merely additive categories; power relations and inequality are part of the construct.

## Self motives, belonging и exclusion

12. **Leary (2007), Motivational and Emotional Aspects of the Self. Annual Review of Psychology 58:317–344.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.psych.58.110405.085658.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: self-enhancement, self-verification and self-expansion form distinct self-related motive traditions tied to social functioning.

13. **Baek, Pourafshari & Bayer (2025), The four conceptualizations of social connection. Nature Reviews Psychology 4:506–517.** DOI: 10.1038/s44159-025-00455-9.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible article structure.  
    Для карты: “social connection” is not one construct; subjective perception, network structure, synchrony and interpersonal behavior must be separated.

14. **Williams & Nida (2022), Ostracism and social exclusion: Implications for separation, social isolation, and loss. Current Opinion in Psychology 47:101353.** DOI: 10.1016/j.copsyc.2022.101353.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: ostracism/exclusion has short- and long-term trajectories and diverse responses; separate branch from generic belonging.

## Social cognition, reputation, norms and influence

15. **Greenwald & Lai (2020), Implicit Social Cognition. Annual Review of Psychology 71:419–445.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010419-050837.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: indirect measures, implicit–explicit relations and insufficient theory are major methodological/theoretical issues.

16. **Connelly & McAbee (2024), Reputations at Work: Origins and Outcomes of Shared Person Perceptions. Annual Review of Organizational Psychology and Organizational Behavior 11:251–278.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-110721-022320.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible sections.  
    Для карты: reputation integrates person perception, self–other asymmetry, impression management, stereotypes, gossip and culture.

17. **Wenzel & Woodyatt (2025), The Power and Pitfalls of Social Norms. Annual Review of Psychology 76:583–606.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020124-120310.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: injunctive norms involve internalization/enforcement but can produce reactance, reinterpretation and motivational side effects.

18. **Spears (2021), Social Influence and Group Identity. Annual Review of Psychology 72:367–390.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-070620-111818.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible sections.  
    Для карты: group-identity explanation of influence must be compared with normative, interdependence, network and persuasion accounts.

## Relationships, trust, culture, power and stigma

19. **Finkel, Simpson & Eastwick (2017), The Psychology of Close Relationships: Fourteen Core Principles. Annual Review of Psychology 68:383–411.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010416-044038.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible article.  
    Для карты: relationship science is a separate theory-rich discipline spanning interdependence, attachment, context and partner processes.

20. **Schilke, Reimann & Cook (2021), Trust in Social Relations. Annual Review of Sociology 47:239–259.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-soc-082120-082850.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: particularized, categorical and generalized trust need to be distinguished.

21. **Ward & Szabó (2023), Acculturation, cultural identity and well-being. Nature Reviews Psychology 2:267–282.** DOI: 10.1038/s44159-023-00171-2.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: cultural identity is dynamic and shaped at micro, meso and macro levels during acculturation.

22. **Guinote (2017), How Power Affects People: Activating, Wanting, and Goal Seeking. Annual Review of Psychology 68:353–381.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010416-044153.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: power is a distinct social-structural variable affecting self-expression, goals and social attention.

23. **Major & O'Brien (2005), The Social Psychology of Stigma. Annual Review of Psychology 56:393–421.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.psych.56.091103.070137.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: discrimination, stereotypes and identity threat form distinct but interacting processes.

24. **Shnabel & Ullrich (2026), Identity Needs in Intergroup Relations. Annual Review of Psychology 77:285–310.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-022625-112840.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible page structure.  
    Для карты: intergroup identity research includes needs arising from moral/group relations, not only category salience.

## Collective, digital and network contexts

25. **González, Carvacho & Tausch (2025), Psychological processes underlying normative transformation and social change. Nature Reviews Psychology 4:404–416.** DOI: 10.1038/s44159-025-00448-8.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible sections.  
    Для карты: norms are dynamic; conflicts across societal, group and individual levels can drive collective action/social change.

26. **Drury (2026), The Psychology of Crowd Behavior. Annual Review of Psychology 77.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-012125-121447.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: crowds/mass gatherings, emergencies and collective action can strengthen or transform social identity.

27. **Bayer, Triệu & Ellison (2020), Social Media Elements, Ecologies, and Effects. Annual Review of Psychology 71:471–497.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-010419-050944.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full accessible page.  
    Для карты: profiles/self-presentation, networks/mobilization, streams/social comparison and messages/connectedness are distinct platform-mediated social processes.

28. **Simpson & Willer (2015), Beyond Altruism: Sociological Foundations of Cooperation and Prosocial Behavior. Annual Review of Sociology 41:43–63.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-soc-073014-112242.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: rules, reputations and social networks provide external social foundations for cooperation beyond internal altruistic motives.

## Formal counterexamples (constructed, not empirical)
1. Y=X+e, E(e|X)=0, Var(e|X)=1, independent noise. Conditional slope is 1 for both whole and X-only restricted sample. With SD(X)=1, corr(X,Y)=1/sqrt(2)=0.707107; with conditional SD(X)=.25, corr=.25/sqrt(1+.25²)=0.242536. Computed from formula using JS Math.sqrt. A lower correlation need not indicate a lower slope. Assumptions explicitly conditional on selection depending only on X; selecting on outcome/other variables may bias slope too. This is not a correction formula for an observed IQ dataset.
2. True Y*=a+bX, observed Y=min(Y*,c) with b>0 and no residual noise: observed slope b below (c-a)/b and 0 above it. Threshold in observed instrument not evidence of latent saturation; harder uncensored outcome or identified censoring model can distinguish. No claim that all saturation is measurement artifact.
3. A logistic probability can be monotone S-shaped on probability scale with constant log-odds slope; a monotonically increasing log function can have negative quadratic term without reversal. Shape depends on chosen estimand/scale; define what change actually means. An interaction on risk difference scale need not imply one on odds ratio scale. A qualitative reversal in same measured outcome requires evidence within supported range, not extrapolated polynomial turning point.

## Operational model comparison
- Define target population, age at actual measurement, predictor instrument/norm/precision, outcome and scale, time/order, confounders/selection and estimand (conditional mean, quantile/variance, association, latent loading, prediction, causal effect). Draw support distribution and tail counts, distinguish direct adult measured FSIQ from early talent/achievement and self-selected groups. Examine independent measurements and invariance; report uncertainty, do not silently equate score scales.
- Predefine meaningful alternatives: M0 constant, M1 a+bX; M2 monotone concave/convex or bounded saturation when theoretically justified; M3 restricted cubic spline/penalized smooth with bounded complexity; M4 continuous segmented a+bX+d(X-c)_+ versus separate discontinuity model; M5 interactions with measured moderator/context (nonlinear terms as justified). Specify link, direction and practical effect contrast before fitting. Unknown c requires search-adjusted inference and interval. Flexible curves may approximate any shape within data; statistical fit does not uniquely identify a mechanism.
- Examine residual variation, outliers/leverage, floor/ceiling, measurement error, missingness, nonlinear age/cohort effects and conditioning/collider paths. If groups selected on ability-related composite, model selection and test alternative recruitment; cannot recover unobserved population without assumptions. Compare effect/slope contrasts and CIs or credible intervals, calibrated tests and uncertainty around entire curves (simultaneous intervals for selected regions if needed). Absence of statistical significance in tail not equivalence; equivalence needs predeclared practical margin and sufficient precision.
- Compare models on same outcome and target; separate descriptive fit, inference and prediction. Use complexity penalty/appropriate tests, nested CV or untouched holdout, cluster by participant/family/cohort as needed, report tail-stratified error/calibration with uncertainty and sensitivity to knots, transformation, tail cutoffs and selection. Plan sample size by local precision or simulation of actual competing effects under selection/error, not total N alone. A tail excluded in training is an extrapolation challenge, not ordinary interpolation. Restrict conclusions to common support; no universal numerical cutoff or extrapolated individual prediction.
- Transfer to psychological analysis: do not posit qualitatively different motivation, emotion or cognition solely from higher IQ or a curved group plot. Independent observation of task/functional context, repeated evidence and alternatives are required; model uncertainty remains. Reuse GFT-13/14 distinction of ability, context, cost, output and diagnosis; no private data or diagnosis.

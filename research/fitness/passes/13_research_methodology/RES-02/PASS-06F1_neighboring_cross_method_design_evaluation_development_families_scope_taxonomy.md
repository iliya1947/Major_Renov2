# RES-02 / PASS-06F1 — neighboring cross-method design/evaluation/development families: scope and taxonomy

Статус: **COMPLETE — F-BRANCH SCOPE/TAXONOMY ESTABLISHED; DEDICATED DEEP PASSES REQUIRED**  
Дата: 2026-11-17  
Topic: RES-02 — Study designs / what they answer

## Purpose

Этот PASS открывает соседнюю ветку после завершённого mixed-methods synthesis и отвечает не на вопрос «какой из этих подходов лучше», а на более базовый вопрос:

> что здесь является parent methodology, что evaluation/development framework, что consensus method, что systems/modeling method family, а что theory-construction methodology — и какие из этих семейств требуют отдельного глубокого RES-02 pass.

Главная причина отдельной F-ветки: эти подходы часто используют QUAL + QUAN, итерации, моделирование, stakeholder input или несколько фаз, но **не должны автоматически классифицироваться как mixed methods или как обычный primary empirical design**.

# 3. Canonical cross-method router

Для RES-02 соседние семейства нужно разводить минимум по следующим уровням.

## A. EMPIRICAL_PARENT_DESIGN

Уже покрытые ветки:

- randomized / quasi-randomized / quasi-experimental;
- observational;
- diagnostic / measurement;
- prediction;
- mechanistic;
- qualitative;
- mixed methods.

Эти design identities сохраняются даже когда исследование находится внутри framework ниже.

## B. PARENT_METHODOLOGY / EVALUATION_METHODOLOGY

Методология определяет научный объект, логику исследования и тип итогового знания.

Текущие кандидаты:

- Q Methodology;
- Realist Evaluation;
- Design-Based Research;
- Design Science Research;
- Theory Construction Methodology.

## C. DESIGN / DEVELOPMENT / IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK

Framework организует процесс разработки или программу исследований, но сам по себе обычно **не создаёт один конкретный causal/prevalence/prediction estimand**.

Текущие кандидаты:

- HCD/UCD;
- Intervention Mapping;
- Implementation Mapping;
- MRC complex-intervention framework;
- MOST;
- ORBIT;
- 6SQuID;
- Person-Based Approach;
- Behaviour Change Wheel / COM-B-linked intervention design.

## D. STRUCTURED CONSENSUS METHOD / ARCHITECTURE

Научный продукт — структурированное коллективное judgement/priority/appropriateness/definition, а не оценка treatment effect.

Текущие routes:

- Delphi;
- modified Delphi / e-Delphi;
- Nominal Group Technique;
- RAND/UCLA Appropriateness Method;
- consensus conference / formal consensus meeting architectures;
- hybrids, если exact procedure сохранён.

## E. SYSTEMS / MODELING METHOD FAMILY

Не один design label.

Сюда могут входить:

- causal loop diagrams / qualitative system maps;
- system dynamics;
- agent-based modelling;
- network analysis;
- group model building;
- other explicit computational or participatory system models.

## F. METHODS / TECHNIQUES

Интервью, focus groups, surveys, Q-sort, voting, workshops, prototyping, usability tests, simulations, factor analysis и т. п. не должны автоматически подниматься до parent-design level.

# 4. Q Methodology

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: STANDALONE PARENT METHODOLOGY.**

Q Methodology предназначена для систематического исследования субъективных viewpoints.

Constitutive architecture:

1. definition of discourse/concourse;
2. construction of Q-set;
3. participant Q-sort / rank ordering;
4. correlation among persons/Q-sorts;
5. by-person factor analysis;
6. factor arrays;
7. interpretive construction of shared viewpoints.

Current healthcare literature продолжает описывать Q как methodology for subjectivity; current 2025 work отдельно формализует Q-sample construction.

## Critical boundaries

- Q-sort alone ≠ full Q Methodology.
- Factor analysis of variables ≠ Q factor analysis of persons/sorts.
- Q may use qualitative and quantitative operations, but project route remains **Q_METHODOLOGY**, not generic MMR.
- Factor/viewpoint frequency in a purposive Q sample ≠ population prevalence.
- Author label “qualitative”, “mixed”, “hybrid” or “qualiquantological” must be stored separately from enacted architecture.

## Exercise relevance

Direct examples exist in exercise/physical-activity research, including viewpoints on exercise enjoyment in arthritis and current physical-education/physical-activity guideline work.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED.**

Reason: unique sampling logic, Q-set provenance, factor extraction/rotation, factor interpretation and prevalence boundary cannot be safely represented by generic qualitative/mixed routes.

Confidence: **HIGH** for taxonomy.

# 5. Realist Evaluation

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: STANDALONE THEORY-DRIVEN EVALUATION METHODOLOGY.**

RAMESES literature frames realist evaluation around:

- programme theory;
- context;
- mechanism;
- outcome;
- context–mechanism–outcome configurations;
- testing/refining explanations of what works, for whom, in what circumstances, how and why.

It is method-neutral: interviews, documents, quantitative data, observations or mixed evidence may be used according to theory-testing/refinement needs.

## Critical boundaries

- Realist Evaluation ≠ mixed methods by definition.
- Realist mechanism ≠ statistical mediator.
- Realist mechanism ≠ automatically a biological mechanism.
- CMOC ≠ conventional causal effect estimand.
- Realist synthesis/review is an evidence-synthesis route and remains outside primary-study taxonomy.
- RAMESES II = reporting standard/guidance, not the evaluation design itself.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED.**

Reason: programme-theory logic, realist interviewing/sampling/analysis, CMOC construction/refinement and claim boundaries require independent routing.

Confidence: **HIGH**.

# 6. Design-Based Research (DBR)

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: PARENT DESIGN/RESEARCH METHODOLOGY FOR ITERATIVE DESIGN-IN-CONTEXT.**

The Design-Based Research Collective defined DBR around theory-driven design of learning environments in authentic settings, with design both as intervention and as a route to theoretical understanding.

Later health-professions/education literature retains recurring features:

- authentic context;
- collaboration;
- theory-informed design;
- iterative analysis/exploration;
- design/construction;
- evaluation/reflection;
- practical and theoretical outputs.

## Critical boundaries

- DBR ≠ one-cycle pilot.
- DBR ≠ “we iteratively improved something”.
- Prototype refinement alone ≠ DBR unless linked to explicit research/theory contribution.
- DBR may use QUAL, QUAN or mixed methods; this does not replace the DBR parent route.
- Effectiveness claims still depend on embedded empirical evaluation design.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED.**

Reason: iteration, design principles, theory contribution, local adaptation and embedded evaluation require a distinct architecture.

Confidence: **HIGH**.

---

# 7. Design Science Research (DSR)

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: PARENT DESIGN-SCIENCE RESEARCH METHODOLOGY / PARADIGM, DISTINCT FROM DBR.**

Hevner et al. describe design science as creation and rigorous evaluation of artifacts that extend human/organizational capabilities.

Peffers et al. DSRM gives a six-step nominal process:

1. problem identification and motivation;
2. objectives for a solution;
3. design and development;
4. demonstration;
5. evaluation;
6. communication.

Artifact forms can include constructs, models, methods and instantiations.

## Critical boundaries

- DSR ≠ generic software development.
- Building an app ≠ research contribution by itself.
- User testing ≠ sufficient design-science evaluation by itself.
- Artifact utility/quality/effectiveness must be linked to explicit evaluation.
- DSR and DBR overlap in iteration but have different methodological lineages and typical scientific products.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED**, separate from DBR.

Reason: artifact ontology, knowledge contribution, demonstration/evaluation logic and DSRM routing are distinctive.

Confidence: **HIGH**.

---

# 8. Human-Centered / User-Centered Design

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: DESIGN PROCESS / DEVELOPMENT FRAMEWORK AND METHOD FAMILY — NOT AUTOMATICALLY A PARENT EMPIRICAL STUDY DESIGN.**

ISO 9241-210:2019 remains current after confirmation in 2025 and specifies human-centred design principles and activities across the lifecycle of interactive systems.

HCD health literature emphasizes:

- understanding users/context;
- stakeholder participation;
- iterative design;
- prototyping;
- evaluation/refinement;
- workflow/system fit.

Current physical-activity/exercise applications verify direct relevance to exercise interventions and exercise-support technologies.

## Critical boundaries

- HCD ≠ mixed methods.
- HCD ≠ co-design by definition; co-design is one possible participatory architecture.
- Usability ≠ clinical effectiveness.
- User satisfaction ≠ behavior change.
- Interviews + prototype testing do not create a new causal design.
- Parent empirical evaluations must remain visible.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED**, with UCD/HCD/co-design/PBA boundary.

Confidence: **HIGH** for framework-level routing.

# 9. Delphi

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: STRUCTURED ITERATIVE CONSENSUS METHOD / CONSENSUS-STUDY FAMILY.**

Core architecture typically includes:

- selected panel;
- multiple rounds;
- individual ratings/responses;
- controlled/anonymized feedback;
- opportunity to revise ratings;
- explicit consensus rule.

CREDES documented major heterogeneity in conduct/reporting. Current meta-research still finds large variation in panel size and consensus thresholds.

## Critical boundaries

- Delphi ≠ survey because it uses iterative feedback/consensus architecture.
- Delphi ≠ NGT.
- “modified Delphi” is not one standardized design; modifications must be recorded.
- Consensus ≠ truth.
- Expert agreement ≠ empirical treatment effect.
- Panel composition, attrition, feedback and stopping rules are methodology-level fields.

Current sport science uses Delphi directly for definitions and professional/practical recommendations.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED.**

Confidence: **HIGH**.

---

# 10. Nominal Group Technique

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: STRUCTURED GROUP IDEA-GENERATION / PRIORITIZATION / CONSENSUS METHOD.**

Classic NGT commonly uses:

1. silent idea generation;
2. round-robin sharing;
3. clarification/discussion;
4. individual voting/ranking.

It is usually face-to-face or synchronous and does not rely on Delphi-style repeated anonymous rounds.

## Critical boundaries

- NGT ≠ focus group.
- NGT ≠ Delphi.
- Discussion may inform judgement, but final ranking/voting structure matters.
- Lack of anonymity creates a different bias structure from Delphi.
- Consensus/priority output ≠ prevalence or causal evidence.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED**, likely adjacent to broader consensus architecture pass.

Confidence: **HIGH**.

# 11. Structured consensus architectures

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: PURPOSE-LEVEL UMBRELLA; EXACT CONSENSUS METHOD MUST BE STORED.**

ACCORD 2024 is explicitly designed to cover consensus exercises broadly and requires authors to report the exact method and modifications.

Important routes include:

- Delphi;
- modified Delphi/e-Delphi;
- NGT;
- RAND/UCLA Appropriateness Method;
- consensus meetings/conferences;
- hybrids.

RAND/UCLA is not merely “another Delphi”: it combines evidence review, individual ratings, structured group discussion and rerating using a specific appropriateness architecture.

## Critical boundaries

- ACCORD = reporting guideline, not consensus method.
- “Expert panel” is insufficient classification.
- “Consensus statement” is a product, not a study design.
- Formal consensus must not substitute for available high-certainty empirical evidence without explicit rationale.
- Consensus criteria must be prespecified/reported where applicable.

## Deep-pass decision

After Delphi and NGT, **DEDICATED STRUCTURED-CONSENSUS CROSSWALK REQUIRED**, especially for RAND/UCLA and modified/hybrid routes.

Confidence: **HIGH**.

# 12. Intervention Mapping

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: INTERVENTION-DEVELOPMENT / PLANNING FRAMEWORK.**

The official Intervention Mapping site states that the current 5th edition appeared in 2026.

Current six-step architecture:

1. needs/assets assessment or problem analysis;
2. matrices of change objectives;
3. theory-based methods → practical applications;
4. organized intervention program;
5. adoption/implementation/sustainability planning;
6. evaluation planning.

## Critical boundaries

- IM ≠ intervention efficacy design.
- IM ≠ mixed methods.
- Needs assessment can contain multiple empirical designs.
- Theory-based method ≠ delivery strategy.
- Completing IM does not prove intervention efficacy.
- Step 6 plans evaluation; it does not replace the evaluation design.

Direct current physical-activity applications verify strong relevance.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED.**

Confidence: **HIGH**.

# 13. Implementation Mapping

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: IMPLEMENTATION-STRATEGY DEVELOPMENT / PLANNING FRAMEWORK.**

Fernandez et al. define five iterative tasks:

1. implementation needs assessment + adopters/implementers;
2. adoption/implementation outcomes, performance objectives, determinants and change objectives;
3. theoretical change methods + implementation strategies;
4. protocols/materials;
5. implementation outcome evaluation.

It extends Intervention Mapping with implementation-science constructs.

## Critical boundaries

- Implementation Mapping ≠ implementation-effectiveness trial.
- Implementation strategy ≠ clinical intervention.
- Determinant assessment ≠ evidence that the selected strategy works.
- Logic model/mechanism hypothesis ≠ established causal mechanism.
- HEI Type I/II/III remains a separate trial-purpose axis.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED**, separate from IM but with explicit crosswalk.

Confidence: **HIGH**.

# 14. Systems modelling / system mapping

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: HETEROGENEOUS SYSTEMS-SCIENCE METHOD FAMILY — NOT ONE ATOMIC DESIGN.**

Systems-science literature distinguishes at least:

- system dynamics;
- agent-based modelling;
- network analysis.

Current public-health methodology also uses:

- causal loop diagrams;
- qualitative system maps;
- group model building;
- participatory community-based system dynamics.

## Critical boundary: map vs model

A causal loop diagram or participatory system map can be a conceptual/qualitative representation.

A calibrated simulation model can make quantitatively testable claims.

These are not the same evidence object.

## Additional boundaries

- systems language ≠ systems method;
- causal arrows in a map ≠ identified causal effects;
- stakeholder-generated link ≠ empirical effect estimate;
- ABM assumptions ≠ observed mechanisms;
- model fit/calibration/validation/sensitivity must be separated;
- network analysis has distinct data/estimand logic from system dynamics;
- GMB is a participatory modeling process, not one universal statistical model.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED**, but this family is too large for one final deep pass.

Initial structural pass should decide whether to split at minimum into:

1. qualitative/system mapping + CLD/GMB;
2. system dynamics;
3. agent-based modelling;
4. network analysis;
5. computational model validation/uncertainty crosswalk.

Confidence: **HIGH** for non-atomic classification.

# 15. Theory Construction Methodology (TCM)

## Taxonomic decision

**Route: THEORY-CONSTRUCTION METHODOLOGY.**

Borsboom et al. 2021 define five steps:

1. identify relevant empirical phenomena;
2. formulate a prototheory;
3. develop a formal model;
4. check explanatory adequacy of the model;
5. evaluate overall theory quality.

The starting point is a set of phenomena; the intended product is an explanatory theory. A 2026 network-theory extension confirms active methodological development of this line.

## Critical boundaries

- TCM ≠ hypothesis-testing design.
- Formal model ≠ automatically empirically validated model.
- Reproducing phenomena in simulation ≠ proving causal truth of the theory.
- Theory construction and empirical theory testing should be stored separately.
- TCM can consume evidence from many parent designs without replacing them.

## Deep-pass decision

**DEDICATED PASS REQUIRED.**

Confidence: **HIGH**.

# 16. Additional neighboring development families found in the scope scan

The initial E9 list was not exhaustive.

## MRC complex-intervention framework

Current framework organizes complex-intervention research across:

- development;
- feasibility;
- evaluation;
- implementation,

with core elements including context, programme theory, stakeholders, uncertainties, refinement and economic considerations.

**Route:** higher-order research-program framework, not atomic study design.

## MOST

Multiphase Optimization Strategy is a distinct optimization framework.

Current architecture:

- preparation;
- optimization;
- evaluation.

Optimization often uses factorial/fractional-factorial or other randomized experimentation and a prespecified optimization criterion.

**Route:** intervention-optimization framework + embedded experimental designs.

Important crosswalk required with PASS-02 and PASS-06C9.

## ORBIT

Behavioral treatment-development model with staged progression, milestones and return to earlier phases for refinement.

**Route:** phased treatment-development framework.

## 6SQuID

Six-step pragmatic intervention-development framework from causal/context understanding through mechanism, delivery, testing/adaptation and evidence before full evaluation.

**Route:** development framework.

## Person-Based Approach

Current 2025 scoping review confirms a mature intervention-development methodology/approach emphasizing iterative qualitative understanding of target users and integration with theory/evidence.

**Route:** intervention-development approach/framework, especially relevant to digital/hybrid behavior-change interventions.

## Behaviour Change Wheel / COM-B

BCW organizes intervention functions and policy categories around COM-B.

**Route:** intervention-design framework/model; not an empirical study design.

### F1 decision on these additional families

They are **not ignored**, but they should not each automatically become a new RES-02 “study design”.

A later dedicated **intervention-development framework crosswalk** is required to compare:

- MOST;
- ORBIT;
- 6SQuID;
- PBA;
- BCW/COM-B;
- Intervention Mapping;
- HCD/UCD,

while preserving embedded empirical designs.

## Evidence preparation

E1 Ackerman & Rolfhus 1999 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10403718/ : 135 middle-aged adults, 20 domain knowledge tests. Cross-sectional design does not establish longitudinal gains. Full effects unverified.
E2 von Stumm & Ackerman 2013 https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030746 : 112 studies, 236 coefficients, N60,097, average association .30, range 0–.58 across trait/outcome measures. Heterogeneity and association not causal effects of intellectual investment. Exact moderator analyses not checked.
E3 Sitzmann & Ely 2011 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21401218/ : 430 studies/effects as reported k, N90,380; goal level, persistence, effort and self-efficacy jointly accounted for 17% of learning variance controlling ability and pretraining knowledge. Planning, monitoring, help seeking and emotion control did not show significant relationships in reported synthesis. This does not establish uselessness of those processes across contexts; measures, overlap and causal direction need scrutiny.
E4 Wai 2014 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intell.2013.08.009 : prospective SMPY and Project Talent plus retrospective data; educational degrees and occupational indicators are not direct tests of strategy or expertise mechanism. SMPY overlaps prior GFT units; do not count as independent replication of same cohort.
E5 Lubinski & Benbow 2006 https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6916.2006.00019.x : indexed author abstract, developmental cohorts >5,000; abilities/interests/values relevant to scientific careers. Not adult IQ remeasurement or direct strategy evidence.
E6 Macnamara et al. 2014 https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614535810 : reported variance associated with deliberate practice differs by domain (26% games, 21% music, 18% sports, 4% education, <1% professions). Not causal percentages or individual ceilings. Definition dispute: Ericsson et al. response https://doi.org/10.1007/s00426-020-01368-3 indexed publisher excerpt; practice operationalization and coaching/feedback criteria differ. Need full comparison before strong conclusion. Hambrick et al. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intell.2013.04.001 indexed abstract supports non-sufficiency of practice within selected domains.
E7 Ackerman & Beier 2006, Determinants of Domain Knowledge and Independent Study Learning in an Adult Sample: indexed author abstract from university-hosted paper; N141 ages18–69; crystallized intelligence stronger predictor of initial financial knowledge than fluid intelligence, nonability traits incrementally predictive; fluid intelligence prediction of post-learning knowledge increased but did not exceed crystallized. Candidate for direct HTML source and gain vs posttest analysis; PDF not opened/analyzed.
E8 Tail search retrieved adult WAIS profiles and adult gifted review, not validated longitudinal self-learning strategy studies for measured adult IQ150–160+. Q-08-tail remains open; no absence-of-literature claim.

## Provisional practical gate
Separate baseline ability and knowledge from actual improvement, delayed retention, near/far transfer, quality of feedback, available time, opportunity and task choice. Test rival models: prior exposure, domain interest, strategy quality, motivation, support, measurement, selection. A high posttest score does not by itself establish high learning gain; a quick solution to familiar tasks does not establish transferable strategy. Repeated observations and matched unfamiliar tasks can discriminate hypotheses without identifying IQ or unique causal mechanism. No attribution of all outcomes to intelligence or practice.

## 9. Anatomy vs biomechanics / technique / pathology boundary

### Anatomy

### Biomechanics

### Technique
squat/hinge/lunge stance, toe angle, depth, ROM strategy.

### Medicine
FAI, dysplasia, labral pathology, SIJ pain, tendon injury.

PASS-01 preserves these boundaries.

## Independent mathematical counterexample (synthetic, not empirical data)
Let X and Y be standardized Gaussian variables with positive correlation r, 0<r<1. Independent standard normals U,V,W. Model A: X=U; Y=rX+sqrt(1-r^2)V. Model B: Y=U; X=rY+sqrt(1-r^2)V. Model C: X=sqrt(r)U+sqrt(1-r)V; Y=sqrt(r)U+sqrt(1-r)W. All generate zero means, unit variances and covariance r, hence the same bivariate Gaussian distribution, but manipulating X changes Y in A (slope r), not in B or C under the stipulated structural equations. Algebra check: A/B variances r^2+(1-r^2)=1; C variance r+(1-r)=1 and covariance r. This demonstrates nonidentification for these models even with perfect measurement and infinite observations. It does not prove every causal question is unidentifiable. A genuine well-defined intervention or additional defensible assumptions can discriminate; predictive fit alone cannot.
## Operational method (PRACTICAL_HEURISTIC; integrate INT-02/03/04)
1. Purpose -> proposition -> time/context -> data quality -> claim type. Define effect of what change, on what outcome, in which conditions and at what horizon. Distinguish observable pattern, explanatory construct, causal arrow, intervention, and mechanism. A trait score or diagnosis is not a causal node unless the proposed process and measurement are specified. Labeling a behavior with a synonym does not explain it.
2. Construct minimal rival architectures, not a maximal list: X→Y; Y→X; X←U→Y; X→M→Y; context Z modifies effect; X→S←Y selection; measurement/response artifacts. These can coexist. If no causal evidence, use undirected/uncertain links; each edge records provenance, temporal scale, status and disconfirming conditions. No forced choice among observationally equivalent rivals. Repeated anecdotes from one source are dependent evidence.
3. Match evidence to edge: direction requires temporal order plus identification assumptions, not merely lag; within-person associations are not individual causal effects. For total effect under a defensible DAG, block appropriate backdoor paths, not causal mediators or colliders/descendants; for direct effect define separate estimand and identification assumptions. In time-varying confounding affected by prior exposure ordinary adjustment can be wrong: specialist longitudinal methods required. In practice qualitative personal analysis usually lacks identification; state hypotheses not coefficients.
4. Use a time-indexed loop X_t→Y_(t+1)→X_(t+2); competing common external driver and measurement schedule remain possible. Lag interval is substantive, not a software default. CLPM/RI-CLPM target different relationships; neither proves a person's mechanism. Check level (biological, cognitive, motivational, emotional, behavior, social context), operational link and time; parallel descriptions at different levels need not compete, but cannot stand in for one another. No neural inference from self-report/IQ.
5. Compare models against pre-specified discriminating observations. Simpler model M0 must use same target and information; M1 earns extra nodes only if it accounts for a relevant anomaly, predicts new observations, or clarifies a consequential mechanism with independent support. Reproducing fitted pattern or compelling narrative is necessary for some explanatory goals, not sufficient. A simple model may be adequate for prediction while mechanism remains uncertain; a more complex model may have greater explanatory reach but uncertain parameters. Do not assert universal optimum or use fit indices across incomparable data/targets. Do not demand formal mathematics for every low-stakes insight; use explicit conditional predictions and minimally necessary evidence.
6. Revision: locate earliest failing premise/edge; verify whether new observation contradicts a necessary prediction or merely reduces likelihood given measurement, context and auxiliary assumptions. Preserve observations and independent supported edges; retract/downgrade dependents, compare rival architecture including possibility of no established relation. Don't protect favored theory with unlimited post-hoc moderators; propose revised test before new observation. If observationally equivalent, record ambiguity and stop or escalate only if utility warrants. No invasive testing, risky experiments or diagnostic/treatment claims.
7. Practical output: map supported patterns, tentative connections, genuinely discriminating conditions, consequences for self-understanding. Separate what is known, what is plausible, and what remains unknown. Preserve contextual variation rather than flattening it into one trait. Revisit GFT-19.1 model illustration: unrestricted person fixed intercepts absorb time-invariant person means, so its b_B term cannot be separately identified alongside unrestricted a_i. Between-person term requires suitable random-intercept/multilevel assumptions or separate between-person analysis. This is an identification clarification, not a change to general guardrail.
## Internal transfer check (all cases invented independently of any real person)
A. Fictional pottery class: people with more practice sessions report greater enjoyment; investigator infers practice causes enjoyment. Alternatives enjoyment→practice, prior interest→both, attendance selection; same cross-sectional distribution cannot orient. New: an externally assigned practice opportunity increases actual practice but no detectable enjoyment change, estimate imprecise and intervention also changes peer contact. Weaken a strong large practice-only effect in this setting if interval rules it out; otherwise ambiguity remains. Don't claim null equivalence or isolated mechanism; keep observed association and separate assignment effect from actual practice. Discriminating evidence: adequately measured contrast with time order, comparable contacts and uncertainty, only if ethically useful.
B. Fictional amateur orchestra: confidence at rehearsal predicts next rehearsal fluency and vice versa in pooled panel. Alternatives stable expertise, conductor changes, reciprocal feedback, shared rating. Need within-person time-index, appropriate lag, direct fluency ratings, context. New: separating stable differences removes positive lagged confidence coefficient but fluency→confidence remains imprecise; objective timing and self-report collected on different dates. Downgrade reciprocal claim; do not declare absence of feedback or reverse causal effect. Compare model/estimand, timing and reliability; preserve level-specific observations, avoid using IQ or personality label.
C. Fictional community garden: analyst explains fewer missed watering shifts by complex mechanism involving intention, planning, peer approval and memory. Simpler model: visible schedule cue makes reminders accessible; more complex model predicts benefit even if cue absent when planning changes. New: logs show cue introduced only after missed shifts, and both cue and completion depend on shift coordinator; when coordinator absent, cue alone does not reliably improve completion. Revise architecture to possible coordinator→cue and coordinator→completion and feedback from misses; separate schedule visibility from actual completion and motivation, test cue independently in comparable conditions if warranted. Preserve completion pattern, not original causal sequence. Selection example: analyzing only volunteers praised for either perfect attendance or high skill can induce association; avoid conditioning on that common effect. No attribution of failure to lack of motivation.

# Источники аудита области 10 — восстановление

Дата аудита: 2026-11-16.

1. NSCA — Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning, 5th Edition  
   https://dxpprod.nsca.com/essentials-strength/

   Chapter 24: Overreaching, Overtraining, and Recovery:
   - overreaching/overtraining continuum;
   - contributing factors;
   - underperformance;
   - physiological mechanisms;
   - assessment;
   - recovery methods/strategies.

2. NSCA — Understanding the Risks of Overtraining Syndrome (2026)  
   https://www.nsca.com/certification/tsac-f/essentials-of-tactical-strength-training-and-conditioning-2nd-edition/excerpts/understand-the-risks-of-overtraining-syndrome/

   Использовано для total-stress framing: training load + nutrition + sleep + psychological/external stressors.

3. ACSM — A Road Map to Effective Muscle Recovery  
   https://www.acsm.org/docs/default-source/files-for-resource-library/a-road-map-to-effective-muscle-recovery.pdf

   Использовано как broad recovery-method inventory, но не как окончательный источник сравнительной эффективности.

4. ACSM — 2026 Resistance Training Guidelines  
   https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/

   Использовано для актуальной рамки consistency/individualization and avoiding unnecessary complexity.

## Ограничение

Эффективность recovery modalities должна исследоваться отдельно: субъективное восстановление, soreness, acute performance и long-term adaptation — разные outcomes.

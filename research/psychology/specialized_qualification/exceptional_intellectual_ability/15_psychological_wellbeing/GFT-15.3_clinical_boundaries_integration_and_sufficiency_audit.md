## Integrated practical procedure (PRACTICAL_HEURISTIC)
1. Purpose gate: clarify requested self-understanding vs clinical concern, avoid unnecessary inquiry. Identify observation, context/time, goal and whether trait/state, capacity, outcome, explanation or prediction. IQ score only if relevant and verified; distinguish absolute normed function, within-person change and relative-to-ability discrepancy. Unusual preference, solitude, intensity or high ability alone is not pathology; distress deserves attention without a diagnostic label.
2. Separate channels: exposure/appraisal, affect/meaning, symptom report, actual functioning across chosen domains, support and strategy, effort/cost, recovery trajectory and immediate safety. Good work output does not measure all functioning or wellbeing; low wellbeing does not establish disorder. Distinguish occupational burnout definition from broader fatigue or research constructs; no diagnosis by questionnaire, IQ or discrepancy.
3. Compare minimal context model with rival or coexisting explanations: workload/task fit, voluntary preference, access and social context, sleep/medical factors, transient stress, prior history/2e, strategy cost, or clinically significant condition. A new abrupt change, persistent severe distress or functional loss calls for appropriate qualified evaluation independent of intellectual status. If imminent safety threat, prioritize immediate local emergency or crisis support over model analysis, per SAF. No diagnosis or treatment prescription. Do not assume subjective experience is false because a group mean differs.
4. Test proportionately: naturally varying ordinary situations, baseline and timing, relevant independent observations, distinct outcomes; do not intentionally induce distress, remove effective or essential supports, or demand private disclosure. Benefits of reducing exposure and changing response are both legitimate. Association, moderation, mediation and causal effects need distinct designs; avoid post-hoc rescue. Preserve strengths and actual supported performance while considering cost. If additional information cannot change useful understanding or appropriate action, stop.
5. Revise per INT-02/03, GFT-19.1–19.3: trace independent roots and premises, update dependent claims, allow concurrent explanations and boundary changes, preserve observations that survive. Separate confidence and use permission. A person can need care even when mechanism uncertain; stakes determine action not truth. No personal data in public cabinet.

A. Fictional glass conservator reports low social interest and high task satisfaction, chooses time alone and has stable functioning. A colleague infers pathology from extreme ability and few friends. Compare preference versus unwanted isolation and context; no diagnosis or inference of IQ from output. Update2: fatigue persists after return to a better-supported schedule and new concentration loss extends beyond work; do not apply occupational label to all symptoms; preserve aid benefit and seek qualified assessment of persistent/new change.

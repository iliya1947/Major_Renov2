## 2. Современные компоненты физической подготовленности

В актуальной рамке ACSM 2026:

1. Cardiorespiratory fitness.
2. Muscular fitness:
   - strength;
   - muscular endurance;
   - power.
3. Body composition:
   - fat mass;
   - fat-free mass.
4. Flexibility.
5. Neuromotor fitness:
   - agility;
   - balance;
   - gait speed.

Компоненты взаимосвязаны; старое жёсткое разделение «health-related vs performance-related» не использовать как единственную современную рамку.

Исходная версия covered movement patterns, cardiorespiratory and anaerobic fitness, concurrent training, mobility and the need to expand beyond arms. Audit added a formal whole-fitness framework:

11. sedentary behavior/NEAT;

13. general preparation vs specialization;
14. minimal complete long-term coverage;
15. staged expansion from arm priority.

ACSM's August 2026 roundtable now defines five interconnected fitness components: cardiorespiratory fitness, muscular fitness, body composition, flexibility and neuromotor fitness, retiring the old strict health/performance split. Current 2026 concurrent-training umbrella evidence indicates that in healthy/recreationally trained populations, combined resistance and endurance training can improve aerobic and strength-related outcomes without a general large penalty to strength, hypertrophy or power compared with resistance training alone, though context and highly trained populations still require caution.

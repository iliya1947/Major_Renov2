# 06 — Нейронаука и нейропластичность: накопленное понимание A1

**Только структура поля. Никакой биологический механизм здесь не предлагается как механизм GPT.**

## После первого прохода

1. **Plasticity is a family, not one update rule.** На разных уровнях изменяются synaptic efficacy, intrinsic excitability, structure, myelin/glia, gene expression and network organization.

2. **Ability to change itself changes.** Metaplasticity, homeostasis and sensitive periods make future plasticity dependent on prior history/state. This is conceptually distinct from storing a particular memory.

3. **Biological learning contains stabilizing processes as well as change processes.** Homeostatic mechanisms can counterbalance destabilizing Hebbian changes; plasticity and stability therefore cannot be treated as opposites where “more plastic = better”.

4. **Memory trace, learning rule and system selection are separate problems.** Synaptic update does not explain which ensemble stores the representation or which memory system controls behavior.

5. **Different neural systems can learn in parallel and compete/cooperate.** Stress/context can bias which system controls behavior. Therefore a behavioral change does not identify one underlying memory mechanism.

6. **Offline/reorganization processes matter.** Sleep, replay and systems consolidation imply that neural consequences of experience continue after immediate interaction, but the theories of exactly what is consolidated/transformed remain contested.

7. **Development constrains plasticity.** Sensitive-period literature shows that the same input at different developmental stages can have different consequences, while adult plasticity does not disappear completely.

8. **Macroscopic network change is not automatically microscopic learning.** Network reconfiguration may correlate with cognition/learning while its structural cellular basis remains unresolved.

9. **Human neuroscience has a translation problem.** Precise causal work often comes from animals/cells; human measures are coarser. This must become part of the future evidence audit, not an afterthought.

10. **For our eventual AI synthesis, the useful level will likely be abstract constraints, not biological imitation.** Candidates to revisit only after all A1/A2 maps: multiple timescales, gating, history-dependent plasticity, stability–plasticity tradeoffs, competing subsystems, allocation and selective persistence. None is yet a design rule.

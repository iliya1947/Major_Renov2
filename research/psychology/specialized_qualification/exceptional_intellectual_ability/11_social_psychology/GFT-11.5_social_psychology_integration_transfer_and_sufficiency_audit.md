## Contradiction and dependence audit
A. Ability and some decoding/communication task outcomes can correlate without a stable global social-accuracy trait. Task-specific verbal demands and ceiling/criterion problems are alternative explanations, not reasons to declare all observed associations spurious or invent hidden superiority. Eyal's experiment concerns acquisition of information, not proof of a universal intervention or ability deficit.
B. Empathy self-report and helping may covary while behavior depends on resources, context and efficacy. A reasoning/cooperation association does not establish compassion or morality; an empathy/giving association does not establish a gifted phenotype. The Hornsey measured-vs-manipulated effectiveness contrast cautions about causal inference and heterogeneous operationalizations.
C. IQ/task-performance association is compatible with null IQ-discrepancy/satisfaction association: different outcomes, roles, distributions, selection stages. An intelligence-match preference under a vignette does not establish actual rejection. Established dyads conditioned on both selection and persistence cannot estimate unmet opportunities for forming relationships. Group average cannot establish a particular person's needs. Absence of detected difference is neither equivalence nor proof that a difference exists.
D. Selected gifted group distress, differing attachment or conflict patterns, and similar average relationship quality can all coexist. Differential recruitment and life context matter. Distinguish clinically assessed impairment from unwanted isolation and freely chosen solitude; do not infer from number of friends. No deterministic gifted adaptation profile.
E. Dependency: 11.2's EI evidence is inherited from 10.2; gifted adult and Denissen sources recur in 08, 11.3/11.4; repeated abstracts and reviews may share primary samples. Do not count citations as independent replications or pool dissimilar constructs. No systematic primary-study overlap matrix or full source status/correction audit completed. ## Transfer protocol (conditional hypotheses, not a validated unified causal model)
1. Define purpose and outcome: accurate perception, sharing/concern, helpful action, semantic accuracy, conflict, closeness, participation, loneliness or functioning. State whether a claim is observation, self/other report, proxy, inference or causal hypothesis.
2. Decompose encounter into actor, partner, shared/unequal knowledge, opportunity, affordances, roles, stakes, goal, cue availability and time. Hypothesize links from available information/processing to inferred meaning, appraisal, motivation, regulation, action, partner response and feedback; allow parallel, recursive and missing links. Relationship history shapes interpretation; outcome can alter later motivation and access. No mandatory sequence or single latent cause. Integrate GFT-05/06 (resources/calibration), 08 (development/context), 09 (interest/effort), 10 (emotion/response).
3. Compare simple local explanations before global profile: missing premise, inaccessible cue, conflicting goals, differing expertise, workload, unfamiliar context, actual skill limitation, reciprocal response, preference and opportunity. If warranted, compare more complex interactions across contexts; a profile label adds no explanation unless it predicts a distinct observation.
4. Define discriminating prospective observation: criterion-based paraphrase or application, voluntary clarification of reported intent, prediction of a later observable action, compare context/role/knowledge, outcome of requested support and self-reported needs. Preserve refusals and privacy; no covert testing, engineered distress or inferred IQ/diagnosis. Distinguish report from independently observed outcome; people may not know or disclose motives, agreement is not comprehension, corroboration is not certainty.
5. Update only affected propositions: keep verified behavior, revise the explanation and all downstream predictions; downgrade confidence if target criterion is weak; preserve alternative mechanisms if new information is nondiscriminating. Avoid confirmation-driven sampling and treating repeated correlated observations as independent. Use confidence qualitatively unless calibrated data justify probability. No diagnostic or psychometric use without validated assessment and qualified clinician.
6. Applicability gate: general adult evidence supports conditional hypotheses and measurement caution; selected youth/adult samples do not give numerical priors for adult IQ150–160+. Ask whether instrument version, population, precision, criterion and context match. If not, withhold tail-specific claim; general methods may still be used. Route persistent functional issues to GFT-12–15, tail to 16–17, cross-domain integration to 18–20. Never export private observations.

## New integration cases (invented before scoring, not independent)
A. Fictional observatory volunteer correctly solves technical coordination problems, predicts a visitor will leave because of disappointment, and offers detailed scientific reassurance. Visitor leaves and volunteer claims accurate empathy and successful help. Separate correct observable prediction from motive, reasoning competence, affective response, offered help and actual benefit. Alternatives: visitor's transport deadline, concern about accessibility, or disappointment. Distinct predictions: direct voluntary clarification of purpose or subsequent attendance under different transport conditions; compare whether requested information was delivered. New evidence: visitor reports last bus deadline and returns the next week asking about wheelchair access; technical explanation did not answer access question. Withdraw disappointment attribution and help success, retain departure forecast and technical ability; preserve uncertainty about mixed feelings. Revise future information-seeking prediction, not global social deficit or superiority. A simpler missing-information account explains this incident better than a general ability model.
B. Fictional amateur orchestra pair accurately paraphrase each other's proposals, one responds calmly to distress by arranging rehearsal cover, the other reports strong feelings but wants a different repertoire. They describe low closeness despite reliable logistics. Analyst labels emotional detachment and assumes cognitive mismatch. Alternative: different goals and preferences for emotional acknowledgment, time constraints, or specific conflict; separate semantic comprehension, affect sharing, concern, support outcome, disagreement and closeness. Prediction: if lack of understanding is central, explicit paraphrase and application should fail; if preference/response mismatch, understanding can be accurate yet closeness changes when desired acknowledgment becomes available. New: both confirm comprehension, support is useful, calm member reports concern, repeated mutually chosen check-ins improve reported connection without change in repertoire disagreement. Drop comprehension deficit and affect-to-concern inference; preserve preference conflict and observed support; responsiveness plausible, not causal proof. Distinguish reported intimacy from verified action. No IQ inference or relationship prescription.
C. Fictional community library program compares high-score volunteers recruited via an online support group with unscreened residents. High-score group performs better on verbal mentalizing vignettes, reports fewer friends and higher loneliness, but makes equal donations. Analyst infers gifted hyperempathy with unavoidable isolation and moral equivalence. Alternative: verbal expertise/test characteristics, ascertainment through support group, fewer available opportunities, distinct desired network sizes, donation resource differences. Discriminators: representative recruitment and actual criterion measures with adequate range, independent ability assessment, repeated context and support ratings, matched resource/opportunity data. New: recruitment through a general community list yields overlapping loneliness ratings; nonverbal task with broad score range shows mixed target-dependent effects; within-person loneliness rises after venue closure and falls after reopening while IQ estimates unchanged. Withdraw inevitable social isolation and global empathy/morality claims; keep original selected-sample association only, no equivalence or unique mechanism. Context/selection are supported alternatives, not proof of full mediation. Need separately measured extreme-tail adults to infer specific effects.

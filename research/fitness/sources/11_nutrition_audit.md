# Источники аудита области 11 — питание и эргогенные средства

Дата аудита: 2026-11-16.

## Основные источники

1. NSCA — Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning, 5th Edition  
   https://dxpprod.nsca.com/essentials-strength/

   Nutrition coverage is separated into:
   - Chapter 10: Basic Nutritional Factors Affecting Health;
   - Chapter 11: Nutrition Strategies for Maximizing Performance;
   - Chapter 12: Performance-Enhancing Substances and Methods.

   Major scope:
   macronutrients, vitamins/minerals, fluids/electrolytes, pre/during/post-event nutrition, body-composition strategies, RED-S, feeding/eating disorders, supplements/hormones.

2. IOC — 2023 Consensus Statement on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs)  
   https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/17/1073

   Used for:
   - problematic low energy availability;
   - health/performance consequences;
   - male and female athletes;
   - individual variability;
   - body-composition and eating-behavior safety;
   - distinction between screening/risk and clinical diagnosis.

3. NIH Office of Dietary Supplements — Dietary Supplements for Exercise and Athletic Performance  
   https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/

   Used to verify the need for ingredient-specific evaluation of:
   - creatine;
   - caffeine;
   - beta-alanine;
   - nitrate/beetroot;
   - sodium bicarbonate;
   - other common performance supplements;
   with explicit safety, dose, evidence and side-effect considerations.

## Ограничение

Этот аудит определяет карту вопросов. Он не устанавливает персональные дозировки и не рекомендует конкретные добавки Илье. Это будет сделано только после отдельного глубокого evidence review и персональной оценки.

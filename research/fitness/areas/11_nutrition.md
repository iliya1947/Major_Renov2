NSCA Essentials 5th Edition separates basic nutrition, performance nutrition, body-composition strategies, RED-S/eating disorders and performance-enhancing substances into dedicated chapters. IOC 2023 establishes problematic low energy availability and REDs as a health/performance concern in both female and male athletes. NIH ODS provides evidence/safety summaries for common performance supplements, reinforcing the need to evaluate each ingredient separately rather than treating “sports supplements” as one category.

**PASS.**

Canonical rule:

**scapulohumeral rhythm = variable coordination pattern, not a gear ratio.**

No replacement fixed number is introduced.

No blocker.

## 3. Structural-chain audit

The closure criterion requires tracing load/motion from axial skeleton to humerus.

Canonical chain:

`sternum/first-rib region → SC → clavicle → AC/CC → scapula/glenoid → GH → humerus`

Functional ST interface:

`scapula ↔ muscular/gliding layers ↔ thoracic wall`

This satisfies the structural dependency for later presses, rows, raises and arm training.

## 6. Fixed-ratio / “setting phase” audit

PASS.

No fixed 2:1, 3:1 or replacement numerical rule remains in the canonical model.

The 2025 systematic review is used for the limited claim it supports:

**substantial variability and absence of one universal ratio/setting phase.**

It is not used to prescribe a new normal trajectory.

## 7. Anatomy vs technique boundary

- exact press path;
- exact elbow angle;
- exact grip;

Those require MEC/TEC/outcome evidence.

- scapular dyskinesis

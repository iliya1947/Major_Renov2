### PSY-RES-02 — Study-design families and causal-inference basics

Canonical block:

`research/psychology/qualification/01_research_methodology/PSY-RES-02_study_designs_and_causal_inference_basics.md`

Established:

1. no universal study-design hierarchy independent of question;
2. design families separated by inferential purpose;
3. claim-to-design compatibility matrix;
4. explicit causal estimand before causal interpretation;
5. counterfactual logic;
6. exchangeability, positivity, consistency, interference, and temporality basics;
7. confounder / mediator / collider role distinction;
8. DAGs as assumption maps rather than proof;
9. prediction != causation;
10. diagnostic accuracy != etiology;
11. intervention effect != mechanism proof;
12. statistical adjustment != causal identification by itself;
13. reporting guideline != quality appraisal.

# BIO-02 — Phosphagen system

Статус: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v2.0 — revalidated 2026-11-19**  
Область: 05 — Bioenergetics / Exercise Metabolism

## 0. Core decision

The phosphagen system is the immediate high-rate, limited-capacity ATP-buffering architecture built around:

- the small stored ATP pool;
- the reversible creatine-kinase / phosphocreatine system;
- secondary adenylate-kinase buffering.

Project rule:

**PCr does not replace ATP as the direct ATPase substrate; it rapidly supports ATP regeneration.**

---

## 1. Stored ATP and PCr are distinct

Stored ATP:

- is directly used by ATPases;
- is quantitatively small;
- must be rapidly resynthesized.

PCr:

- is a phosphorylated creatine pool;
- transfers phosphate through creatine kinase;
- supports ATP regeneration from ADP.

Project rule:

**stored ATP ≠ PCr.**

---

## 2. Phosphocreatine

PCr is a rapidly mobilizable high-energy-phosphate buffer.

It is useful for abrupt increases in ATP demand because:

- PCr and CK are already present in muscle;
- phosphate transfer is rapid;
- the reaction responds quickly to ADP/ATP changes.

Project rule:

**PCr is a rapid ATP-buffering reservoir, not a direct mechanical fuel.**

---

## 3. Creatine-kinase reaction

Core reaction:

`PCr + ADP + H+ ↔ Cr + ATP`

The reaction is reversible.

During rapid muscular work, net CK flux shifts toward ATP regeneration.

During recovery, oxidative ATP supply supports re-phosphorylation of creatine back to PCr.

Project rule:

**“PCr breakdown” and “PCr resynthesis” are opposite net directions of one reversible reaction network.**

---

## 4. Temporal and spatial energy buffering

The CK/PCr system supports:

- rapid temporal buffering of ATP/ADP;
- intracellular energy transfer between ATP-producing and ATP-consuming regions.

The project does not require one universally literal “PCr shuttle” topology in every fiber or condition.

Project rule:

**retain the broad temporal/spatial buffering model without overcommitting to one subcellular schematic.**

---

## 5. Adenylate kinase

Adenylate kinase / myokinase catalyzes:

`2 ADP ↔ ATP + AMP`.

Its functions include:

- secondary nucleotide buffering;
- amplification of energetic-state changes through AMP;
- interface with AMP/IMP metabolism and signaling.

Project rule:

**AK is mechanistically important but is not a large independent fuel reservoir.**

No universal fractional ATP contribution is retained.

---

## 6. High rate, limited capacity

The phosphagen system can regenerate ATP at very high rates.

Its capacity is finite because it depends mainly on:

- limited intramuscular PCr;
- the small ATP pool;
- secondary adenylate-kinase buffering.

Project rule:

**high ATP-regeneration rate ≠ high total capacity.**

---

## 7. Very short maximal effort

PCr/CK provides a major contribution during abrupt maximal or near-maximal work.

Direct human studies show rapid PCr depletion within seconds.

But:

- glycolytic ATP production is active early;
- oxidative ATP production is also active early;
- relative contributions change continuously.

Project rule:

**there is no universal “phosphagen-only” time window.**

---

## 8. Energy systems are concurrent

Reject the literal model:

`phosphagen → glycolytic → aerobic`

as three isolated systems switching on one after another.

A better model is:

**all relevant ATP-resynthesis pathways can operate concurrently, while their relative contributions change with demand and time.**

---

## 9. PCr depletion

PCr depletion reflects net use of the CK/PCr buffer.

Its magnitude depends on:

- intensity;
- duration;
- recruited muscle;
- prior work;
- starting PCr;
- recovery state;
- metabolic environment.

Project rule:

**PCr depletion ≠ ATP depletion.**

---

## 10. PCr depletion and fatigue

Low PCr can reduce rapid ATP-buffering capacity and can matter for high-power output.

But fatigue also depends on other metabolic, excitation-contraction, neural and task factors.

Project rule:

**PCr depletion is one contributor/state variable, not a complete fatigue diagnosis.**

---

## 11. PCr resynthesis

After exercise, PCr is regenerated as creatine is re-phosphorylated.

In intact human skeletal muscle this process is predominantly supported by oxidative ATP production.

Project rule:

**post-exercise PCr recovery is primarily an oxidative recovery process.**

---

## 12. O2 dependence

O2 availability can materially alter PCr recovery.

Human hypoxia/hyperoxia experiments directly demonstrate this.

But:

**oxidative dependence does not imply O2 delivery is always the sole rate-limiter.**

PCr recovery also depends on:

- mitochondrial oxidative capacity;
- pH;
- degree of depletion;
- recruitment;
- measurement protocol;
- health/training state.

---

## 13. PCr recovery as an oxidative-capacity marker

31P-MRS PCr recovery can index muscle oxidative capacity.

Interpretation requires control/consideration of:

- O2 delivery/utilization;
- end-exercise pH;
- contraction standardization;
- muscle recruitment/localization;
- depletion;
- recovery model.

Project rule:

**PCr recovery is a context-dependent marker, not a universal constant.**

---

## 14. No universal PCr recovery time

Different protocols produce different recovery trajectories.

Therefore the project rejects:

- “PCr is fully restored after exactly 3 min”;
- “PCr is fully restored after exactly 5 min”;
- any fixed recovery stopwatch independent of task/context.

Project rule:

**full-PCr-recovery time is protocol- and physiology-dependent.**

---

## 15. Rest interval

Longer recovery generally permits more PCr restoration before another high-intensity effort.

This can support repeated high-rate performance.

But rest time alone does not determine:

- exact %PCr recovery;
- complete performance recovery;
- optimal strength/hypertrophy programming.

Project rule:

**rest duration changes next-bout phosphagen state; exact prescription belongs BIO-12/PRO.**

---

## 16. PCr recovery and repeated-bout performance

Greater PCr restoration can support restoration of early high-power output in repeated-bout tasks.

It is not the only determinant.

Project rule:

**PCr restoration is important, but repeated-bout performance is a multi-factor outcome.**

---

## 17. Creatine supplementation boundary

Creatine availability can modify:

- total muscle creatine;
- PCr availability;
- PCr recovery behavior.

BIO-02 retains the mechanism only.

Dosing, loading, safety, efficacy and individualized prescription belong NUT.

Project rule:

**creatine mechanism ≠ creatine prescription.**

---

## 18. Canonical non-equivalences

1. Stored ATP ≠ PCr.
2. PCr ≠ ATP.
3. PCr use ≠ direct ATPase fuel.
4. CK reaction ≠ irreversible one-way reaction.
5. High rate ≠ high capacity.
6. Adenylate kinase ≠ large independent fuel reservoir.
7. Major early PCr contribution ≠ exclusive ATP source.
8. “First 10 s” ≠ phosphagen-only window.
9. PCr depletion ≠ ATP depletion.
10. PCr depletion ≠ fatigue.
11. PCr recovery ≠ universal time constant.
12. Oxidative dependence ≠ O2 delivery always limiting.
13. Longer rest ≠ guaranteed full PCr restoration.
14. Rest duration ≠ universal %PCr-restored.
15. PCr restoration ≠ complete performance recovery.
16. Creatine mechanism ≠ supplementation prescription.
17. Phosphagen/glycolytic/oxidative pathways ≠ sequential tanks.

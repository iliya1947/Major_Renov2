# A1 №4 — Мотивация, эмоции и интересы: источники картографии

**Назначение:** доказательная опора для первичной карты области. Не глубокий разбор найденных механизмов.

## Мотивация, goals и self-regulation

1. **Eccles & Wigfield (2002), Motivational Beliefs, Values, and Goals. Annual Review of Psychology 53:109–132.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev.psych.53.100901.135153.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и структура обзора.  
   Для карты: expectancies, task value, intrinsic motivation, self-determination, interest, goals, attribution и self-regulation представлены как разные теоретические линии.

2. **Wigfield, Muenks & Eccles (2021), Achievement Motivation: What We Know and Where We Are Going. Annual Review of Developmental Psychology 3:87–111.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-devpsych-050720-103500.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступная структура.  
   Для карты: developmental линии motivational beliefs, values, goals, intrinsic/extrinsic motivation, identity, emotion и intervention research.

3. **Inzlicht et al. (2021), Integrating Models of Self-Regulation. Annual Review of Psychology 72:319–345.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-061020-105721.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: self-regulation literature spans multiple levels, conflict, emotion и cognitive functioning; отдельная integrative ветвь.

4. **Brandstätter & Bernecker (2022), Persistence and Disengagement in Personal Goal Pursuit. Annual Review of Psychology 73:271–299.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-110710.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: persistence и disengagement — равноценные стороны goal pursuit; motivation science исторически переоценивала persistence.

5. **Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2025), Psychology of Planning. Annual Review of Psychology 76:303–328.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-021524-110536.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: goals, plans и implementation intentions требуют отдельного различения.

6. **Salamone & Correa (2024), The Neurobiology of Activational Aspects of Motivation: Exertion of Effort, Effort-Based Decision Making, and the Role of Dopamine. Annual Review of Psychology 75:1–32.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020223-012208.  
   Просмотрено: abstract.  
   Для карты: motivation имеет directional и activational/effort aspects; effort нельзя свести к reward preference.

## Self-determination, needs и intrinsic motivation

7. **Ryan & Deci (2020), Intrinsic and extrinsic motivation from a self-determination theory perspective. Contemporary Educational Psychology 61:101860.** DOI: 10.1016/j.cedpsych.2020.101860.  
   Просмотрено: abstract и доступные sections.  
   Для карты: intrinsic/extrinsic, autonomous/controlled motivation и basic psychological needs — разные constructs внутри SDT.

8. **Vansteenkiste, Soenens & Ryan (2023), Basic Psychological Needs Theory: A Conceptual and Empirical Review of Key Criteria. Oxford Handbook of Self-Determination Theory.** DOI: 10.1093/oxfordhb/9780197600047.013.5.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/description.  
   Для карты: autonomy, competence, relatedness claims требуют отдельной проверки universality, distinctness и explanatory status.

9. **Fishbach & Woolley (2022), The Structure of Intrinsic Motivation. Annual Review of Organizational Psychology and Organizational Behavior 9:339–363.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-012420-091122.  
   Просмотрено: abstract/overview.  
   Для карты: intrinsic motivation рассматривается как отдельный research program, а не просто synonym interest/enjoyment.

10. **Bureau et al. (2022), Pathways to Student Motivation: A Meta-Analysis of Antecedents of Autonomous and Controlled Motivations. Review of Educational Research 92:46–72.** DOI: 10.3102/00346543211042426.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: empirical line on need support, need satisfaction и autonomous/controlled motivation; context and source of support matter.

## Interest, curiosity и boredom

11. **Renninger & Hidi (2022), Interest: A unique affective and cognitive motivational variable that develops. Advances in Motivation Science 9:179–239.** DOI: 10.1016/bs.adms.2021.12.004.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/preview.  
    Для карты: interest is both state during engagement and motivation to reengage; linked but not identical to curiosity/intrinsic motivation.

12. **Hidi & Renninger (2026), The four-phase model of interest development: Addressing individual differences. Learning and Individual Differences.** DOI: 10.1016/j.lindif.2025.102865.  
    Просмотрено: highlights и preview.  
    Для карты: interest development explicitly includes individual variation and sociocultural context.

13. **Yan et al. (2025), From situational interest and state curiosity to personal interest: developmental pathways and underlying mechanisms. Frontiers in Psychology 16:1700208.** DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1700208.  
    Просмотрено: full accessible HTML sections.  
    Для карты: situational interest, state curiosity and personal interest must be kept conceptually separate.

14. **Jirout, Evans & Son (2024), Curiosity in children across ages and contexts. Nature Reviews Psychology 3:622–635.** DOI: 10.1038/s44159-024-00346-5.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: curiosity as internally motivated information seeking in response to knowledge gaps; developmental/contextual line.

15. **Curiosity-driven exploration: foundations in neuroscience and computational modeling (2023). Trends in Neurosciences 46:1054–1066.** DOI: 10.1016/j.tins.2023.10.002.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: no single accepted curiosity framework; novelty, uncertainty and information seeking form separate dimensions.

16. **Seiler & Dan (2024), Boredom and curiosity: the hunger and the appetite for information. Frontiers in Psychology 15:1514348.** DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1514348.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and overview.  
    Для карты: boredom and curiosity can both drive information seeking and need to be distinguished experimentally.

## Emotion theory

17. **Scherer & Moors (2019), The Emotion Process: Event Appraisal and Component Differentiation. Annual Review of Psychology 70:719–745.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-122216-011854.  
    Просмотрено: abstract and accessible overview.  
    Для карты: appraisal, action tendencies, physiology, expression, conscious feeling and labeling are distinct components/stages.

18. **Yeo & Ong (2024), Associations between cognitive appraisals and emotions: A meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin 150:1440–1471.** DOI: 10.1037/bul0000452.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: appraisal-emotion literature is large but heterogeneous in appraisal dimensions, terminology and predictions.

19. **van Heijst, Kret & Ploeger (2025), Basic Emotions or Constructed Emotions: Insights From Taking an Evolutionary Perspective. Perspectives on Psychological Science 20:377–391.** DOI: 10.1177/17456916231205186.  
    Просмотрено: abstract/full-text availability.  
    Для карты: basic-emotion vs constructed-emotion remains an active theoretical divide.

20. **Barrett et al. (2025), The Theory of Constructed Emotion: More Than a Feeling. Perspectives on Psychological Science 20:392–420.** DOI: 10.1177/17456916251319045.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: competing emotion theories rest on different assumptions and evidence standards; they cannot be treated as minor variants.

## Emotion regulation, social emotion и culture

21. **Bjureberg (2026), The Role of Emotion Regulation in Clinical Interventions. Annual Review of Clinical Psychology 22:105–133.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-061324-072853.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: regulation includes abilities and dynamic process-level strategies; development, beliefs and context matter.

22. **Arican-Dinc & Gable (2026), Dyadic Emotion Regulation. Annual Review of Psychology 77:365–391.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020325-033825.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: emotion regulation is not only intrapersonal; dyadic/interpersonal regulation is a separate branch.

23. **van Kleef & Côté (2022), The Social Effects of Emotions. Annual Review of Psychology 73:629–658.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-020821-010855.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: emotional expression influences observers via affective and inferential routes across interaction contexts.

24. **Tamir, Chentsova-Dutton & Miyamoto (2026), Culture and Emotion Regulation. Annual Review of Psychology, Review in Advance.** DOI: 10.1146/annurev-psych-110625-121512.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: regulation goals, preferred emotional experiences and expression are culturally patterned.

25. **Ong et al. (2025), Affect dynamics and depressive symptomatology: Revisiting the inertia-instability paradox. Emotion 25:1060–1064.** DOI: 10.1037/emo0001465.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: mean affect, variability, instability and inertia are distinct dynamic properties and require appropriate longitudinal methods.

26. **Exploring emotions beyond the laboratory (2024), Psychophysiology.** DOI: 10.1111/psyp.14699.  
    Просмотрено: abstract.  
    Для карты: ecological momentary assessment plus ambulatory physiology creates separate measurement/reliability problems from laboratory emotion induction.

## Coverage-check

Дополнительно проверены работы по regulatory focus, attribution, values, self-control, social/self-conscious emotion, culture, emotion dynamics, question asking and information seeking. После этого новые sources преимущественно уточняли уже найденные блоки, а не создавали новые крупные оси.

Это достаточно для **A1-картографии**, но не означает глубокого исследования ни одной темы.

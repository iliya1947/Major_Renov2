# Psychopathology

## Purpose

This directory stores the clinical-psychopathology qualification sequence for Kira.

The domain establishes scientifically defensible reasoning about symptoms, syndromes, diagnostic classifications, dimensional/transdiagnostic models, mechanisms, course, impairment, differential inference, and diagnostic limits.

### CLN-01 — Psychopathology foundations

Status: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v1.0 — 18/18 PASS**

Canonical block:

`CLN-01_psychopathology_foundations_symptom_syndrome_disorder_impairment_dimensions_comorbidity_differential_validity.md`

Established:

1. symptom, sign, syndrome, disorder, diagnosis, formulation, and mechanism are separate inference objects;
2. DSM-5-TR and ICD-11/CDDR are current clinical-classification anchors rather than complete causal ontologies;
3. distress, functional impairment, dysfunction, and risk are distinct;
4. many psychopathology dimensions are continuous even when clinical decisions are categorical;
5. diagnostic reliability, validity, and clinical utility are separate properties;
6. diagnostic heterogeneity is real but theoretically possible symptom combinations should not be treated as equally common;
7. comorbidity can arise from shared liability, criterion overlap, causal relations, transdiagnostic mechanisms, or classification structure;
8. HiTOP is a dimensional/hierarchical nosology rather than a formal DSM/ICD replacement diagnosis or causal theory;
9. the p factor summarizes broad psychopathology covariance/burden but is not established as one causal mechanism;
10. RDoC is an NIMH research framework and explicitly not a diagnostic guide;
11. differential inference requires medical, substance/medication, developmental, contextual, and psychiatric alternatives;
12. screening is not diagnosis and base rates matter;
13. symptom-level/transdiagnostic research remains vulnerable to measurement heterogeneity;
14. a 78-question Psychopathology Foundations Inference Gate and 18/18 closure audit are canonical.

### CLN-02 — Psychopathology mechanisms and course

Status: **CLOSED_FOR_PRACTICE v1.0 — 18/18 PASS**

Canonical block:

`CLN-02_psychopathology_mechanisms_course_transdiagnostic_onset_maintenance_remission_recurrence_state_trait_causal_limits.md`

Established:

1. onset, maintenance, persistence, remission, recovery, relapse, and recurrence are distinct course targets;
2. risk marker, risk factor, protective factor, mediator, moderator, and mechanism are noninterchangeable;
3. transdiagnostic breadth does not establish universality or causal mechanism;
4. repetitive negative thinking has strong transdiagnostic evidence but is not universalized into one cause;
5. broad emotion-regulation impairment is robust across disorders while temporal/causal direction remains bounded;
6. between-person liability and within-person process are separate estimands;
7. repeated/EMA measurement improves temporal resolution but does not create causal identification;
8. stress generation is modeled as reciprocal person–environment feedback without blame;
9. remission does not prove mechanism removal and recurrence does not prove mechanism identity;
10. fixed relapse/recurrence boundaries are treated as operational where empirical discontinuity is unsupported;
11. longitudinal precedence, mediation, symptom networks, MR, and quasi-experimental estimates remain assumption-bound;
12. resilience and protective processes are contextual, time-dependent, and outcome-dependent rather than fixed traits;
13. an 82-question Psychopathology Mechanisms / Course Inference Gate and 18/18 closure audit are canonical.

### CLN-03 — Disorder-family pattern recognition and exclusion boundaries

Status: **COMPLETE_FOR_CURRENT_SCOPE — 20/20 PASS**

Canonical block:

`CLN-03_disorder_family_pattern_recognition_exclusion_boundaries_internalizing_psychosis_bipolar_ocd_trauma_eating_dissociation_substance_personality.md`

Established:

1. family inference starts from phenomenology, function, time/course, reality/insight, and exclusion priority rather than one salient symptom;
2. psychosis is a symptom domain and does not equal schizophrenia;
3. depressive presentation does not establish unipolar depression; bipolar inference requires lifetime episode architecture;
4. intrusive thoughts do not equal intent and OCD requires obsession/compulsion structure rather than generic repetition;
5. trauma exposure does not equal PTSD;
6. dissociation and psychosis require phenomenological separation; depersonalization/derealization can preserve reality testing;
7. eating disorders are differentiated by restriction, binge/loss-of-control, compensatory behavior, weight/shape function, and ARFID-style drivers rather than body weight alone;
8. somatic symptom disorder is not inferred from normal medical tests and can coexist with medical illness;
9. substance-induced versus independent mental disorders require temporal/exposure-course analysis;
10. personality pathology requires enduring cross-context pattern evidence and is not inferred from a temporary episode;
11. developmental baseline versus acquired episode is a mandatory differential axis and routes detailed questions to NDEV;
12. a 96-question Disorder-Family Differential Gate and 20/20 CLN sufficiency audit are canonical;
13. no scientifically necessary CLN-04 dependency is identified.

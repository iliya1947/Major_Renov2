### EXE-02 — Goal representation, proactive/reactive control, and hierarchical task control

Canonical block:

`research/psychology/qualification/04_executive_functions_and_metacognition/EXE-02_goal_representation_proactive_reactive_hierarchical_control.md`

Established:

1. goal selection, representation, maintenance, updating, and implementation are distinct;
2. goal representations need not be verbal or strictly discrete;
3. goal maintenance is a control use of WM, not all working memory;
4. goal shielding/persistence can be adaptive or maladaptive depending on environmental stability;
5. update rate should match environmental volatility rather than be maximized;
6. recent feedback and learned context models can drive different updating strategies;
7. goals/task rules can be organized hierarchically across abstract context, subgoal, rule, and action levels;
8. different hierarchical levels can reconfigure at different speeds and with different susceptibility to perceptual interference;
9. task-set inertia can preserve previously relevant representations after a switch;
10. proactive control uses goal/context information before demand, while reactive control recruits/retrieves it after demand;
11. proactive/reactive are modes/dimensions, not mutually exclusive stable person types;
12. proactive control is not globally superior and reactive control is not inherently deficient;
13. AX-CPT/control-mode signatures can mix active maintenance with associative learning, attention, WM, and response processes;
14. control mode does not uniquely specify its implementation mechanism;
15. goal selection != goal implementation;
16. intention formation != intention retrieval;
17. control policy != current control state, and reconfiguration has measurable inertia/cost;
18. failed intentions can arise from multiple distinct mechanisms;
19. a 50-question Goal-Control Inference Gate is canonical.
